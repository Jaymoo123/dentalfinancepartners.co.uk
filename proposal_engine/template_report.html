SECTION __Contactability and automated follow-up

Since $nurture_golive an automated follow-up sequence has run on covered portfolio sites, built to make sure every lead that reaches a receiving firm can actually be contacted. The moment an enquiry is submitted it receives an instant acknowledgement, then a short SMS and email sequence that confirms the enquirer's contact details are live, captures anything missing from the original submission, and offers a link to book a call directly. The funnel reads top to bottom: each stage is a subset of the stage above.

Methodology. Lead counts: complete rows from the live lead database, no sampling. Arrival trend: Poisson regression (log-linear) fitted to the most recent eight complete calendar weeks, excluding the launch ramp so the trend is not overstated; the shaded band in Figure 2 is a 90% interval on the trend mean via the delta method. Trailing 30-day interval: nonparametric bootstrap of daily arrival counts, 5,000 resamples. Case types: classified individually on the work each enquiry describes, in the enquirer's own words. No figure in this document is derived from an estimated client value. The observed series is $n_weeks complete weeks, so interval estimates accompany every point estimate.

This report is informational: it describes observed history and is not an offer capable of acceptance. Case types describe the work an enquiry asks for; they are not a prediction that the enquirer will engage, or of what any engagement will be worth. Volumes shown are observed history, not a commitment to future volume.
