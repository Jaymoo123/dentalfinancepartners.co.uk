Grading is on case type only, never on a guess at what a client might be worth, and anything sitting between two tiers is graded down. SECTION __Contactability and automated follow-up

Since $nurture_golive an automated follow-up sequence has run on covered portfolio sites, built to make sure every lead that reaches a receiving firm can actually be contacted. The moment an enquiry is submitted it receives an instant acknowledgement, then a short SMS and email sequence that confirms the enquirer's contact details are live, captures anything missing from the original submission, and offers a link to book a call directly. The funnel reads top to bottom: each stage is a subset of the stage above.

Methodology. Lead counts: complete rows from the live lead database, no sampling. Arrival trend: Poisson regression (log-linear) fitted to the most recent eight complete calendar weeks, excluding the launch ramp so the trend is not overstated; the shaded band in Figure 2 is a 90% interval on the trend mean via the delta method. Trailing 30-day interval: nonparametric bootstrap of daily arrival counts, 5,000 resamples. Search figures: pulled on $report_date from the Google Search Console API (date-dimension totals, unsampled) and the Bing Webmaster Tools rank-and-traffic API. $search_coverage_note Tiers: graded leads are assessed individually against the published rubric on the work each enquiry describes; enquiries sitting between two tiers are graded to the lower one, so the tier mix shown understates rather than overstates the flow. No figure in this document is derived from an estimated client value. The observed series is $n_weeks complete weeks, so interval estimates accompany every point estimate.
