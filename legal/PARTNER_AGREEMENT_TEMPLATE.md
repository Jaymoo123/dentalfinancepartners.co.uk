## BACKGROUND

(A) The Supplier operates a portfolio of specialist enquiry websites, each under its own trading name, through which people seeking specialist professional advice submit enquiries (each a "Site" and together the "Sites"). It operates them as a lead distribution network under the name Ashfield Partner Network (the "Network").

(B) The Supplier offers enquiries to firms in the Network. A firm chooses which enquiries it wants and receives only those, and pays only for those. There is no subscription, no minimum and no commitment to take any enquiry.

(C) The same enquiry may be supplied to more than one firm, within the limits in Schedule 2. This agreement does not give the Recipient exclusivity over any Site's enquiry flow.

(D) This agreement has two layers. Clauses 1 to 21 and Schedule 1 set the commercial terms. Schedule 2 and the Annexes are the data sharing agreement between the parties as independent Controllers. Where the two layers conflict on a data protection matter, Schedule 2 prevails.

IT IS AGREED as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 In this agreement:

"Alert" has the meaning given in Schedule 2: a summary of an enquiry offered to firms in the Network, from which the enquirer's name, telephone number, email address and enquiry message have been removed.

"Billing Period" means a calendar month.

"Bulk Supply" has the meaning given in Schedule 2.

"Cascade" means the Supplier withdrawing an enquiry from one Lane, and offering it on another, because no firm on the first Lane Claimed it within the period in Schedule 1.

"Charges" means the Claim Prices payable under this agreement and any other sums stated in Schedule 1.

"Claim" means the Recipient telling the Supplier, in the manner in clause 3.4, that it wants an enquiry it has been Alerted to. "Claimed" and "Claiming" are construed accordingly. A Claim is an order, and is irrevocable once accepted.

"Claim Price" means the price payable for a Claimed Lead, determined under clause 5.

"Delivery" means the Supplier sending the Recipient the Shared Data for a Claimed Lead, and "Deliver" and "Delivered" are construed accordingly.

"Delivery Log" means the Supplier's record of Alerts, Claims and Deliveries kept under clause 3.7.

"Exclusive Claim" means a Claim made and accepted under clause 4.3.

"Lane" means a group of professions in which Claims are counted separately, as described in clause 4.2 and set out in Schedule 1.

"Lead" means a verified enquiry which the Supplier offers to the Network, meeting the description in clause 6.1.

"Price Sheet" means the Supplier's published price sheet, the current version of which is reproduced in Schedule 1.

"Rubric" means the Supplier's published lead classification rubric, the current version of which is reproduced in Schedule 1, and references in this agreement to the Supplier's published rubric are to it.

"Shared Claim" means a Claim that is not an Exclusive Claim.

"Shared Data" has the meaning given in Schedule 2 and is specified in Annex A.

"Standard Terms" means the Supplier's published standard terms, reproduced verbatim in Schedule 1, on the Price Sheet, in every Delivery and on every invoice.

1.2 Terms defined in Schedule 2, including "Working Day", have the same meaning in clauses 1 to 21 unless the context requires otherwise, and are defined once only. Clause headings are for convenience only. References to legislation include any amendment or replacement. "Including" is not limiting.

## 2. TERM AND JOINING

2.1 This agreement begins on the date of the last signature below and continues until terminated under clause 16. There is no minimum term, no minimum spend and no notice period for the Recipient beyond clause 16.1.

2.2 Joining the Network is free. The Supplier charges no joining fee, subscription, listing fee or minimum monthly amount, and the Recipient owes nothing in any month in which it makes no Claim.

2.3 No payment is due on joining. The Recipient pays only for Leads it Claims, in arrears, on the invoicing terms in clause 7. Before accepting a Claim the Supplier may require the Recipient to confirm the billing contact and address to which invoices are to be sent.

## 3. THE NETWORK, ALERTS AND CLAIMS

3.1 During the term, the Supplier will offer Leads to the firms in the Network by sending an Alert. Every active firm on the relevant Lane receives the Alert at the same time. The Recipient is under no obligation to Claim any Lead, and the Supplier is under no obligation to Alert any minimum number of Leads.

3.2 An Alert contains the Lead's tier, case type, rough geographic area, a one-line summary of what the enquirer wants, the enquiry in the enquirer's own words with identifying details removed, the Site it came from, the verification status, and the Shared and Exclusive prices. It contains no contact details and no unredacted enquiry. The Recipient reads the enquiry and decides whether to Claim on that basis; the unredacted enquiry and the enquirer's contact details are Delivered only if it Claims.

3.3 There is no allocation, no rotation, no right of first refusal and no head start. Claims are accepted strictly in the order they reach the Supplier.

3.4 The Recipient Claims a Lead by replying to the Alert, or by such other method as the Supplier notifies in writing from time to time. The Supplier will confirm acceptance or rejection of each Claim. A Claim takes effect only on the Supplier's acceptance.

3.5 On accepting a Claim the Supplier will Deliver the Lead in full, promptly and by a method that encrypts it in transit, to the Nominated Address, together with the lawful-basis information required by Schedule 2.

3.6 The Supplier gives no warranty as to the volume, frequency, timing, tier mix, geography, value or conversion of Leads. Any volumes shown on the Price Sheet or in any proposal are typical figures, not commitments. The Supplier retains discretion over which Leads it offers, to which Lane, and in what order.

3.7 The Supplier will keep an accurate Delivery Log recording, for each Lead, the Alert and its time, each Claim and its time, the Lane, whether the Claim was Shared or Exclusive, the Claim Price and the time of Delivery. The Delivery Log is the basis for invoicing under clause 7, is retained for at least 24 months, and relevant extracts are available to the Recipient on reasonable request.

3.8 The Recipient will keep its Nominated Address operational and able to receive Deliveries. The Supplier is not responsible for a Delivery failure caused by the Recipient's systems, mailbox or filtering.

## 4. SHARED AND EXCLUSIVE CLAIMS

4.1 Leads are shared by default. Up to the number of firms stated in Schedule 1 may Claim the same Lead on the same Lane, first come, first served. The cap is on Claims that succeed, not on which firms may attempt one.

4.2 Claims run in separate Lanes, and the Lanes are independent of each other. A Claim on one Lane never consumes a slot on another. The Lanes, and the cap on each, are stated in Schedule 1. Firms on a different Lane are in a different profession and do not compete with the Recipient for the work the enquirer is asking about.

4.3 A Lead which no firm has yet Claimed on the Recipient's Lane may be Claimed exclusively. An accepted Exclusive Claim locks that Lane: the Supplier will not Deliver that Lead to any other firm on that Lane. The price of an Exclusive Claim is the multiple of the Claim Price stated in Schedule 1.

4.4 The race decides, and the Supplier will not manipulate it. If a Shared Claim on that Lane is accepted first, exclusivity is no longer available for that Lead; the Recipient may still take a Shared Claim at the Shared price, and the exclusive premium is not charged. The exclusive premium is charged only where the Exclusive Claim is the first accepted Claim on that Lane.

4.5 An Exclusive Claim closes the Recipient's own Lane only. It does not prevent the Supplier offering or Delivering the same Lead on another Lane to a different profession, and the Recipient acknowledges this when making an Exclusive Claim.

4.6 The Recipient is not told which other firms, if any, have Claimed a Lead, and the Supplier will not disclose the identity of any other firm in the Network to the Recipient.

4.7 Exclusivity attaches to individual Leads only. Nothing in this agreement gives the Recipient exclusivity over a Site, a geographic area, a case type, a tier or a Site's enquiry flow.

## 5. PRICE, DECAY AND PRICE CHANGES

5.1 The price of each Lead is the price for its tier on the Price Sheet, as shown on the Alert. Prices are exclusive of VAT (clause 7.2).

5.2 A Lead's price is fixed at the moment of its first accepted Claim on a Lane. Every firm Claiming that Lead on that Lane afterwards pays that same price, whatever the Price Sheet may say by then. No firm obtains the same Lead more cheaply than another.

5.3 A Lead holds its price for as long as it is unclaimed. There is no reduced or last-call price. A Lead which no firm has Claimed may Cascade to another Lane after the period stated in Schedule 1. Cascade applies only while a Lead is wholly unclaimed: once any Claim is accepted, the Lead does not re-price and does not Cascade.

5.4 The Supplier may change its published prices on not less than 30 days' written notice to the Recipient. A change never applies retrospectively: the price of a Lead already Claimed is unaffected, and the price shown on an Alert is the price for that Lead. If the Recipient does not accept a price change it may terminate under clause 16.1, and until termination takes effect it is charged the old prices.

5.5 Where the Supplier and the Recipient have agreed a price different from the published price for any tier, it is stated in Schedule 1 and prevails over the Price Sheet for that tier.

## 6. QUALIFYING LEADS AND CREDITS

6.1 What the Recipient is paying for. A Lead qualifies for its Claim Price if, at the time of Delivery, it: (a) is a genuine enquiry submitted through a Site by the enquirer; (b) has been verified by SMS and email, meaning the Supplier has confirmed those channels are live and the enquirer has actively responded; (c) carries the enquirer's name, telephone number, email address, the enquiry message and a tier label graded under the Supplier's published rubric; and (d) is Delivered with the lawful basis required by Schedule 2 and materially corresponds to the Alert the Recipient Claimed from. Whether the contact details later prove functional in the Recipient's hands is not a condition of qualification; it gives rise only to the credit in clause 6.5(a).

6.2 Billable on Claim. The Claim Price is earned and payable on Delivery. It is not contingent on whether the enquirer responds to the Recipient, is contactable, engages, attends a meeting, accepts a proposal, becomes a client or generates revenue, except as provided by the credits in this clause 6. An enquirer who has spoken to another adviser is still chargeable unless that adviser was formally engaged for the same work before the enquiry was made (clause 6.6).

6.3 Grading, and how it is checked. Every Lead is graded by case type against the Rubric, on the work the enquiry describes and never on the Supplier's estimate of what the enquirer might be worth. Where an enquiry sits between two tiers it is graded to the lower one. The Rubric is set out in full in paragraph 7 of Schedule 1, and the grading is recorded against a Lead at the time it is graded, so that the Recipient can check any Delivered Lead against the case-type list for its tier. The Rubric is the reference for whether a Lead was "materially different" under clause 6.5(b). The Supplier may amend the Rubric on 30 days' written notice, and no amendment applies to a Lead already Delivered.

6.4 Credits, not refunds, and on Exclusive Claims only. Credits are available on Exclusive Claims only. A Shared Claim, and anything supplied by Bulk Supply, is sold as seen and carries no credit. The Recipient acknowledges the reason and that it is reflected in the price: a Shared Claim costs a fraction of the Exclusive price, and a complaint that an enquirer could not be reached cannot be verified fairly when several firms hold the same Lead. The level of protection is the Recipient's choice, made Lead by Lead.

6.5 Grounds. On an Exclusive Claim, and within the window in clause 6.7, the Recipient may flag a Lead in writing, with evidence sufficient for the Supplier reasonably to verify the ground, on these grounds only:

(a) Dead or unreachable contact details. Every contact channel Delivered with the Lead is non-functional after Delivery, so the enquirer cannot be reached on any of them. Where both an email address and a telephone number were Delivered, this requires both the email to hard-bounce and the number to be invalid, disconnected or unobtainable. A live number that rings out, goes unanswered or diverts to voicemail, and a mailbox that accepts mail, do not qualify. This ground does not apply where the failure is attributable to the Recipient's own systems, delay or error. Because contact details are verified live before any Alert, a flag on this ground must show a channel failure arising after Delivery.

(b) Materially different from the description. The Delivered Lead differs from the Alert the Recipient Claimed from in a way fundamental to the nature or subject of the enquiry, such that a reasonable specialist firm, shown that difference, would not have Claimed it. Immaterial variance, further detail consistent with the Alert, or the Lead proving less valuable than the Recipient hoped, is not a material difference.

(c) No response after sustained pursuit. The contact details are functional but the enquirer does not engage at all, and the Recipient evidences a genuine, prompt and sustained pursuit, being all of: no fewer than 7 and ordinarily up to 9 genuine contact attempts within the 14 days following Delivery; the first attempt within 2 Working Days of Delivery; all attempts made to the details Delivered; attempts using every channel Delivered that is available for pursuit, including at least one telephone call and at least one written message where both were Delivered; and attempts spread across no fewer than 5 separate days within the period. Attempts clustered so as to manufacture the count do not qualify. The credit is not available if the enquirer engaged in any way during the period, including any reply by any channel, an answered call or a booking.

(d) Not genuine. The enquiry is spam, bot-generated or fabricated and is not from a genuine human enquirer. A genuine human enquiry is not within this ground merely because the Recipient considers it low value or would prefer not to act on it.

(e) Duplicate. The same enquirer and the same underlying matter were the subject of a Lead already Delivered to and payable by the Recipient within the previous 30 days. A fresh enquiry about a different matter from the same enquirer is not a duplicate.

6.6 Already engaged elsewhere. A Lead is credited where, before submitting the enquiry, the enquirer had already formally engaged another adviser to carry out the specific work the enquiry is about. It is not enough that the enquirer has an existing general accountant or adviser for other matters. An enquirer who engages another adviser at or after the time of enquiring, or has done so by the time the Recipient makes contact, is a matter of contact timing and not of Lead quality, and remains chargeable; the Recipient should respond promptly to minimise this. A flag under this clause requires the enquirer's own confirmation, volunteered unprompted, that the prior engagement pre-dates the enquiry. The Supplier will provide the enquiry date and time from the Delivery Log on request.

6.7 Flag mechanics. A flag must be in writing to the Supplier's contact in Schedule 1; must be made within 3 Working Days of Delivery for grounds (a), (b), (d) and (e), within the 14-day window for ground (c), and promptly on becoming aware and within 14 days of Delivery for clause 6.6; must identify the Lead by its Delivery Log reference; must state the single ground relied on; and must include the supporting evidence. Time is of the essence. A late, unevidenced or insufficiently evidenced flag is not valid and the Claim Price remains payable. A Lead may be credited on one ground only, and a Lead once credited is not also billable.

6.8 Review. The Supplier will review each valid, evidenced flag reasonably and in good faith and notify its determination within 10 Working Days. For ground (a) only, if the Supplier does not respond within 10 Working Days of a valid, evidenced flag, the flag is treated as accepted. For every other ground the Supplier's silence is not acceptance. An accepted flag is credited against the next invoice, or refunded within 30 days if no further invoice is due.

6.9 The Recipient's own records. The Recipient is solely responsible for tracking each Lead it Claims, for its own outreach, and for creating and keeping, at the time, the records a credit requires. The Supplier has no obligation to prompt, remind, chase or reconstruct the Recipient's evidence, and a shortfall in the Recipient's own record-keeping is not a ground for a credit and is not a reason to extend any window.

6.10 Except as provided in this clause 6, the Recipient has no right to withhold, set off or reduce a Claim Price, and no ground other than those above entitles it to a credit.

## 7. CHARGES, INVOICING AND PAYMENT

7.1 The Recipient will pay the Claim Price for each Lead it Claims, calculated from the Delivery Log, together with any other Charges in Schedule 1.

7.2 VAT. Charges are exclusive of VAT. The Supplier is not currently registered for VAT, so no VAT is chargeable as at the date of this agreement. If the Supplier becomes registered it may add VAT at the prevailing rate against a valid VAT invoice.

7.3 The Supplier will invoice monthly in arrears, on the first day of each month, for Leads Claimed in the previous Billing Period. Each invoice states each Lead Claimed with its Delivery Log reference, the Lane, whether the Claim was Shared or Exclusive, the Claim Price, any credits under clause 6 as negative lines, and the total payable.

7.4 Invoices are payable by bank transfer, in cleared funds, to the account stated on the invoice, within 14 days of the invoice date. The Recipient bears any charges its own bank makes for the transfer, and will quote the invoice reference with the payment.

7.5 Reconciliation. Within 10 Working Days of each invoice the Recipient will notify the Supplier in writing of any discrepancy, identifying the affected Leads. The parties will reconcile in good faith and any agreed over-charge is credited or refunded. Absent a timely, substantiated notice, the Delivery Log is the agreed record for that Billing Period.

7.6 Audit. No more than twice in any 12-month period, on reasonable written notice and under confidentiality, the Recipient or its auditors may inspect the Delivery Log and the Supplier's lawful-basis records for the preceding 12 months, to verify the invoiced Claims and the warranty in clause 11.3. The Recipient bears its own costs, unless a material discrepancy or non-compliance is found, in which case the Supplier bears the reasonable cost.

## 8. LATE PAYMENT AND SUSPENSION

8.1 If the Recipient fails to pay any undisputed sum when due, interest accrues on the overdue amount at 8% per year above the Bank of England base rate from the due date until payment, before and after judgment. This is equal to, and in satisfaction of, statutory interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, and the Supplier is also entitled to the fixed-sum compensation and reasonable recovery costs under that Act.

8.2 The Supplier may stop accepting Claims and stop Delivery where any undisputed sum is 14 or more days overdue, until all overdue sums with interest are paid. A valid suspension is not a breach by the Supplier and the Supplier has no liability for it.

8.3 Clauses 8.1 and 8.2 apply to undisputed sums only. The Recipient must notify any genuine, good-faith invoice dispute within 10 Working Days of the invoice, giving reasons, and must pay the undisputed balance when due.

10.3 The Recipient will not scrape, harvest, bulk-copy or automate the collection of Alerts, and will not use an Alert other than to decide whether to Claim the Lead it describes.

10.4 Non-circumvention. During the term and for 12 months after it ends, the Recipient will not structure any arrangement to obtain the benefit of a Lead it has been Alerted to otherwise than under this agreement, or to avoid the Claim Price for a Lead it has Claimed. 10.5 Neither party will knowingly solicit or entice away any employee of the other materially involved in this agreement. General recruitment advertising not targeted at such individuals, and engaging anyone who responds to it or approaches independently, is permitted.

10.6 The Recipient will not approach, or seek to identify, any other firm in the Network for the purpose of circumventing this agreement.

The indemnified party may keep control of any claim affecting its own regulatory or professional standing, but will not settle it without the indemnifier's consent (not to be unreasonably withheld). There are no uncapped indemnities.
