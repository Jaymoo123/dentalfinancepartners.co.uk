# LEGITIMATE INTERESTS ASSESSMENT: ADDENDUM

Addendum to the standing Legitimate Interests Assessment of Ashfield Trading Limited dated 14 August 2026. Addendum date: 26 August 2026. Review with the main assessment.

## What has changed

A firm in the partner network, Aswatax Ltd (company number 12923632, registered in England and Wales), receives referrals of advisory enquiries under the standing data sharing agreement and a referral agreement. Its relevant personnel, including its director, are ordinarily resident in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and access referred data from there. Separately, Sidekick Accounting Ltd (company number 12250170) triages enquiries and receives those needing compliance work.

## Effect on the balancing test

The purpose and necessity limbs of the standing assessment are unchanged: the sharing still connects an enquirer who has asked for specialist help with a regulated or professionally qualified firm able to give it, and no less intrusive route achieves that.

On the balancing limb, the overseas access point adds a factor that was not present when the standing assessment was made. It is assessed as not tipping the balance, because: the receiving firm is established in the United Kingdom and remains directly subject to the UK GDPR, so the enquirer's rights stay enforceable through UK routes; the data remains hosted in UK or EEA systems and is accessed remotely, with no local copy of the dataset held overseas; the access takes place under an international data transfer agreement in the form issued by the Information Commissioner, supported by a documented transfer risk assessment dated 26 August 2026 which concluded the risk is low; and the data itself is ordinary contact and enquiry data with low potential for harm.

The enquirer's reasonable expectations are unchanged: they expect their enquiry to reach a professional firm that will respond, and the on site notice and privacy policy disclose the sharing and the right to object. Each relevant site's privacy policy will disclose, before the first referral to this firm, that partner firms may access data from outside the United Kingdom with safeguards in place.

## Conditions

This addendum's conclusion depends on the conditions in the transfer risk assessment remaining true, in particular UK or EEA hosting with remote access only, the receiving firm remaining UK established, and the data specification remaining as set in the data sharing agreement. If any fails, the balancing test must be redone before further sharing with that firm.

The conditions of the standing assessment continue to apply unchanged, including the caps on the number of recipient firms and the two layer transparency requirement on every site whose enquiries enter the network.
