<p>This page is a reference set, not an argument. It collects the current UK landlord and buy-to-let company statistics in one place, each figure carrying the period it covers and the source it came from, so that it can be quoted directly. Every number below is derived from the Companies House register via the UK SPV Incorporation Index, which is published in full on the <a href="/research/landlord-tax-index">UK SPV Incorporation Index</a> hub. What the numbers mean, and the charts behind them, live there rather than here.</p>
<p>Two conventions apply throughout. Incorporation counts are gross, so a company formed in 2019 and dissolved in 2024 still counts in 2019. The most recent two months of the series are provisional because Companies House indexes new incorporations with a short lag, so the last settled month in this release is <strong>June 2026</strong>, and July and August 2026 are excluded from every total below. Both conventions are set out in the <em>UK SPV Incorporation Index methodology</em>.</p>

<h2 id="key-statistics">Key statistics</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>69,039</strong> companies were incorporated under SIC 68209, the standard buy-to-let letting code, in calendar year 2025, the highest annual total in the series. Source: Companies House register via the UK SPV Incorporation Index.</li>
<li><strong>4,840</strong> companies were incorporated under SIC 68209 in June 2026, the latest settled month, 20.1% below June 2025.</li>
<li><strong>66,312</strong> companies were incorporated under SIC 68209 in the twelve months to June 2026, and 90,390 across the four-code union over the same twelve months.</li>
<li>Net formation across the four codes was <strong>plus 3,475</strong> in June 2026: 6,614 incorporations less 3,139 dissolutions.</li>
<li><strong>665,645</strong> companies carrying at least one of the four real-estate SIC codes were on the live register at the Companies House bulk snapshot dated 1 September 2026.</li>
<li><strong>London</strong> accounted for 35.3% of property company formations in the twelve months to that snapshot, the largest regional share.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="formations">Annual and monthly formations</h2>
<p>Calendar 2025 is the largest year in the series: <strong>69,039</strong> incorporations under SIC 68209 and <strong>95,140</strong> across the deduplicated four-code union. The previous high was 64,583 (68209) in 2024. The busiest single month on record is September 2025, at 6,672 under 68209 and 9,035 on the union. The monthly average across 2025 was 5,753 under 68209.</p>
<p>The first half of 2026 sits below the equivalent half of 2025. January to June 2026 recorded <strong>32,441</strong> incorporations under SIC 68209, against 35,168 in January to June 2025, a fall of 7.8%. On the union the comparison is 44,076 against 48,826, a fall of 9.7%. The monthly average for the first half of 2026 was 5,407 under 68209. In the single month of June 2026 the count was 4,840, 20.1% below June 2025.</p>

<h2 id="net-formation">Dissolutions and net formation</h2>
<p>Dissolutions are counted by dissolution date across the same four codes. In calendar 2025 there were <strong>40,785</strong>, against 34,664 in 2024 and 31,996 in 2023. In the first six months of 2026 there were 22,836, against 17,999 in the first six months of 2025. In June 2026 alone there were 3,139.</p>
<p>Net formation is the union incorporation count less the union dissolution count in the same month. For June 2026 that is <strong>plus 3,475</strong>. On a full calendar year basis it was plus 54,355 in 2025 and plus 55,193 in 2024. For January to June 2026 it was plus 21,240, against plus 30,827 for January to June 2025. What that balance says about the claim that landlords are quitting the market is worked through in <a href="/blog/incorporation-and-company-structures/are-landlords-really-leaving-net-formation-data">are landlords really leaving</a>.</p>

<h2 id="live-stock">Live stock and age profile</h2>
<p>At the Companies House bulk snapshot dated <strong>1 September 2026</strong>, <strong>665,645</strong> companies on the live register carried at least one of the four real-estate SIC codes. This is a live-register measure: companies formed and since dissolved are absent from it, which is why it cannot be reconciled directly against the gross incorporation counts above. The composition of that stock, including its regional split and how our definition compares with the narrower buy-to-let counts published elsewhere, is set out in <a href="/blog/incorporation-and-company-structures/how-many-buy-to-let-limited-companies-uk">how many buy-to-let limited companies there are in the UK</a>.</p>
<p>The stock is young. <strong>76.7%</strong> of those 665,645 companies were incorporated in 2016 or later, and <strong>53.8%</strong> in 2021 or later. Companies incorporated before 2016 make up 23.3%. The single largest incorporation year represented in the live stock is 2025, at 88,028 companies, or 13.2% of the total. The full banded profile, back to the handful of survivors incorporated in the nineteenth century, is in <a href="/blog/incorporation-and-company-structures/how-old-are-uk-property-companies-age-profile">how old UK property companies are</a>.</p>

<h2 id="growth-since-2016">Growth since 2016</h2>
<p>Annual incorporations under SIC 68209 went from <strong>19,733 in 2016 to 69,039 in 2025</strong>, an increase of <strong>249.9%</strong>, or about 3.5 times. On the four-code union the same span runs from 33,040 in 2016 to 95,140 in 2025, an increase of <strong>188.0%</strong>. Both endpoints are complete calendar years. The full monthly series behind them starts in September 2015 and is charted on the <a href="/research/landlord-tax-index">Index hub</a>.</p>
