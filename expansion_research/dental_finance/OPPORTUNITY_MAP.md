## The reframe
The biggest prize is NOT the dental / SPV-BTL micro-clusters we started with. It is a **shared, mostly-unregulated business-services lead engine** — specialist tax + business finance + exit/EOT — that every estate site can feed, plus a separate property-secured-finance track gated on one IAR. Three of the top clusters are **fully build-now with no reg gate at all**.

## Two hard constraints that shape everything
1. **Every head term is AI-Overview-eaten** and held by banks/gov.uk/Big-4. Low KD is misleading for head terms. ALL clusters = **long-tail + tools/calculators + tax-intersection + segment pages**, never head-on.
2. **Reg splits the program into two tracks:**
   - **Track A (build-now, no gate):** specialist tax, business finance (company-gated), exit/EOT. Fully unregulated, multi-buyer, monetise immediately.
   - **Track B (IAR-gated):** landlord commercial finance + SPV-BTL. One shared broker principal covers both; build educational content now, gate conversion.

## Estate cannibalisation / natural hosts (from GSC sweep)
- **Found money:** property ranks **pos-1 capital allowances** (97 queries) + **pos-1 R&D** (property + generalist). Re-route to specialist-tax buyers now — no build.
- **Greenfield (no estate site ranks):** commercial mortgage, invoice finance/factoring, business loan/unsecured, MCA, EOT, MBO, van/plant finance.
- **Avoid worsening existing internal overlap:** SPV/ltd-co BTL (property+generalist+agency) and R&D (property+generalist+founder) already compete internally — consolidate, don't proliferate.
- **Natural hosts:** specialist tax + landlord commercial finance + BTL → property/generalist; business finance + EOT → new hub or generalist; CIS → tradetaxspecialists; dental → dentalfinancepartners.

## Recommended build sequence
1. **Immediate (days, no build):** re-route existing pos-1 capital-allowances + R&D traffic to specialist-tax buyers; line up specialist-tax buyer relationships.
2. **Track A build (no reg gate):** page-map + generate **specialist-tax hub**, then **business-finance suite (company-gated)**, then **EOT/exit hub**. Same page-mapping rigor as the dental/BTL pass before generating.
3. **Track B (parallel):** secure ONE bridging/commercial + BTL broker IAR principal; build **landlord commercial finance** + **SPV-BTL** educational content now, switch on conversion once IAR signed. Commercial finance leads BTL on value.
4. **Bolt-ons:** dental (extend dentist site), multi-sector practice finance, law-firm finance — low effort, when capacity allows.
