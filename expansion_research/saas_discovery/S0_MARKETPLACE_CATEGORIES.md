Method: public listing summaries on Microns.io (sold listings show ask + revenue), Flippa sold pages, SaaS Watch weekly roundups (aggregating Acquire/Flippa/Microns/LittleExits/SideProjectors), Acquire.com Jan-2026 multiples report, named open-revenue indie companies. Acquire/Flippa gate full financials behind login, so individual-listing figures are seller-claimed unless marked Stripe-verified. Uncorroborated = **[unverified]**.

Market anchors: median profit multiple Acquire.com 2024-2025 = 3.9x annual profit, avg 71% margins, avg 81 days to sale. Micro-SaaS under $1M ARR ~2.85x avg, 6.13x top quartile [unverified aggregation]. Sub-$10k listings priced on hope; at the tiny end revenue is the proof, asking price is noise.

**Pattern across ~120 listings:** real revenue at small scale = (a) boring utilities with free tier ranking for a transactional keyword, or (b) vertical versions of a generic tool. AI-branded listings mostly $0-200 total revenue despite $5-15k asks — that tier fails our filter.

## Agent's top 5 categories

1. **QR generator for one non-English geo** (dynamic-QR paywall + biolink) — QR-Kode.no = complete verified blueprint.
2. **Niche slot-checker/finder tools** (driving tests, exam bookings, cancellations) — pure search intent, zero competition per micro-niche, 2-3 day build; UK DVSA checkers charge £15-25, proven.
3. **Vertical booking/scheduling (one trade)** — low churn (holds the diary), uncontested long-tail.
4. **QR equipment/asset tracking for small trades** — lowest-saturation B2B entry, adjacent to our construction/trades audience.
5. **Screenshot/OG-image API** — deepest proven demand pool; enter via free OG-image generator page, not head-on API keywords.

Avoid despite big numbers: form builders, standalone link-in-bio (free incumbents), social schedulers (platform risk, not SEO-acquired).
