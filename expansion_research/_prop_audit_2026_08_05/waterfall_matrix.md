This is a real ceiling on the data, not an oversight — flagged rather than filled with unsourced numbers.

## 2. Waterfall failures, ranked

1. **Bare "property accountant" (278 impr) splits 11 ways, and the top server is a career page, not a commercial page.** `how-to-become-property-accountant` (career/jobs content) takes 195 of 278 impressions (70%) at pos 24.8. No page with clear commercial intent (`/services`, `/`, `/locations/*`) appears in the top 3 servers for this query. The equity for the site's single highest-volume head term is concentrated on the wrong page type.

2. **"property accountants" (plural, 83 impr) splits 9 ways**, again topped by a page titled for London-specialism (`property-specialist-accountant-london`, 50 impr at pos 42.5) rather than any hub or money page.

3. **Money pages (`/`, `/services`, `/incorporation`, `/calculators`, `/property-tax-rates`) do not appear as servers anywhere in the 20-query head-term pull.** Every impression on "property accountant," "property accountants," "property accountant specialist," "residential property accountant," "buy-to-let property accountants," and all city variants lands on blog posts or `/locations/*`, never on the core commercial pages. This is measured directly from `gsc_headterm_pages.json`: 0 of the ~40 page-slots across the 20 queries point at a core page.

4. **"property accountant south london" (33 impr) and "best property accountants in london" (36 impr) are each split across 3–5 different London-titled posts** (`property-tax-accountant-london`, `london-property-accountant` at two different URL paths, `property-specialist-accountant-london`, `best-property-accountant-london`, `property-accountant-london-expert-services`) plus `/locations/london` (80 impr per `gsc_pages.json`, pos 33.4, not in the head-term server list for either query). London-intent equity is the most fragmented cluster measured: at least 6 distinct URLs targeting overlapping London-property-accountant intent.

5. **Manchester intent duplicated across two posts** (`manchester-property-accountant` and `property-tax-accountant-manchester`) serving the same two queries ("property accountant manchester," "property accountants manchester") at near-identical positions (9.2 vs 9.4, 11.4 vs 12.2) — direct self-competition with no differentiation visible in the query data.

6. **Career/jobs content (3 posts: `how-to-become-property-accountant`, `property-accountant-jobs-uk` x2 duplicate paths, `property-accountant-job-description`) absorbs impressions on commercial-shaped queries** ("property accountant," "property accountant specialist," "residential property accountant," "what does a specialist property accountant do?") that a hiring/career searcher and a landlord searching for a firm would both trigger, with no way in the query string alone to separate intent — but the site's only answer to that ambiguity is career content, at positions 21.5–58.

7. **`/property-tax-rates` is orphaned on every axis measured**: 0 blog in-body links (of 5,979+87+55+15+14 = 6,150 total in-body hrefs counted across the 6 tracked destinations, /property-tax-rates gets 0), not in nav, not in footer, not in homepage body links, not a server for any of the 20 head-term queries.

8. **`/locations/london` and `/locations/manchester` are near-invisible relative to `/locations/leeds`**: 80 impr (pos 33.4) and 6 impr (pos 24.8) respectively vs Leeds' 805+140=945 impr combined (`gsc_pages.json` locations_host_split). London and Manchester are the two largest UK property markets and the weakest-performing `/locations/*` pages in the pull, while equivalent demand is instead being captured (fragmented) by 6+ separate blog posts per city.

9. **Calculators (87 in-body blog links, described in the task as a "246k/mo demand cluster") are absent from the homepage body** (`page.tsx` links `/contact`, `/incorporation`, `/locations`, `/blog`, 2 blog pillars — confirmed by direct read; the calculators section on the homepage is present but calculators are anchor-linked within the page itself via `#calculators`, not linked as a standalone crawlable page from the body copy, and `/calculators` the hub route is not linked from the homepage body at all). `/property-tax-rates` is also absent from the homepage body, matching the known-structure list supplied in the task.

10. **11-page cannibalisation on "property accountant" and the 5-page cannibalisation on "best...london" both route positions no better than pos 21.8**, i.e. even the single best-performing page in each cannibalised cluster sits outside page 1 (pos 1-10) for its own head term — the fragmentation is not just diluting equity across pages that would otherwise rank, the aggregate cluster does not have a page in top-10 position for either of the two largest head terms measured.

|---|---|

## 10-line summary of worst failures

1. The site's #1 head term, "property accountant" (278 impr), is topped by a career/jobs post (195 impr, pos 24.8) — not any commercial page.
2. All core money pages (`/`, `/services`, `/incorporation`, `/calculators`, `/property-tax-rates`) serve zero impressions across all 20 top head-term queries pulled.
3. "property accountant" splits across 11 different URLs; "property accountants" splits across 9; neither cluster has a page in the top 10 positions.
4. London-property-accountant intent is fragmented across at least 6 URLs (2 of them literal slug/path duplicates) plus `/locations/london`, none ranking better than pos 19.
5. Manchester intent is duplicated across 2 near-identical posts competing for the same 2 queries at pos 9.2 vs 9.4 and 11.4 vs 12.2.
6. `/property-tax-rates` has zero internal links (nav, footer, homepage body, or blog in-body) and zero head-term GSC presence — fully orphaned.
7. `/locations/london` (80 impr, pos 33.4) and `/locations/manchester` (6 impr, pos 24.8) badly underperform `/locations/leeds` (945 impr combined) despite London and Manchester being larger markets.
8. Calculators and `/property-tax-rates` are absent from the homepage body links; calculators only exist as an on-page anchor section, not a linked hub route, from the homepage.
9. Two confirmed category-frontmatter duplicate pairs (`property-accountant-jobs-uk`, `london-property-accountant`) each split equity for identical content across two URLs.
10. Canonical host is www-configured but `/locations/leeds` (and likely `/locations/london`, `/locations/manchester`) show a measured live www/non-www serving split in GSC, and the `/embed` gallery page is unindexed-unprotected while its children are noindexed.
