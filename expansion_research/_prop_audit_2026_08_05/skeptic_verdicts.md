# Adversarial verdicts on the five decision-driving conclusions (Opus skeptics, 2026-08-05)

## 1. "Far from ceiling" — SURVIVES-WITH-CAVEATS
Dilution attack contradicted by measurement: monthly decomposition shows avg position IMPROVED 13.8 (Apr) to 10.1 (Jul) while impressions grew 6.7x and clicks 73x; CTR rose monotonically (0.10% to 1.05%). Pages 78 to 725. Aug run-rate 2,664 impr/day (2 weekend days only) is above July's 2,499/day, zero plateau signal but also zero information. Caveats: (a) it is an impressions ceiling verdict, not a revenue ceiling; the ~600-page tail is informational at 0.26% CTR / pos ~30, activating it buys impressions not leads; (b) launch curve off a near-zero base (Mar 230 impr) proves non-saturation, not durable headroom; first real read when Aug-Sep flatten against a mature base.

## 2. "Gap = commercial capture, not content volume; CTR weakness = query mix" — SURVIVES-WITH-CAVEATS; query-mix clause REFUTED
Query-mix clause dead: informational CTR 0.26% at pos 29.8 is exactly what position predicts, no residual anomaly for query mix to explain; Bing leg unfalsifiable (no position field in API; 6-9% norm unsupported, real mid-page Bing norm plausibly 2-4%, site at 2.51% may be near-normal). "CTR weakness" is a restatement of "ranks page 3". Capture gap survives on one clean fact: landlord tax 2,900/mo, 0 Google impressions exact, while Bing serves it 1,064 impr / 121 clicks / 11.4% CTR. Zero on one engine while the other converts the same term = serving/targeting failure, not authority ceiling (unauthoritative sites get impressions at pos 40, not zero). Authority-ceiling hypothesis remains UNEXCLUDED for the head terms (Bing also serves us only 45 impr on them). W1/W2 remain the cheap test.
Bucket validation (100-query hand-label): commercial precision 1.00 recall 0.80, commercial impressions understated ~25% (4,856 -> ~6,080), so the gap is real and slightly larger. BUT form/HMRC recall 0.38 (unusable as published; rate-table blindness: LBTT/ATED/devolved lookups leak to informational) and informational total roughly 2x overstated (19,033 -> ~10,400 truth). Regex precedence a non-issue (0.1% of impressions).

## 3. "Local pages wrong, consolidate into national pages" — SURVIVES-WITH-CAVEATS; 301 DIRECTION REFUTED
Survives: intra-city duplication measured (6 London pages + /locations/london, 814 impr, 0 clicks); Bing veto clear (0 of 191 Bing pages are city pages). Refuted: (a) "zero volume" not established (Google Ads rounds to 0; landlord tax burn proves tool blindness; autocomplete shows near-me demand on every seed); (b) denominator wrong (24 real city assets, not 40); (c) 1 click at pos 12-24 is exactly what the site-wide CTR curve predicts, cannot indict city pages specifically; (d) city queries are the site's BEST commercial positions (9.2-14.8) vs national head terms (25-34): redirecting pos-10 assets into pos-25 targets destroys the only page-1-adjacent commercial rankings. Amended playbook: consolidate duplicates WITHIN each city into one canonical city page (keep /locations/[slug]), after canonicalisation fix settles; London first is right.

## 4. "Decision-stage content wins; stop commissioning reference content" — REFUTED (the ban half)
Top-10 = one n=8 outlier + noise: every other named winner is n=2; two of the top 10 are commercial/local pages not decision-informational; 28 of 52 blog leads sit in an uninspected 1-lead tail across 746 posts. No denominators anywhere (raw lead counts track traffic, not conversion propensity). 54% of leads (32 homepage + 17 unattributed + 14 other of 116) have no blog entry; self-referral host carries 8 July leads, proving multi-session journeys that last-session attribution hides; reference pages could be systematically first-touch and score zero by construction. The claimed mechanism also fails: form/HMRC CTR 0.19% vs informational 0.26%, same near-zero, no differential; sa105 form post (37 clicks/90d) is itself a reference winner the plan freezes. SALVAGE: "commission more decision-stage" fine as a bet; the categorical stop on reference content is unsupported; keep per-page judgment.

## 5. "Geography: hold" — SURVIVES-WITH-CAVEATS (wrong load-bearing argument)
ccTLD claim overstated: GSC geo-targeting unavailable for ccTLDs (true) but ccTLD is a relevance signal not a filter; link authority is domain-level and crosses borders; hreflang on /us/ is the documented mechanism. Correct weaker claim: .co.uk is a persistent handicap vs .com incumbents on US commercial terms (mostly local-intent, the exact class Property loses at home). "Near-zero marginal cost" also wrong: build system transfers, content does not (Opus-only A* authoring is the expensive half). Decisive real grounds: (1) no US buyer relationship, rankings without a buyer are unmonetisable; (2) landlord tax 2,900/mo uncaptured in the home market proves the capability gap travels. Re-found the hold on those two; demote ccTLD to handicap.
