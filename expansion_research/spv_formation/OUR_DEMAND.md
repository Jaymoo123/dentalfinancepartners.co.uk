## Reading (honest, not sums-as-totals)

`gsc_query_data` is a PARTIAL/sampled table (per standard_terms: never sum it as a site total).
Bing figures below are a single trailing-window snapshot, not 90 days of daily rows. Both sets below
are relative demand signals inside our own SPV/formation query set only, not estate totals and not
external market volume.

1. **Transfer-existing-property dwarfs new-formation intent.** "transfer existing property" (9,625 impr,
   434 queries) beats "how to set up" (2,076 impr, 181 queries) by 4-5x. Our audience is mostly landlords
   who already own property and are weighing moving it into a company, not people starting from zero.
2. **SDLT/incorporation-relief mechanics are the single hottest cluster.** The top non-holding-company
   query (sdlt transfer property to limited company connected party 2025, 721 impr) and several
   neighbours all point at `sdlt-transfer-property-company-cost` and
   `how-to-transfer-property-into-limited-company-uk`, genuine, specific, high-intent queries.
3. **One outlier query (1,977 impr, "uk holding company non-resident board meetings uk") sits at
   site-level Bing data (`__site__`, no page attributed) with 0 clicks and pos 5.5**, likely a
   Bing-side query-normalisation artefact bundling several real queries; worth a manual look before
   acting on it as one signal.
4. **We already rank well on SDLT/transfer mechanics** (several queries at position 1.5-3.9 on
   `sdlt-transfer-property-company-cost` and `how-to-transfer-property-into-limited-company-uk`) but
   badly on broad incorporation queries, `landlord portfolio incorporation uk` (pos 52.6),
   `property incorporation capital gains tax` (pos 70.3), `stamp duty on property incorporation uk`
   (pos 50.3). The mechanics pages work; the higher-level "should I incorporate my portfolio" pages
   don't yet.
5. **"other/general SPV" (799 queries, 11,342 impr) is the largest bucket by volume but converts near
   nothing** (1,424 clicks estate-wide across it, many individual 0-click rows), it's a long tail of
   loosely-matching queries (my regex bucket-of-last-resort), not one coherent intent; don't read it as
   a single opportunity.
6. **Holding-company queries are impression-heavy, click-dead** (2,659 impr, only 30 clicks across 64
   queries) on generalist, either the wrong audience finding these pages or a snippet/title mismatch,
   given `holding-company-structure-uk-tax` sits at position 77.3 for its own top query.
7. **Non-resident/expat SPV demand is real but tiny in our own data** (8 queries, 162 impr, 0 clicks) —
   too small in this snapshot to call a bucket, worth cross-checking against external keyword tools
   before investing.
8. **Mortgages-in-company queries (33 queries, 276 impr, 76 clicks) convert better than their volume
   suggests**, a 27% click share against impressions, higher than most other buckets, suggesting this
   sub-intent is currently under-served relative to demand.
