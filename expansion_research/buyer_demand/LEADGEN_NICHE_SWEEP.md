# Lead-gen niche sweep — what else is there?

Date: 2026-08-08. 16 candidate verticals, ~13,000 keywords, plus Labs difficulty on 142 head terms
and 6 live SERP checks. Seeds `seeds5.json`, data `sweep5.csv`, raw `raw/difficulty5.json`,
`raw/serp5_*.json`.

**Short answer: distressed sale is not the only option and is not the best risk-adjusted one.**
There are at least seven cleaner niches with lower competition and comparable or better lead value.

## Tier 1 — build these

### 1. Settlement agreements / employment law — the standout
| KD | Vol | CPC | Keyword |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 9,900 | **£41.59** | settlement agreement lawyer |
| **0** | 2,900 | £30.53 | settlement agreement solicitor |
| 7 | 2,400 | £21.13 | employment law advice (+ "for employers") |
| 33 | 1,300 | £43.30 | acas employer advice |

SERP: `settlementagreement.com` at #1 and `gte-settlementagreements.co.uk` at #10 are single-purpose
niche sites, not law firms — the model is already proven on this exact term. Trustpilot, Citizens
Advice and mid-size firms (Moore Barlow, Irwin Mitchell) fill the rest. Nothing structural blocking us.

Why it is the best find: KD 0-1 at £40 CPC is the strongest ratio in the entire dataset, employment
solicitors buy leads readily, the client is an employee who has just been handed an agreement and
needs a solicitor *today* (statutory requirement — they must take independent advice, and the
employer usually pays the fee, so conversion is unusually high), and we already run a Solicitors brand.

## Tier 2 — strong, secondary

**Commercial property / business rates.** `small business rates relief` 6,600 @ £6.54 **KD 0**,
`business rate` 18,100 @ £7.67 KD 13, `commercial epc` 1,300 @ £20.45 **KD 0**. Caveat: the rates-appeal
sector has a cowboy reputation (unsolicited agents, VOA warnings), so the clean entry is commercial
EPC / MEES compliance rather than appeals.

**Insolvency — MVL specifically.** `members voluntary liquidation` 880 @ £19.64 **KD 0**,
`creditors voluntary liquidation` 1,300 @ £21.20 KD 13. MVL is a *solvent* closure, so no distressed
client, and IP fees are £2-4k, making leads very valuable. SERP is Begbies Traynor, Clarke Bell,
Crowe — beatable mid-market firms.

**Landlord compliance certificates.** `eicr certificates` 18,100 @ £5.84, `fire risk assessment cost`
720 @ £16.20 **KD 0**, `landlord licensing` 1,600 @ £7.35 KD 11. High volume, low difficulty, and it
plugs straight into the existing Property landlord audience.

**Health & safety consultancy.** `health and safety consultant` 1,600 @ **£70.44** KD 24,
`health and safety advisors` 720 @ £69.92 KD 27. SERP is HSE, Citation and small consultancies.
£70 CPC B2B with moderate difficulty.

**Trademark / IP.** `register trademark uk` 4,400 @ £6.46 KD 9-15. Clean, transactional, low KD.

**Planning consultancy.** `architect for extension` 480 @ £10.90 KD 11, `planning permission
consultant` 170 @ £8.60 **KD 1**, `agricultural permitted development rights` 170 @ £25.87 **KD 0**.
Low volume but very soft and high case value.

## Tier 3 — avoid, with reasons

- **Van / motor insurance** — 27,100 @ £22.28 looks enormous, but FCA-authorised introduction plus
  GoCompare/CompareTheMarket. Structurally closed.
- **Equity release** (`equity release calculator` 6,600 @ £18.64 KD 3) and **funeral plans**
  (2,900 @ £22.78) — both FCA-regulated since 2022, both ethically contested with vulnerable
  customers. The low KD is a trap: the barrier is permissions, not rankings.
- **Business energy** — the highest CPCs in the whole sweep (`compare business electricity prices`
  1,900 @ **£102.88**, `compare business gas prices` 880 @ £110.44, KD 20-37). Tempting, but the TPI
  broker sector is under Ofgem scrutiny with an ombudsman redress scheme and a mis-selling
  reputation. Same grey-area objection you raised about distressed sale.
- **Immigration** — KD 36-50 and vulnerable clients.
- **Tutoring** (KD 0 across the board) and **construction trades** — marketplace-locked by
  Checkatrade/MyBuilder/Superprof, and low lead value.
- **Solar** — 5,400 @ £7.91 but KD 27-36 and a cowboy-ridden sector.

## The pattern worth noticing

Every Tier 1 winner is the same shape: **a regulated professional (employment solicitor, leasehold
solicitor, RICS surveyor, insolvency practitioner) who is good at their job, legally barred from
aggressive marketing, and structurally bad at SEO.** That is precisely the estate's existing model
and the answer to the buyer-shortage problem in `FINDINGS.md` — these buyers already exist, already
pay for leads, and are reachable through their professional bodies rather than through search.

It also means the editorial position is advisory and pro-consumer in every case, which is
the opposite of the distressed-sale problem.
