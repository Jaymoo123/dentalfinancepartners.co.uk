## What each angle actually contains

**Agent-as-client is a mirage.** `agent_tax_accounting` looks like 2,410/mo, but the top rows are
`plaza estates agency ltd` 480, `lomond property lettings limited` 170, `connells limited` 140 —
people looking up companies, not accountants. The genuine term, `accountant for estate agents`, is
**70/mo @ £13.55**. `vat on estate agent fees` (140) and its variants are *vendors* asking whether
they pay VAT on a bill, not agents asking about their own returns. There is no accounting niche here.

**Agent-as-lead-buyer is dead.** Confirmed twice. From the earlier pull, the entire
landlord-supply-buyer corpus (`how to get landlord instructions`, `letting agent lead generation`,
`vendor leads estate agents`) = **10/mo**. Agents do not search to buy leads. The 880/mo
`agent lead generation` @ £49 seen earlier is US realtor SaaS, not UK.

**Agent back-office is small but genuinely underserved.** `how to start an estate agency` /
`how to run an estate agency` = 170 each @ £8.54. `letting agent accounting software` is 10/mo but
**£50.89 CPC** — a real, expensive, tiny market. Client-money-protection and client-account
reconciliation terms returned essentially nothing, which means the pain is real but nobody searches
for it. Not an SEO play.

**Selling SEO to agents is a service line, not a niche.** `estate agent seo` / `seo for estate
agencies` = 480 @ £13.16, plus `seo for letting agents` 50, `local seo for estate agents` 90.
Call it ~700/mo of clean UK intent at £13 CPC. That is small, but it is the highest-intent B2B
pocket found, and it is literally what we do.

## Read

Estate agents are a bad **client** niche and a bad **lead-buyer** niche. Every B2B angle lands in the
2k/mo band and most of that is contamination. Do not build an "accountant for estate agents" site,
do not build an agent lead-gen product.

But the **vendor** — the person about to sell — is a large, high-CPC, calculator-shaped audience that
sits directly adjacent to what Property already does. Someone searching `estate agent fees uk` or
`sell house without estate agent` is 0-3 months from a disposal. That single person is simultaneously:

- a **CGT lead** (disposal of a second property / former let)
- a **conveyancing lead** (£16 CPC corpus, confirmed earlier)
- a **mortgage lead** (onward purchase — strongest adjacent buyer at 260/mo @ £14)
- an **accountant lead** if they are a landlord exiting

That is the same multi-sell logic from the buyer research, except this time the front-end corpus is
45k/mo rather than 960/mo. `estate agent fees calculator uk` at 720/mo is a single-page build with a
calculator we can ship in a day, and a calculator is the natural place to ask "are you selling a
property you have let out?" — which is the qualifying question that turns a vendor into a CGT lead.

## Recommended

1. **Cost-of-selling calculator + fee cluster on Property.** Target `estate agent fees uk`,
   `estate agent fees calculator uk`, `how much do estate agents charge`, `average estate agent fees
   uk`, `cheapest estate agent fees`. Output total cost of selling: agent fee, conveyancing, EPC,
   removals, **and CGT if it is not your main home** — the CGT line is the lead capture.
2. **`sell house without estate agent` cluster.** 390/mo × 3 variants at £13.46, highest CPC in the
   clean vendor set, and a private-sale seller needs conveyancing and tax advice more than anyone.
3. **`seo for estate agents` as a service page on the agency brand**, not a new site. ~700/mo, £13
   CPC, on-brand. One page, no programme.
4. **Skip entirely:** accountant-for-estate-agents, agent lead-gen product, client-account /
   compliance content, franchise and self-employed-agent angles.
