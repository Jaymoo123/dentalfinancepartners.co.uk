## The steps, in order

### Step 1. Get the two key guides indexed (highest value fix)
- **What:** a genuine refresh of the CGT gifting guide and the "tax when selling a rental" guide under the rewrite programme (full overhaul driven by their current search data, A* bar, Opus writer, two Opus QA tracks). Then request indexing for each in Search Console (legitimate: the pages will have changed).
- **Why:** together they own 1,540 commercial searches and Google dropped them after last reading them in early July. Pages Google re-read since August were indexed 93 to 98% of the time.
- **Cost:** about 6 agents (1 writer and 2 QA per guide). **Blast radius:** two blog posts. **Revert:** git.
- **When:** written during the freeze, shipped after 23 October.

### Step 2. Hand-off links from the owning guides (small, highest value per link)
- **What:** one natural sentence in each owning guide that hands ready readers to its sales page, written to the link rubric (your standard, quoted in it): how-to transfer guide and section 162 guide to the incorporation page; buy-to-let limited company guide to the SPV page; second-home guide to the selling page; landlord tax pillar to the tax advice page; and the two refreshed guides once indexed. Plus the reverse link from each sales page back to its guide (best practice, LE-22).
- **Count:** about 14 sentences. **Cost:** 1 Opus writer, 1 Opus reviewer. You read every sentence.
- **When:** written now, shipped after 23 October.

### Step 3. Service pages: judge on fact, not hope
- **What:** check that indexing was requested on 9 October (if not: 3 clicks in Search Console). On 23 October, run the day-14 read and URL Inspection. If Google has re-read them, judge them; if not, wait.
- **Cost:** free.

### Step 4. Re-run the link plan on the final map, then wave 1
- **What:** re-run page assignment, budget and gap list on the corrected map (cheap), then the guides that already bring leads first, in priority order, only into pages marked ready.
- **Cost:** re-run about 1 agent; wave 1 priced at that point.

### Step 5. Hygiene (independent, small)
- Fix the 6 broken links in FAQ answers.
- Give the two near-duplicate CGT calculation guides clearly different angles (never-collapse rule).
- Correct the "editorial team typed as a person" schema fault.
