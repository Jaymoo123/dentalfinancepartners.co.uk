Companies House ID Verification Begins Today: What Changed on 18 November 2025 and What You Need to Do

On 18 November 2025, identity verification at Companies House moved from voluntary to mandatory. If you accept a director appointment or become a Person with Significant Control on or after that date, the company cannot lawfully record the appointment on the public register until you have completed identity verification under the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023 (ECCTA 2023) and obtained a Companies House personal code. If you were already a director or PSC on 17 November 2025, nothing changed for you instantly: you have a twelve-month transition window, during which the company's next confirmation statement must carry verified personal codes for every in-scope individual, ending around November 2026.
