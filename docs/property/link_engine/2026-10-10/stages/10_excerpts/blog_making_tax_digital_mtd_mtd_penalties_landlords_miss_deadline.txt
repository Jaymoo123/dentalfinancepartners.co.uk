The good news first: the 2026/27 soft landing gov.uk states it directly: "There are no penalties for missing a quarterly update deadline for the 2026 to 2027 tax year." That is the soft landing, and it is real. If you miss a quarterly update in the first mandated year, you collect no penalty points and pay no £200 fine for that miss.
