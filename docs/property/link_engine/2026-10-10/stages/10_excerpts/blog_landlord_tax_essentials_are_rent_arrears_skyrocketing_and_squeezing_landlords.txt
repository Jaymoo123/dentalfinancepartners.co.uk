The Post-RRA-2025 Possession Timetable: 13 Weeks Plus 3 Weeks Renters' Rights Act 2025 Sch 1 para 24, in force from 1 May 2026 per SI 2026/421 reg 2, made two operative changes to Housing Act 1988 Sch 2 Ground 8 (the mandatory possession ground for rent arrears): The arrears threshold rose from 8 weeks to 13 weeks. The tenant must be 13 weeks in arrears at both the notice date and the court hearing for Ground 8 to be made out.
