Corporation Tax Group Relief for Property Companies: Surrendering Losses Across SPVs

Many UK landlords end up with more than one limited company. The standard reasons are mortgage-driven (lenders prefer a clean single-asset SPV per property), liability-driven (segregating risk between properties), or tax-driven (different investor groups owning different shares of different properties). Whatever the route in, once a landlord has three, four or five property companies, the question of whether losses in one can be used to shelter profits in another moves from academic to material. Corporation tax group relief is the answer in most cases, and the regime is more accessible than landlords typically assume. This page works through the mechanics for a typical property group: the 75% direct-or-indirect ownership test, what can and cannot be surrendered, the corresponding accounting period rule, the CT600 mechanics for making the claim, the parallel mechanism for capital losses under section 171A TCGA 1992, and the failure modes we see most often.
