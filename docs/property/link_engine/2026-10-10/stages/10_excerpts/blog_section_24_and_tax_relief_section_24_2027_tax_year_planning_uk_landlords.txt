Section 24 and the 2027 Tax Year: What Actually Changes for UK Landlords?

The 2027/28 tax year is the biggest change to how UK landlords are taxed since Section 24 (S24) was phased in. From 6 April 2027, rental profit stops being taxed at your ordinary income tax rates and gets its own set of rates: 22%, 42% and 47%. Section 24 does not go away, and the way it works does not change. Three things change on 6 April 2027, and one important thing does not. The change is enacted law, not a proposal: it was announced at the Autumn Budget 2025 and is set out in Finance Act 2026 (c.11), which received Royal Assent on 18 March 2026. The headline is a flat 2 percentage point premium on property income at every band. Crucially, the Section 24 credit rises by the same 2 points, from 20% to 22%, so it tracks the new basic rate.
