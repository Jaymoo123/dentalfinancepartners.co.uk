Dilapidations and VAT: Supply or Damages at Lease End?

At the lease-end handover the VAT question on dilapidations finally has to be answered. Surveyor on site, schedule of dilapidations served, the tenant's counter-quote in the file, a number on the table both sides can live with. Then the invoice question: do you add 20 percent VAT to the settlement, or does the agreed sum stand alone outside the scope of VAT entirely? On a £180,000 settlement that is £36,000 of VAT either charged or not charged, and the money flows very differently for each side depending on the answer. HMRC's published position has been stable for years and survived the wider 2020 and 2022 reset of the compensation-payments framework. VATSC05910 treats genuine dilapidations as normally outside the scope of VAT. The reasoning in the manual is that a dilapidations settlement lacks the reciprocal link to a supply that VAT requires: the tenant pays for breaching a contractual obligation to maintain, not for any further service you provide in return.
