Land Remediation Relief is the most generous corporation tax relief in the property cleanup space and the most procedurally narrow. The headline rate is a 150 per cent deduction on qualifying expenditure (a standard 100 per cent deduction at Corporation Tax Act 2009 section 1147 plus an additional 50 per cent deduction at section 1149). Loss-making companies can surrender the resulting qualifying land remediation loss for a payable cash credit at 16 per cent under section 1154. The relief is corporate-only, regime-agnostic between residential and commercial use, and gated on a strict polluter-pays exclusion at section 1150 that strips relief where the contamination or dereliction was caused wholly or partly by the claimant or a connected person.
