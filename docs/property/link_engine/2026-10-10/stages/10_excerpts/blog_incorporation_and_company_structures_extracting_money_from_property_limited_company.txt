Extracting Money from a Property Limited Company: Salary, Dividends, Pension and DLA Repayment Compared

Once the property portfolio is inside a limited company, getting the cash out to the director efficiently is the recurring question. Four real levers are available: salary up to the personal allowance, dividends after the £500 allowance, employer pension contributions paid by the company, and director's loan account repayment where a credit balance exists. Each carries a different combination of corporation tax, income tax, national insurance, and pension-allowance consequences. This page compares the four routes side by side and works through a £40,000-per-year extraction example from a property company generating £150,000 of profit.
