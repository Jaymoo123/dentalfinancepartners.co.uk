How to Book an EPC: The Fastest Route to a Valid Certificate

The fastest way to get a valid Energy Performance Certificate is often not to book one at all. EPCs last ten years, they survive a sale and any number of re-lets, and every certificate ever lodged sits on a free public register you can search in under a minute. So the correct first step is to check whether your property already has one. If it does not, booking is quick: find an accredited assessor through the government's own service, get the visit done inside a week in most areas, and the certificate is lodged a day or two later. What follows is that process in order: the register check, who is legally allowed to produce your certificate, the three ways to book and what each adds to the price, the realistic timeline, what happens on the day, and the preparation that can improve the band you end up with. The rules cited here are the Energy Performance of Buildings (England and Wales) Regulations 2012, so they apply in England and Wales; the gov.uk register and booking services also cover Northern Ireland under its own parallel regulations, while Scotland runs a separate system through its own register, covered at the end.
