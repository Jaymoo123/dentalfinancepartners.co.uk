Section 24 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 2015 stopped individual landlords deducting residential mortgage interest from rental income. It phased in over four years from April 2017 and has applied in full since the 2020/21 tax year. The operative provisions sit in ITTOIA 2005 sections 274AA to 274C and ITA 2007 section 399B.
