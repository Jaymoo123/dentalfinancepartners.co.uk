Corporation tax rates for property companies in 2026/27 A property company is taxed on its profits at exactly the same rates as any other UK company. There is no special property rate. What is specific to property is one anti-avoidance rule, the close investment-holding company charge, covered further down. The three rates for the financial year from 1 April 2026 are unchanged from 2025/26. The £50,000 small profits rate A company with three buy-to-let properties producing £40,000 of taxable profit pays corporation tax at 19% on the whole amount. This rate applies whether the profit is kept in the company
