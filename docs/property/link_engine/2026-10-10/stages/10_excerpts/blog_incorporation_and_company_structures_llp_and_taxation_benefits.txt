Tell us your numbers and a specialist will send your figure and the next sensible step, with no obligation. The tax-transparency baseline An LLP is a body corporate at law. Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000 s.1(2) registers it as such at Companies House. But for income tax it is treated as a partnership. ITTOIA 2005 s.863 provides the transparency. Each member is taxed individually on their share of LLP profits per the LLP agreement; the LLP itself pays no income tax. For capital gains tax the same transparency applies through TCGA 1992 s.59A, operationalised by HMRC Statement of Practice D12. The transparency has one important disapplication. ITTOIA 2005 s.863(2) to (3) disapplies the partnership treatment on commencement of liquidation or winding-up. An LLP in liquidation is taxed as a body corporate for the wind-up period.
