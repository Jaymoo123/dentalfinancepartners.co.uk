ATED Relief and Related Persons: When Market Rent Does Not Cure Connection

The most common ATED relief, Property Rental Business Relief under s.133 Finance Act 2013, requires the dwelling to be let to a tenant who is not connected with the holding company on commercial terms. The "commercial terms" element is widely understood: an open-market rent, a standard AST, evidence of arm's-length negotiation. The "not connected" element is widely misunderstood: most directors expect that paying full market rent through a properly-documented tenancy cures the family-letting position. It does not. The connection under s.1122 CTA 2010 is a status test; the rent does not affect it. This page walks through ten family-letting scenarios in turn, sets out HMRC's likely stance on each, and closes with the documentary discipline that supports relief in the cases where it is genuinely available.
