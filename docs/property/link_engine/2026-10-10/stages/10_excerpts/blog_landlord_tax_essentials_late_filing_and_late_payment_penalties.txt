Late Filing and Late Payment Penalties: The Self-Assessment Cascade for Individual Landlords (Pre-MTD and MTD ITSA from April 2026)

Miss your self-assessment deadline and the penalty you face from 6 April 2026 depends entirely on one number: your qualifying income. The regime is splitting in two. If your qualifying property and trading income sits below the Making Tax Digital for Income Tax Self Assessment (MTD ITSA) threshold, you stay on the long-established Finance Act 2009 cascades. If it sits above, you move to a newer and tighter architecture: a points-based late-submission regime under Finance Act 2021 Schedule 24, plus an accelerated late-payment regime introduced in the Spring Statement 2025. The split is mandatory, not optional, and it follows the phased threshold (£50,000 from 6 April 2026, £30,000 from 6 April 2027, £20,000 from 6 April 2028). So which one applies to you? With £52,000 gross rent in 2024/25 you hit MTD ITSA from 6 April 2026 and face the new regime from that point. With £40,000 gross rent you stay on the legacy regime until the £30,000 mandate bites in April 2027. The same default (a missed filing or a missed payment) produces different numbers under the two regimes, so the first thing to get right is knowing which regime governs you in any given tax year.
