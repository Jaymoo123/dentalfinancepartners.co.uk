Take the rent, take off the mortgage payment, then take off everything else the property costs you to run. £1,200 of rent, less a £600 mortgage payment, less £250 of other costs, leaves £350 a month. Twelve months of that is £4,200 a year. A £130,000 interest-only loan at 5.5% costs about £600 a month, which is where that middle figure comes from.
