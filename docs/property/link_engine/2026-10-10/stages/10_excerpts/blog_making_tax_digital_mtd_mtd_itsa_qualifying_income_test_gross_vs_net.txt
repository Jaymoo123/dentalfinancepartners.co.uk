The qualifying income test in one sentence Qualifying income for MTD ITSA equals gross self-employment turnover plus gross UK and overseas property receipts, aggregated across all your trades and lets, measured for the reference year, before any expenses or reliefs come off. The statutory basis is Schedule A1 to the Finance (No. 2) Act 2017, as amended by subsequent Finance Acts to install the £50,000 / £30,000 / £20,000 phased thresholds.
