How a development finance facility is sized Development lenders cap the loan by two separate tests, and lend against whichever gives the lower figure: a percentage of the gross development value (GDV), the expected value once the scheme is finished and sold, and a percentage of total cost, the loan-to-cost (LTC) ratio. The loan-to-GDV cap protects the lender if the finished value comes in lower than expected, while the loan-to-cost cap ensures the developer has meaningful equity or profit tied up in the scheme. Whichever of the two produces the smaller loan is usually the effective ceiling on what a lender will advance. Any gap between the total cost and the maximum facility has to be found from the developer's own funds, or a second layer of finance. Profit on cost, the gross profit as a percentage of total spend, is a common quick check lenders and developers both use to judge whether a scheme has enough margin to be worth the risk.
