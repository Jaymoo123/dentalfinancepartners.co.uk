The work What a property accountant does for you A property accountant keeps the records, prepares the returns and plans the tax for people who own rental and investment property, and we do all three as one service. Most people use several of the six services below. Rental accounts and Self Assessment We prepare your rental accounts property by property, then complete the property pages of your Self Assessment return from them. What you get: a profit figure for each property, so you can see which one carries the rest. What we check: the finance cost restriction applied correctly, every expense claimed, and joint ownership split the way the property is actually owned. Property company and SPV accounts We prepare and file the year-end accounts and corporation tax return for a property company, often called an SPV (special purpose vehicle). What you get: accounts filed on time, a director's loan account that reconciles, and a plan for drawing money out. Why it differs: a company deducts its mortgage interest in full, which changes where your next purchase should sit.
