The accounting-standards decision: FRS 105 vs FRS 102 The first operational decision a BTL LtdCo makes is which accounting standard to apply. The choice is made at first accounts and is operationally costly to reverse. Three regimes are available. FRS 105: the micro-entity regime FRS 105 is the default for most single-SPV BTL limited companies.
