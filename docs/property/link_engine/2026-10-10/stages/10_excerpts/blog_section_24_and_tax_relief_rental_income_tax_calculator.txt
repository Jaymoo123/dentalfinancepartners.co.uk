How to work out your rental income tax, step by step Whatever rental income tax calculator you use, it should follow this sequence. Skip a step and the figure is wrong, usually in HMRC's favour. 1. Total your gross rental income Add up everything received for the tax year (6 April to 5 April): rent, service charges you collect from tenants, ground rent you receive, and any lease premiums. Most landlords use the cash basis by default (income when received, expenses when paid) if gross receipts are 150,000 pounds or less. Above that, or by election, you use the accruals basis and count rent on the date it falls due. 2. Deduct allowable revenue expenses Subtract costs incurred wholly and exclusively for the letting.
