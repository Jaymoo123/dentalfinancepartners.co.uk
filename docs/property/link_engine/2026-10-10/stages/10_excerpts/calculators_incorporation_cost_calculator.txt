What incorporating a property actually costs Moving a personally-held rental into a limited company is a sale to the company at market value. That triggers two upfront taxes: Capital Gains Tax on the gain (18% within the basic-rate band and 24% above, for residential property) and Stamp Duty Land Tax, because the company is acquiring an additional dwelling, so the 5% surcharge applies on top of the standard SDLT bands. Against those costs, a company deducts mortgage interest in full (Section 24 does not apply to companies) and pays Corporation Tax (19% on profits up to £50,000, rising toward 25%) rather than income tax. The annual tax saving versus personal ownership is what eventually repays the upfront cost. This calculator estimates that break-even point.
