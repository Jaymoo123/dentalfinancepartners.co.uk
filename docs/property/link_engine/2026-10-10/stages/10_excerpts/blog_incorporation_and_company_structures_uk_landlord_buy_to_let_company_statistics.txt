UK Landlord and Buy-to-Let Company Statistics

This page is a reference set, not an argument. It collects the current UK landlord and buy-to-let company statistics in one place, each figure carrying the period it covers and the source it came from, so that it can be quoted directly. Every number below is derived from the Companies House register via the UK SPV Incorporation Index, which is published in full on the UK SPV Incorporation Index hub. What the numbers mean, and the charts behind them, live there rather than here. Two conventions apply throughout. Incorporation counts are gross, so a company formed in 2019 and dissolved in 2024 still counts in 2019. The most recent two months of the series are provisional because Companies House indexes new incorporations with a short lag, so the last settled month in this release is June 2026, and July and August 2026 are excluded from every total below. Both conventions are set out in the UK SPV Incorporation Index methodology. Key statistics 69,039 companies were incorporated under SIC 68209, the standard buy-to-let letting code, in calendar year 2025, the highest annual total in the series. Source: Companies House register via the UK SPV Incorporation Index. 4,840 companies were incorporated under SIC 68209 in June 2026, the latest settled month, 20.1% below June 2025. 66,312 companies were incorporated under SIC 68209 in the twelve months to June 2026, and 90,390 across the four-code union over the same twelve months. Net formation across the four codes was plus 3,475 in June 2026: 6,614 incorporations less 3,139 dissolutions.
