Why Liverpool Landlords Now Reach for a Specialist Property Accountant Three things have reshaped your tax position as a Liverpool landlord in the last six years: Section 24 phase-in. Since the 2020-21 tax year, mortgage interest on personally-held BTLs is no longer a deductible expense. It is given as a basic-rate (20%) tax reducer.
