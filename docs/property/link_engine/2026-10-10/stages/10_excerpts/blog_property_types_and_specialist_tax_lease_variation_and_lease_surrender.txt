Section 43(3)(b): the SDLT treatment of surrender The common SDLT-adviser misstatement is 'there is no SDLT on surrender'. The statute says otherwise. FA 2003 s.43(3)(b) treats the surrender or release of a chargeable interest as BOTH an acquisition by any person whose interest or right is benefitted or enlarged by the transaction AND a disposal by the person ceasing to be entitled to the interest.
