If you are reconstructing a 2024/25 capital-allowances claim, checking a return you have already filed before the amendment window shuts, or trying to square guidance written "for 2024/25" against the rules today, the figure that anchors everything is this: the Annual Investment Allowance (AIA) gave 100% first-year relief on qualifying plant and machinery up to £1 million for 2024/25. The cap is the easy part. The traps are in who could claim, what counted, and how much of it has moved since. Annual Investment Allowance 2024/25 at a glance For 2024/25 the headline numbers were stable. AIA gave 100% first-year relief on qualifying plant and machinery up to a permanent cap of £1 million, set by CAA 2001 section 51A(5) ("The maximum allowance is £1,000,000"). That cap had been made permanent by Finance (No. 2) Act 2023 section 8 from 1 April 2023 for companies and 6 April 2023 for unincorporated businesses, which ended years of uncertainty about a reversion to £200,000.
