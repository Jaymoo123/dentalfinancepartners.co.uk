# Property Tax Partners: research record, 2026-08-16

Everything here was pulled fresh today. Methods are stated so any figure can be
re-derived. Where I got something wrong during the session, the correction is
recorded next to it rather than quietly fixed.

**The short version:** the site is growing hard, the technical foundation is
clean, and every on-page lever I could measure turned out not to work. The head

demonstrably working and should be the programme.

## 1. Channel truth

Aligned window 2026-05-17 to 2026-08-13. Cross-checked two ways: GSC date
dimension 202,842 impressions against page-dimension sum 209,733, a 3.4% gap
which is expected multi-page attribution inflation.

| Engine | Impressions | Clicks | CTR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Google | 199,173 | 1,655 | 0.83% |
| **Bing** | **167,983** | **4,280** | **2.55%** |

**Bing is 67% of search clicks.** Growth 28d over prior 28d: **Google +76.1%,
Bing +52.7%.** The previous session reported this as "decayed to +1.7% per
month". That was wrong.

CTR by position, same pages, both engines:

| Position | **Bing** | Google |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1-3 | **18.18%** | n/a |
| 3-5 | **4.98%** | 1.06% |
| 5-10 | **1.86%** | 0.86% |
| 10-20 | n/a | 0.82% |

On Bing position pays. On Google it does not: CTR is flat from position 3 to 20.
Measure everything on Bing.

Bing has no AI-performance API (`GetAIPerformance` → 404). `GetPageStats` covers
60.2% of true impressions (matches the known 0.628 truncation), so every Bing
page-level figure is a floor.

## 2. Every on-page hypothesis, tested and falsified

Spearman against position, 760 posts, fresh data, both engines. Negative is good.

| Hypothesis | Google | Bing | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Inbound editorial links | -0.063 | -0.052 | **Dead** (14x spread in data, so a real null) |
| Word count | -0.151 | +0.133 | Under-powered (only 1.9x spread) **and winners are shorter** |
| H2 count | -0.019 | -0.057 | Dead |
| Tables | +0.047 | -0.236 | Weak, confounded |
| Title covers query terms | - | +0.044 | Dead |
| Wrong page type | - | - | **Dead: we have the same type** |
| Wrong H1 | - | - | **Dead: we have the same H1** |
| Competition density | +0.167 | - | **Dead: we sit at position 68 even where no competitor holds top-10** |

### The natural experiment that settles it

| | uklandlordtax | us |
|---|---|---|
| BTL mortgage calculator H1 | "Buy to Let Mortgage Calculator" | "Buy-to-Let Mortgage Calculator" |
| Body words | ~850 | 783 |
| Calculator inputs | 4 | 10 |
| Internal links | 45+ | 21 |
| Keywords absorbed | **75** | few |
| **Position** | **1** | **85** |

Their SDLT calculator absorbs **92 keywords at position 2** on ~1,200 words with
**zero worked examples and no FAQ**. Ours is 1,463 words and ranks nowhere. They
have **394 URLs to our 817**.

**Conclusion: the binding constraint is not on-page, and I could not identify it
from data we hold.** What remains is domain authority and off-page signal, for
which we have no instrument. Consistent with the existing finding that estate
link equity is near zero.

**This means the head-asset strategy's own definition of an upgrade (~3,500
words, worked examples, FAQ) describes something the winning pages do not do.**
Building to that standard moves away from what ranks. Resolve before writing.

### Intent fragmentation, which is real and self-inflicted

`/calculators/stamp-duty-calculator` is indexed (GSC inspection PASS, crawled
2026-07-04), canonical correct, 1,463 server-rendered words, in the sitemap, and
**serves zero queries in 90 days**.

Meanwhile **28 other pages of ours serve stamp duty / SDLT queries**, pulling
3,636 impressions across 298 distinct queries, with **up to 4 of our own pages
competing on the same query**. Our first-time-buyer calculator absorbs 92 queries
at position 81 for **0 clicks**.

We did not fail to build the asset. We built 28 things that crowd it out.

### Live defects

- **1 redirect 301s into a 404**: `middleware.ts:491`,
  `property-accounting-software-uk` → `portfolio-management` but the post's
  category is `Making Tax Digital (MTD)`.
- **`/embed` is in the sitemap while `embed/page.tsx:14` sets `noindex`.**
- **All 6 `/resources` guides canonical to the homepage**, not themselves. This
  blocks publishing them: removing noindex without fixing this ships six pages
  that hand every signal to `/`.
- **4 pages emit duplicate `BreadcrumbList`**, root cause
  `components/ui/Breadcrumb.tsx:15-22` self-injecting on top of hand-built blocks.
- **D2**: `CTA_BY_CATEGORY`, `getRelatedPosts`, `isMTDPost` match the raw category
  label, so 57 posts get a generic CTA and 7 lose `<MTDCountdown/>`.
- **143 legacy blog URLs** pull 11,058 impressions with no backing post.

### Deployability: the emergency is not live

The 19.07 MB ceiling applies to a single response body, not the corpus. Largest
actual artefact is `/blog` at **0.81 MB, 23x under**. The structural fix already
shipped (`blog/page.tsx` projects to metadata instead of spreading
`contentHtml`). **A big build is technically safe.** The live risk is that **17
routes call `getAllPosts()`** and any one spreading full post objects into a
client payload re-creates the failure.

### ResourceGate

Already deleted this morning (`5c156c51`, `a59a2064`), functionally off since
2026-06-16 (`f90f6cca`). **It never gated `/resources`** — it gated downloads on
calculator and blog pages. The six guides are 2,269-3,144 words, raw HTML, no
tables, already prerendered.

**The trap:** all six topics duplicate existing content. Four have pillar routes
and every one has a 21-39 post blog cluster with a near-exact "complete guide"
match. Only **stamp-duty** and **capital-gains** lack a pillar route, so those two
are the genuine gap.

## 5. Corrections made during this session

Recorded so nobody re-derives the wrong version.

1. **"5 broken city redirects pulling 2,512 impressions into 404s"** — wrong. They
   live in `BLOG_TO_LOCATION` (`middleware.ts:8-14`), spread into
   `DUPLICATE_REDIRECTS` at line 503. My parser read only literal key/value pairs
   and skipped the spread. All five resolve correctly. **Verified count is 1.**
2. **"Move 67 Bing pages from position 5-10 into 3-5, worth +2,227 clicks"** —
   wrong twice. No mechanism (I had already falsified every position lever), and
   the 2.9x compared different pages in different bands, not the same page moving.
   The honest residual is 10 pages more than 2 sigma below their own position's
   CTR, worth ~400 clicks/90d, fixable by title and description only.
3. **"77% of the pool is sub-100/mo noise"** — wrong, they are NULL-volume (§4).
4. **Ranking the build by `competitor_urls_seen.our_coverage`** — caught before
   use. That metric never exceeds 0.36 in any lane including ones we own, so it
   flags everything as a gap.
5. **`competitor_rank_score == intersection_count` in every row** (claimed by a
   previous reviewer) — false. True in 20 of 8,430 rows.

**Pattern:** every error in this programme, mine and the previous session's, came
from hand-rolling a one-off parser over source code and trusting the output.
Every figure taken from the GSC and Bing APIs survived cross-checking. **Decide
from the APIs; where a fact must come from code, read the built artefact.**

## 6. Recommendation

1. **Do not commission 33 head assets.** The BTL mortgage calculator is a natural
   experiment that already ran: right shape, right title, position 85. Upgrade
   the **three calculators already chosen** (stamp-duty, rental-yield,
   capital-allowances) and measure on Bing against the 16 untouched as control.
   Three pages decides whether the other 30 are worth building.
2. **Run the long tail, because it demonstrably works.** Median position 8.6,
   Google +76%, Bing +53%, and competitors hold near-zero top-10 blog placements
   above 3,000/mo. That territory is ours. 24 clear topics now, 86 after triage,
   up to 4,191 more once priced.
3. **Ship Wave 11.** 19 finished posts and 3 edits, uncommitted. It also closes 2
   of the 14 head-map builds.
4. **Fix the six defects in §3.** All small, all independent of strategy.
5. **Fix the SDLT fragmentation.** 28 pages splitting 298 queries while the
   calculator serves zero. Self-inflicted, measured, and worth correcting whatever
   the calculator test says.
6. **Resolve the contradiction in the head-asset strategy** before anyone writes:
   its ~3,500-word teardown standard is not what the winning pages do.
