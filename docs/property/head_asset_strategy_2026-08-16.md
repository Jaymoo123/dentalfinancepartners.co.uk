### The winning hub

- The ranking asset is almost never the hub index: it is a flagship "complete guide" of 1,800-3,000+ words with direct-answer opening, anchor TOC, worked examples, visible "Last updated [month year]", FAQ block, 10-15 internal links.
- Best-in-class shape (Provestor): one chaptered pillar (overview + 6-8 chapter children, each owning a sub-query, prev/next nav, embedded calculator) PLUS a separate granular help layer of 30-60 single-job pages in a two-level taxonomy. 5-10 heavyweight spokes per hub, never 30 thin ones.
- Beat vectors nobody in the set combines: full schema on Provestor-grade structure + named-author E-E-A-T (only Which? has it) + FA2026/Apr-2027-current numbers + calculators embedded in-pillar.
- Failure modes to avoid: flat child URLs outside the hub path, template breadcrumbs, bare card-grid indexes (uklandlordtax).

### Freshness discipline

Visible dual dates (NRLA: "first published / last updated"), tax-year-pegged refreshes (Which? updates on 6 April), and rate-change proof points in body copy. Competitors are 1-2 years stale on flagships (Landlord Studio 2025-26 title, Taxfix defaulting 2024/25, GoSimpleTax no year at all, a live MDR zombie tool). In a niche where rates changed 4 times in 24 months, the date is a ranking asset. We hold the Apr 2027 ground truth (22%/42%/47% property rates, 22% reducer, WDA 14%, 40% FYA, BADR 18%, £2.5m BR/APR cap): stating it visibly out-dates every stale competitor.

## 4. GEO/AI-answer playbook (serpAi stream)

**Bing IS the AI channel.** ChatGPT live retrieval is Bing-powered (~4 sources per answer, cites ~15% of retrieved candidates); Bing top-10 is effectively the Copilot citation gate. We are 97% Bing-indexed with 2.1x Google clicks and ChatGPT already out-converts Google. Compound this: IndexNow every publish, treat Bing rank as the primary KPI on head terms, monitor the Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance report. Google's AIO finance pool is the slow game: only 11.3% of finance AIO citations come from organic top-10, 65.7% from outside the top 100, and the trusted pool barely admits newcomers.

One page template for every explainer head:

| Lever | Rule | Lift (cited studies) |
|---|---|---|
| Front-load | Answer in the first 30% of the page | 44.2% of all LLM citations come from there |
| Length | 2,500-3,500 words, 7-15 H2s | 1.6x citations at 2,500+; peak at 7-15 H2s |
| Tables | One extractable rates/threshold table per page | Tables beat prose for rates; most-extracted block |
| FAQ block | Q&A format, FAQPage schema | Copilot's preferred shape; schema pages get 2.3x AIO citations |
| Statistics | Proprietary numbers + >=2 inline named sources (HMRC, OBR, ONS) | +40% visibility, the single strongest lever; inline sources 2.1x |
| Comparisons | Side-by-side pages for every landlord fork (own vs ltd, FHL then vs now, 2026/27 vs 2027/28) | 95% ChatGPT citation rate, the highest of any format |
| Calculator pairing | Every calculator gets an adjacent citable methodology/rates page | AIs cannot cite an interactive tool; the sidecar catches the citation |
| Stability | Tax-year titles for CTR, but no churn rewrites for citation's sake | Median cited page is 14 months old; authority accrues to stable URLs |

Off-site, faceless-compatible: 84% of AI citations correlate with earned media, which is exactly what the data-asset program in §5 manufactures. Priority order by opportunity x intent (per the stream): MTD (urgent) → non-resident landlord + HMO licensing (softest SERPs) → the two ltd-co comparison pages (lead value) → holiday-let post-abolition → calculators (the AIO-protected traffic, 7-11% AIO coverage) → section 24 / landlord-tax hubs → IHT/SDLT long-tail only.
