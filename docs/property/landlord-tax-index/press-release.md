**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## 4,840 new buy-to-let companies registered in June 2026, down 20.1% on a year earlier, new index shows

4,840 new UK buy-to-let companies were incorporated in June 2026, down 20.1%
on the same month a year earlier, according to the UK SPV Incorporation Index,
a monthly count of the Companies House register published by Property Tax
Partners.

The Index counts companies incorporated under four real-estate SIC codes, with
the headline series tracking SIC 68209, "other letting and operating of own or
leased real estate", the code the great majority of buy-to-let special purpose
vehicles register under. Across all four codes, 6,614 property companies were
incorporated in the month, and 90,390 over the trailing twelve months.

Unlike quarterly market estimates, the Index is a direct count of a published
register, refreshed monthly, and every figure can be reproduced from the
Companies House Advanced Search API using the codes and date filters set out in
the published methodology.

### Regional picture

London accounted for 35.3% of new property company incorporations over the
last twelve months, ahead of the North West on 11.3% and the South East on
8.5%. The
regional series is derived from registered-office postcodes in the Companies
House monthly bulk snapshot and is published as shares rather than exact counts,
because postcode areas do not align perfectly with regional boundaries.

### Net formation, not just new registrations

The Index also publishes the net rate, which subtracts dissolutions of companies
in the same SIC set from new incorporations. In June 2026, 6,614 property
companies were incorporated and 3,139 were dissolved, a net addition of 3,475
to the property company population. Headline incorporation figures, here and
elsewhere, are gross and do not net off closures.

Over the trailing twelve months, net formation ran at +44,768: 90,390
incorporations against 45,622 dissolutions.

## Notes to editors

**What the Index measures.** Companies incorporated at Companies House whose
recorded SIC codes fall in a fixed set of four: 68209 (other letting and
operating of own or leased real estate), 68100 (buying and selling of own real
estate), 68320 (management of real estate on a fee or contract basis) and 68201
(renting and operating of Housing Association real estate). The headline series
is 68209 alone. The "all property companies" series is a deduplicated union of
all four, taken from a single combined-SIC query, because a company may hold more
than one code and summing the individual series double-counts. The per-code
series therefore do not add up to the union, by design.

**Gross and net.** Monthly incorporation counts are gross: companies later
dissolved remain counted in the month they were incorporated, so the series
carries no survivorship distortion. Dissolutions are counted separately, by
dissolution date, to produce the net formation rate.

**Provisional window.** The two most recent months are provisional and are
excluded from all headline figures, because Companies House indexes recent
incorporations with a short lag.

**Regional data.** Derived from the Companies House Basic Company Data monthly
bulk snapshot, mapped from registered-office postcode area to the 12 ITL1
regions, with an "Other / unknown" bucket. The bulk file contains only live
companies, so regional counts run below the gross national series (the measured
ratio is published, currently 99.9% for the most recent settled month and
85.9% across the full 36-month window) and the series is
restricted to the last 36 months. Regional figures are best read as shares.

**Known limits.** SIC codes are self-reported at incorporation and are rarely
revised. Some property vehicles register under other codes and are not captured.
LLPs rarely file SIC codes and are effectively excluded. SIC 68201 is a social
housing code, published for completeness but not part of the private landlord
picture. The Index counts companies, not properties.

**How this differs from other published figures.** Quarterly estimates from
other analysts, including Hamptons, apply their own definition of a buy-to-let
company. The Index applies a fixed, published SIC filter to the register itself,
monthly, and adds a net formation rate and a regional split. The series are
complementary rather than competing, and both methods are published.
