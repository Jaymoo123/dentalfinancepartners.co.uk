# Track A, factual QA: supported-accommodation-registration-and-tax

Site: care. Reviewed 2026-09-27. Verdict: **PASS** (1 edit).

## Load-bearing claim

"Ofsted registration makes a provider state-regulated for the VAT welfare exemption." House positions are HP-numbered and silent on supported accommodation and Ofsted (HP1 covers CQC only), so this went to primary source.

**CONFIRMED.** VAT Notice 701/2 (gov.uk, welfare services and goods) lists the state-regulated bodies verbatim as: "Care Quality Commission, Scottish Commission for the Regulation of Care (The Care Commission), Care and Social Services Inspectorate Wales, Northern Ireland Regulation and Quality Improvement Authority, Office for Standards in Education (OFSTED), any other similar regulatory body". The same notice states: "A welfare provider becomes state regulated when the relevant regulatory body approves its application to register. The provider is not state regulated whilst its application is under consideration and so it may not exempt its supplies of welfare services during that period." Both the naming of Ofsted and the post's approval-not-application timing point are correct.
