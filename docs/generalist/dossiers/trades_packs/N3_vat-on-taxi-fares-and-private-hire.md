## 5. Whitespace (§18.4 — fresh Supreme Court position, nobody covers it correctly)

- **The full date-tagged litigation line, which no fetched competitor carries at all:** fares
  standard-rated; a driver as principal rarely breaches £90,000 so most drivers charge no VAT;
  an operator as principal charges 20% on the full fare; Uber Britannia v Sefton [2023] EWHC 1975
  (KB) said operators outside London contract as principal, **reversed by the Court of Appeal
  July 2024 (Delta Merseyside and Veezu), reversal upheld by the Supreme Court 29 July 2025**:
  outside London an operator is NOT obliged to contract as principal; London operators under the
  TfL regime are. This position is weeks-fresh in SERP terms and completely uncovered.
- The November 2025 consultation response: PHV operators excluded from TOMS from 2 January 2026
  (hedged, see fences).
- The plain consumer answer done properly: "do taxis charge VAT" answered in sentence one with
  the threshold figure the incumbent omits.
