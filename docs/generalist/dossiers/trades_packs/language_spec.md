# Language spec — trades_transport cluster (one spec, all 9 packs inherit it)

Written 2026-08-25 per REWRITE_PROGRAM.md §9.11, from the ranking specialist pages fetched for
the pack teardowns. Sample (rank-weighted winners, not brands): gondal `accountants-for-drivers`
(p1 on `accountant for taxi drivers` AND `accountants for uber drivers`, ~5,500w), livingstones
`self-assessment-for-taxi-drivers` (p7-10 across the whole T-SA set, ~3,200w), livingstones
`taxi-driver-allowable-expenses` (p11, ~2,200w), livingstones `vat-for-taxi-drivers` (p16-24,
~1,700w), clarkwell `accountants-for-transport-and-couriers-uk` (p37-49, ~2,100w). No paid calls;
all from teardown fetches.

## 1. Measured answer patterns (what the winners do)

- **Length is not the lever.** p1 is ~5,500 words but p7-10 is 3,200 and p11 is 2,200; clarkwell's
  2,100-word page sits p37-49. What separates gondal (p1) from the rest is CURRENT figures embedded
  in second-person sentences plus a 15-18 question FAQ, not bulk.
- **Ruling sentence first, always with the audience in it.** The p7-10 opener: "If you drive a taxi
  or private hire vehicle in the UK, you almost certainly need to file a Self-Assessment return."
  Copy that shape: the reader's situation, then the obligation, then the number.
- **Numbers live mid-sentence** ("no tax on the first £12,570 of total income", "exceeds £90,000
  in any rolling 12-month period"). The one ranking page that omits its own core number
  (livingstones VAT: never states the threshold) is the cluster's weakest performer per word.
- **Nobody in the entire SERP set carries a worked example.** Zero worked calculations on all five
  fetched pages (gondal's best is a one-line MTD illustration). A recomputable 2026/27 example per
  page is the single biggest structural gap we can take.
- **Question-form H2s win on explainer pages** (gondal's FAQ blocks; livingstones' "Do Taxi
  Drivers Have to File..."); noun-phrase H2s on service pages. Our N3 and all FAQ blocks use
  question H2s; N1/N4/N7/N8 service pages mix.
- **Second person throughout** ("you drive, you pay, your profit"), short declarative sentences,
  fork-naming instead of mush ("you must pick one method and stick with it for that vehicle" —
  livingstones states the stick rule plainly; keep that directness, at the correct rates).

## 2. House voice constraints (binding)

- Plain cost-conscious British English, consumer register, reader in the sentence. These are the
  least tax-literate audiences on the site (dossier §7): shortest sentences of any cluster to date,
  every figure in pounds, no unexplained acronym (spell out MTD, AMAP, PHV, ADI, SIA at first use).
- **No em-dashes** anywhere in copy (every fetched winner uses them; we do not).
- **No "this guide"/"in this article" intros**, no "unique financial challenges" filler
  (clarkwell's opener is the specimen to avoid). H1 poses the real question or names the real
  service; sentence one answers or asserts.
- **No AI tells:** no rule-of-three stacks, no "it's important to note", no mirrored "not only...
  but also", no summarising sections.
- **Worked examples with recomputable figures**, 2026/27 rates, house_positions.md sections cited
  by number (§2, §7, §8, §9, §12, §13, §17, §18). Every rate date-tagged.
- Hedges are house-locked wordings, not vibes: the §18.1 "reported does not mean newly taxable"
  pair, the §18.2 worker-vs-tax fence, the §18.4 PHV VAT date-tagged forks, §18.5 employed-only
  flat rates, §18.6 per-vehicle stick rule. Use them verbatim in substance.

## 3. Do-not-copy list (measured on the same sample)

- livingstones expenses page: **45p/25p AMAP stated as current after 6 April 2026** (55p/25p now,
  §12); reproduce the stick-rule directness, never the rates.
- livingstones SA page: **Class 2 quoted at £3.45/week as a live payment** (Class 2 treated as
  paid since 6 Apr 2024, §2); firm-voice "How Livingstones Helps" mid-article sell sections.
- livingstones VAT page: names no threshold figure, no agent/principal fork, no Sefton/Supreme
  Court position; the whole §18.4 area is our whitespace, not a model.
- gondal: em-dashes throughout, zero worked examples, 5 nav-ish trailing H2s; the figures and FAQ
  breadth are the model, the punctuation and sell-tail are not.
- clarkwell: platitude opener ("unique financial challenges"), zero figures anywhere (staleness-
  proof but content-free), "keep more of what you earn" framing.
- lanop: "Maximise Your Earnings, Minimise Your Stress" H1 sloganeering; uniform-tax-rebate
  page model (rebate-agent adjacent, excluded EX-OFFNICHE, never imitate).

## 4. Differentiation note — lead structures (assigned, so 9 parallel pages do not converge)

One lead structure per pack; the opening 40% of each page must follow it, and no two siblings may
share an H2 phrasing. Additional bans, all used by today's creative wave and off-limits here:
**no "older articles" openers; no persona names Maya, Amara, Jess, Daniel, Sophie or Priya
(pick different names for worked examples); no Manchester, Nottingham, Sheffield, Leeds or
Bristol as example cities.**
