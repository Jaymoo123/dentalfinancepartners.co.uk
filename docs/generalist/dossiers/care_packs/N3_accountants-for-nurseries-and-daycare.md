## 5. Whitespace (what §19.3 lets us own)

- **Funded hours are taxable trading income, stated plainly.** The 15 and 30 funded childcare hours
  paid by the local authority, and receipts arriving via Tax-Free Childcare, are ordinary taxable
  trading income of the provider, exactly like parent-paid fees. Government-routed payment does not
  change its character. This is the single most common misunderstanding in the sector and not one
  ranking page addresses it.
- **The VAT exemption explained with its consequence.** Childcare by a state-regulated
  (Ofsted-registered or devolved-equivalent) provider is exempt as a welfare service. Exempt means
  no output VAT **and no input-VAT recovery** on related costs. That makes the VAT on a new
  building, a refurbishment or a minibus a real cost, not a reclaim. wearegolding names the
  exemption and stops. We explain the bill.
- **Partial exemption, flagged rather than computed.** A provider with mixed taxable supplies (items
  sold to parents, room hire) is into partial exemption. Say so, describe what it means, and route
  it to advice. Do not attempt a partial-exemption calculation on a public page.
- **Employer cost done as arithmetic.** Staff payroll, employer NIC and auto-enrolment as a loaded
  cost per employee, with a worked figure. In a sector where staff cost is the dominant line and
  margins are set by funding rates, this is the most useful thing on the page.
- **The occupancy and funding-rate margin frame** built from the reader's own numbers rather than
  asserted as a "unique challenge".
