## 5. Whitespace

- **One car followed end to end**, which nobody does: taken in part-exchange (at what value, and
  what that does to the margin on both vehicles), possibly used as a demonstrator (which changes its
  character), then retailed (margin scheme or not). The single-vehicle journey makes four separate
  technical points readable in sequence.
- **Part-exchange done correctly**: the part-exchange allowance is not a discount, both legs are
  separate transactions for VAT, and over-allowing on a part-exchange quietly destroys the margin on
  the vehicle sold.
- **Demonstrators**, the genuinely difficult question: a demonstrator is stock that has been put to
  use, and its treatment (stock versus fixed asset, the capital-allowance position given cars are
  excluded from AIA and the first-year allowances, private use and the benefit-in-kind consequence
  if a director or employee drives it) has no accountancy page addressing it in this field.
- **Stocking finance accounted for properly**: the facility, the interest, and the fact that the
  vehicle remains the dealer's stock, presented as bookkeeping and tax, never as a product view.
- **The garage and MOT side**: a workshop is a services business with different VAT treatment from
  the vehicle sales side, which is why so many dealer accounts are wrong.
- A recomputable 2026/27 worked example following the one vehicle through with figures.

## 6. Fences (binding)

- **§21.2 motor-finance fence, in full.** Permitted: margin-scheme VAT, **stock finance accounting**,
  dealer tax mechanics. **Barred: motor-finance commission or mis-selling claims content, any
  eligibility-check or start-a-claim invitation, any referral of a claimant, any link whose purpose
  is claims traffic.** RAO art 89G; FSMA s.19 and s.23. Ceiling: one neutral factual sentence with a
  gov.uk or FCA link; omitting it is the safer default. Line-by-line adversarial QA.
- **Stocking finance is treated as accounting only.** No comparison of stocking-finance providers,
  no commentary on rates or terms, no view on whether a dealer should use one, and **no mention of
  commission arrangements between finance providers and dealers in any form**. That last point is
  the exact ground the fence exists to keep the page off. Describe how the facility appears in the
  accounts and what the interest does to taxable profit, and stop.
- **No consumer-facing vehicle content.** EX-CONSUMER-CAR is excluded intent: no car values, no
  depreciation-for-buyers content, no "is a demo car a good buy". The reader is a dealer principal.
- **Assignment split:** the hire framing is N8's; the margin-scheme arithmetic is N9's (reference the
  result, never re-run the calculation); the company-car and P11D core pages own general benefit-in-
  kind mechanics and this page states the demonstrator-specific point and links out. See §9.
- **No AMAP or mileage depth.** §12's 55p/25p from 6 April 2026 is stated once and date-tagged if
  the page needs it at all, and the existing mileage content owns the rest.
- No house-position citations in reader copy (report only): cite VAT Notice 718/1, CAA 2001 and the
  gov.uk pages instead. No em-dashes. Rates date-tagged; 2025/26 bands and Class 4 carry the "still
  current when checked August 2026" hedge.
