## 5. Whitespace

- **The agreement's money clauses as the spine**, which nobody uses: initial fee, ongoing royalty,
  marketing or brand fund contribution, term and renewal, and the tax consequence attached clause by
  clause. It matches how a franchisee actually reads their own contract.
- **The initial fee answered firmly and sourced.** It is **capital**, and BIM57620 says so. The best
  page in the niche hedges into "may need to be capitalised"; the specialist says nothing. Stating
  it plainly, with the apportionment point for a contractually identified training element, is the
  page's core differentiator. Depth goes to N13.
- **The company-versus-sole-trader asymmetry**, hedged per §21.5 and open question 3: a company may
  get relief through the intangibles regime; a sole trader gets no amortisation route and waits for
  CGT base cost on disposal. Nobody in the field mentions it. Depth goes to N14 if N14 ships.
- **VAT on royalties**, which livingstones lists as a consideration and never answers.
- A recomputable 2026/27 worked example: one franchisee's first two years, showing the initial fee
  outside the profit computation and the royalties inside it.

## 6. Fences (binding)

- **Reverse-intent screen, enforced in metaTitle, H1 and sentence one.** The page serves franchisees.
  **It never reads as, and never links to, an offer to buy an accountancy franchise.**
- **The initial fee is capital. Never write that it is deductible against income**, and never
  reproduce livingstones' vague "may need to be capitalised and amortised" formulation, which
  conflates the sole-trader and company positions.
- **The company intangibles route stays hedged** (facts and accounting treatment govern) until §21
  open question 3 is CIRD-verified. This hedge is a house lock, not a stylistic choice.
- **No franchise selection or brand-comparison content**, no franchise-opportunity listings, no
  "best franchises to buy". `how to fund a franchise` is answered as tax and structure only (what
  the funding is spent on and how each element is treated), never as finance-product advice or a
  lender recommendation.
- **The 4% to 12% royalty range, if used at all, is attributed as a market observation from
  published commentary and never presented as a rule or a benchmark we assert.**
- **Assignment split:** fee mechanics depth is N13's; structure and VAT depth is N14's if N14 ships,
  and folds in here if it does not (dossier §6). One paragraph plus a link each.
- No house-position citations in reader copy (report only): cite **BIM57620**, BIM57600+ and
  CTA 2009 Part 8 instead. No em-dashes (livingstones uses them frequently; we do not). No
  "Introduction" H2. No "unique accounting complexities". No "Maximise Profits & Minimise Tax"
  sloganeering. Rates date-tagged; 2025/26 bands and Class 4 carry the "still current when checked
  August 2026" hedge.
