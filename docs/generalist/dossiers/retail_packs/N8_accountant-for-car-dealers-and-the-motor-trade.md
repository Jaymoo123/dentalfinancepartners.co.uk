## 6. Fences (binding — C1 CONDITIONAL row 70, §21.2 locked wording rule)

**Permitted on this page:** margin-scheme VAT, stock finance accounting, dealer tax mechanics,
ordinary accountancy service framing.

**Barred, absolutely:**

- **Motor-finance commission or mis-selling claims content of any kind**, including any
  discretionary-commission arrangement explainer or any successor redress scheme explainer.
- **Any invitation to check eligibility or start a claim.**
- **Any referral of a claimant** to a claims management company or a law firm.
- **Any link whose purpose is claims traffic.**

Managing such claims is **regulated claims management activity in the financial services sector
(RAO art 89G; FSMA s.19 and s.23, where unauthorised carrying-on is a criminal offence)** and the
estate holds no such permission. **The ceiling is one neutral factual sentence that redress
litigation exists, with a gov.uk or FCA link.** That sentence is optional; omitting it entirely is
also acceptable and is the safer default. Adversarial QA checks this page against the barred list
line by line.

Other fences:

- **No client-recovery headline.** themotortradeaccountants leads with "How we helped a client
  reclaim £20,000"; the estate publishes anonymised social proof only and never a monetised saving
  as a heading.
- **Assignment split:** the margin-scheme arithmetic is N9's (one summary paragraph plus a link
  here, never a worked calculation). Stocking finance, demonstrators, part-exchange and the
  garage/MOT side are N10's. Jewellers' use of the same scheme is N11's.
- **No consumer content.** EX-CONSUMER-CAR (12 keywords, 2,480/mo) is an excluded intent: car
  values, depreciation for buyers, what a car is worth. The reader is a dealer principal.
- No house-position citations in reader copy (report only): cite VAT Notice 718/1 and the gov.uk
  margin scheme page instead. No em-dashes. Rates date-tagged; 2025/26 bands and Class 4 carry the
  "still current when checked August 2026" hedge.
