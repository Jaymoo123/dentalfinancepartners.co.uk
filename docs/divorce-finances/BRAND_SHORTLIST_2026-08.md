# Divorce-finances brand shortlist

For the owner G1 decision. Created 2026-08-03 because no shortlist existed for this site
(wills-probate had one from 2026-07-24; this is the sibling).

Same naming constraints as the wills shortlist, plus one extra:

- Avoid "Law", "Legal", "Solicitors" — implies a regulated legal practice, which this faceless
  content brand is not.
- Avoid "Advisers", "Wealth", "Financial Planning" — implies FCA-regulated financial advice.
  This matters more here than on wills: the site's lead-regulatory position is that pensions
  content is **information only**, so the brand must not imply advice.
- Sibling reference formula: "Trade Tax Specialists" (construction-cis), descriptive category
  plus "Specialists", no personal names, no regulated title.

## Recommended pick

**Divorce Finance Specialists** (divorcefinancespecialists.co.uk)

Direct mirror of the proven "Trade Tax Specialists" formula, covers the whole content scope
(settlements, pension sharing, CGT, child maintenance) rather than boxing the site into
settlements only, no regulated-title implication, and no proximity to a named incumbent
(Stowe, amicable, The Divorce Surgery).

Runner-up: **Divorce Settlement Specialists** if the owner wants the highest-intent term
foregrounded. It reads narrower than the content actually is.

Avoid: **Divorce Finance Partners**. "Partners" reads as a firm of advisers rather than a
publisher, which is the implication the regulatory position asks us to avoid, and it collides
in shape with the estate's own Property Tax Partners / Dental Finance Partners branding for a
site that is deliberately a different kind of asset.
