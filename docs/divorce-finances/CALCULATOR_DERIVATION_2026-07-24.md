# Divorce-finances calculator fleet derivation, 2026-07-24

Derived from the niche screener research export (`optimisation_engine/niche_screener/out/divorce-finances_research_export/`): 636-query UK universe, 300 volumed, 50 fetched SERPs, domain classes. Selection rules applied strictly per tool-answerable family:

- R1: explicit tool-frame UK query volume > 0
- R2: family volume >= 1,000/mo AND decision/computation-shaped AND SERP top-10 prose-only (no interactive tool ranking)
- R3: screener-validated regulatory shock justifies the tool (rule_churn component scored 23 spikes, normalised 1.0; HMCTS fee uplift 13 July 2026: divorce application £612 to £628, consent order £60 to £62)

Volumes are Keyword Planner values; near-duplicate phrasings share a rounded figure, so family totals below use the deduplicated head figure plus distinct variants, not the raw sum of clones.

## Final fleet (5 tools)

1. Divorce cost calculator UK (R1, R2, R3). Route-based: DIY vs fixed-fee vs contested, with the 13 July 2026 fee uplift built in and dated.
2. Help with Fees (EX160) remission checker (R3, rides the cost family). Module within tool 1 plus its own route.
3. Consent order and clean break order cost calculator (R1, R3).
4. Financial settlement range estimator (R1). Range output, stated s.25 assumptions, indicative-only framing.
5. Mediation vs solicitor cost comparison (R1). Includes Family Mediation Voucher.

Information-only by design (no calculator): spousal maintenance, pension sharing and offsetting (compliance flag), child maintenance (gov.uk official tool owns it), legal aid. Watchlist for a later add if UK volume evidence appears: house buyout / transfer of equity calculator.

Data note: the raw calculator_demand screener component (22,100) is dominated by cost-family keyword clones; the fleet above is derived from deduplicated family volumes and per-query tool-frame evidence, not that headline figure.
