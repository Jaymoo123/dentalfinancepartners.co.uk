## Edit log

1. **Body, "What if private or locum work starts when you get back?"** — "so it is normally taxed at
   the higher rate plus Class 4 National Insurance at 2%" replaced with "so it is taxed at your
   marginal rate, with Class 4 National Insurance at 2% above £50,270". Reason: the original asserts
   the higher rate and the 2% Class 4 rate as the normal outcome without the threshold that makes
   either true. Class 4 is 6% between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% only above £50,270 (§8), and a
   returning doctor below the higher-rate threshold meets neither. WRONG.
2. **Body, "Is your overseas income taxed here in the year of return?"** — removed the sentence
   "Earnings for duties performed abroad before the split date stay outside the UK charge for that
   year." Reason: word band only. The same rule is already stated in the opening paragraph and in
   FAQ 3, so the section loses no content. This took the body from 1,212 to 1,196 words.

- FAQ 1 says you are resident "if you meet an automatic UK test", without the prior step that the
  automatic overseas tests are tested first. It cannot produce a wrong answer here (the third
  automatic overseas test caps UK presence below 91 days, so it cannot collide with the 183-day
  test) and the body states the order correctly, so it was left alone.
- The post's split-year priority sentence is sourced to FA 2013 Sch 45 **para 55**, not para 54.
  Para 54 governs Cases 1 to 3 (departure). Recording this because a reader checking the citation
