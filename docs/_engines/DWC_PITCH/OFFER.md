# Double Wired Creative: the accounting firm retainer

A website that brings you enquiries, and the engine that keeps it doing it.

## The price

- **£1,500 a month**, 12 month minimum.
- **£2,500 one-off** to build the site.
- **Founding rate: £1,000 a month** for the first three firms, in exchange for letting us
  write up what we did for you as a case study (your name used only with your sign-off).

No setup fees beyond the build. No ad spend. No percentage of your fees.

## What you get every month

1. **Your site, on our design system.** Built once (the build fee), then maintained. Fast,
   mobile first, and designed so that a visitor who is ready to talk can do it in two clicks.
2. **Four new posts and one rewrite.** Written to questions people actually type, not to a
   content calendar. Tax facts are checked and kept current as rates change, so last year's
   page does not quietly go wrong.
3. **Lead capture and nurture.** Forms that people finish, plus an automatic email sequence
   so an enquiry that goes quiet is followed up without you remembering to do it.
4. **Lead ops.** Every enquiry arrives in your inbox and in a Google Sheet within a minute,
   deduplicated, with spam filtered out before it reaches you.
5. **A one-page monthly read.** Traffic by search engine, enquiries, the queries you are
   showing up for, what we changed, what we are doing next. One page, no dashboard logins.

## What you do not get

- No tax advice. We are designers and marketers. The advice stays yours.
- No paid ads. This offer is organic only.
- No Google Business Profile work. We will not touch it, because the wrong edit can get a
  profile suspended and that is your local presence gone.
- No promised number of leads. Anyone quoting you a monthly lead figure is guessing.

## Why 12 months

Content compounds and it compounds slowly. A post published in month one is usually at its
weakest the week it goes live and at its strongest a year later, once search engines have
seen it, other pages link to it, and it has accumulated the related queries it did not rank
for at first. Twelve months is roughly 48 posts and 12 rewrites working together. Three
months is a handful of pages that never had time to be found, and neither of us learns
anything from it.
