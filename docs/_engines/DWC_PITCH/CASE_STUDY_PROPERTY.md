## The client

Property Tax Partners, a UK specialist site answering property and landlord tax questions.
It is the flagship of a 17 site accounting and finance portfolio. We were brought in to do
design, branding and conversion, not content.

## Before

The site already had the hard part: a deep library of tax content, [NUMBER: owner to fill
from dashboard, published pages] pages of it, and traffic arriving from Google and Bing
every day.

What it did not have was a shape. Readers landed on a good answer and then met a page that
did not know what to do with them. There was no consistent design system, the chrome was
inherited rather than designed, and the moment where a reader turns into an enquiry was left
to chance.

## What we changed

Eleven working sessions, delivered as one body of work: a new design system, around 40
bespoke storytelling components, a rebuilt blog, and reworked chrome, homepage, services,
calculators and legal pages. In total the redesign touched 252 files.

The parts that matter to a business owner rather than a designer:

- **A design system, not a skin.** Every page is built from the same tokens and components,
  so a new page looks right by default and the site cannot drift back into inconsistency.
- **Calculators as the front door.** Property tax questions are arithmetic questions. The
  calculators answer them, and the moment the answer appears is the moment the reader is
  most willing to talk to someone.
- **Capture designed into the page, not bolted on.** Enquiry surfaces sit where the reading
  actually ends, and they are measured individually so we can tell which ones earn their place.
- **Nothing that interrupts the reader.** No exit-intent popups, no timed modals.

The new design went live on **23 August 2026**.

## What it produces now

- **Enquiries land in a working process, not an inbox.** Every enquiry is written to a
  Google Sheet within about a minute (new rows insert at the top of the sheet), deduplicated,
  and emailed out at the same time.
- **Nothing goes quiet by accident.** An automatic nurture sequence follows up, and a warm
  handoff email quotes the enquirer's own details back to them, including the call slot they
  booked, so the first phone call does not start with "how did you get my number".
- **Follow-up is auditable.** Every enquiry carries the exact consent wording it was shown,
  stored against the record.
- **The site tests its own ideas.** In September 2026 we launched a paid document offer under
  the premium calculator results, flag-gated so it can be switched off in under a minute
  without a deploy, with a pre-agreed kill line.
