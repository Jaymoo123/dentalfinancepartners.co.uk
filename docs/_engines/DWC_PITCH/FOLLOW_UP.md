# After the call

Three touches, then stop. Every one goes in TRACKER.md.

## Same day: recap and proposal

Within four hours of the call, while they still remember it. Short.

> Subject: <Firm> and Double Wired Creative
>
> Hello <name>,
>
> Thanks for the time. To recap what you told me: <their situation in one sentence, in their
> words> and what you want more of is <the work they named>.
>
> The proposal is attached. It is the same thing I described: the site, four posts and a
> rewrite a month, capture and nurture, lead ops, and a page a month telling you what happened.
> £1,000 a month as one of the first three firms, twelve months, £2,500 to build.
>
> You can sign it at the link. Any questions, reply here.
>
> Tanjiah

Attach: OFFER.md rendered as a PDF with their firm name on it. Nothing else. No deck, no
portfolio, no case study unless they asked.

## Day 3: one useful thing

Not a chase. Send something they can use whether or not they buy.

> Subject: The <topic> thing you mentioned
>
> Hello <name>,
>
> You mentioned <the specific problem>. Here is what I would do about it on your site:
> <two or three concrete sentences>. That holds whether or not we work together.
>
> Proposal is still with you if you want it.
>
> Tanjiah

## Day 10: close the loop

> Subject: Closing this off
>
> Hello <name>,
>
> I have not heard back, so I will assume the timing is wrong and stop writing.
>
> If it changes, the offer is the same and the founding rate holds until the three places are
> gone. Reply any time.
>
> Tanjiah

After day 10, mark the row CLOSED and stop. Reopen only if they write first.

## Close mechanics

1. **Proposal.** OFFER.md, their firm name in the title, the founding rate stated as a price
   not an offer, dated. One page. Do not add scope on the way out of the call.
2. **E-sign.** Send as an e-sign link, not a printable PDF. A document that needs printing
   loses a week.
3. **First invoice.** On signature: £2,500 build plus the first month, payable before the
   build starts. Then monthly on the same date.
4. **Case-study clause.** Founding rate only. The clause says we may write up the work, and
   the firm signs off the wording before anything is published. If they will not accept the
   clause, they pay £1,500.
5. **Kick-off.** Book it in the same email as the invoice, inside 7 days of signature.

## Never

- No discount to close. The founding rate is the discount and it is already given.
- No new scope invented to win a deal. Paid ads and Google Business Profile stay out.
- No promised launch date on the call. The date goes in the kick-off, after the content brief.
