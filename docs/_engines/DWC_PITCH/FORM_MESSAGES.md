# Contact-form messages

Sent by the owner through the firm's own website contact form. Never by email.

Rules for every send:
- Replace every `<...>` with something real. A message with a placeholder in it is worse than
  no message.
- The specific observation must be true and checkable on their site. Generic flattery reads as
  a template and gets deleted.
- One Property fact only. Never a lead count, never a promise.
- Name, fields, variant and date go into TRACKER.md the moment it is sent.
- One message, one follow-up at day 7, then stop.

---

## Variant A: the dead blog

Subject: Your <topic> page

Hello,

I read your <topic> page on <domain>. It answers the question properly, which is rare, but
the last post before it is from <year> so almost nobody will find it.

We design and run sites for accounting firms. We rebuilt a property tax site last August and
the same design system now runs nine sites.

Would 15 minutes with our designer be useful? She has worked with accounting firms.

Tanjiah, Double Wired Creative

(72 words)
