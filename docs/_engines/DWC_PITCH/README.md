# DWC sales kit

Everything needed to sell a productised "website + content engine + nurture + lead ops"
retainer to small UK accounting firms (1 to 10 staff), on behalf of Double Wired Creative
(DWC, https://www.doublewiredcreative.com/).

## What we are selling

A monthly retainer, not a project. The firm gets a site on the DWC design system, four
authoritative posts plus one rewrite a month, a lead capture and nurture sequence, lead ops
that puts every enquiry in front of them within a minute, and a one-page monthly read.
Full terms in [OFFER.md](OFFER.md).

## Why DWC can say this with a straight face

DWC designed Property Tax Partners, the flagship site in a 17 site UK accounting and finance
estate run by Ashfield Trading Ltd. The redesign went live on 23 August 2026. That design
system was then ported to 8 more sites in the estate, all live on 16 September 2026. Every
one of those sites carries "Built by Double Wired Creative" in the footer, on every page.
So the proof is public, linkable and already indexed.

## Who does what

| Role | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| Outreach | The owner | Builds the 50 firm list, writes and sends the contact-form messages, chases at day 7, logs everything in TRACKER.md |
| Calls and delivery | DWC's principal | Takes the 15 minute call, presents the offer, sends the proposal, delivers the work |

She is the face. The owner and the estate stay faceless. Nothing in the outreach or on the
call names Ashfield, the estate, or the other sites as anything other than "sites we have
built and run".

## The flow, list to close

1. Build the list (50 firms, name and website only). [TARGET_LIST_SPEC.md](TARGET_LIST_SPEC.md)
2. Send the contact-form message, one variant per firm, logged. [FORM_MESSAGES.md](FORM_MESSAGES.md)
3. Silence at day 7, send the follow-up. Same file.
4. Reply comes in, book 15 minutes with her.
5. She runs the call off one page. [CALL_SHEET.md](CALL_SHEET.md)
6. Same day recap, day 3, day 10. [FOLLOW_UP.md](FOLLOW_UP.md)
7. Proposal is OFFER.md with their name on it, e-signed, first invoice.
8. Every step lands in [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md).

Case study copy for the DWC website is in [CASE_STUDY_PROPERTY.md](CASE_STUDY_PROPERTY.md).

## Two rules that are never broken

- Never promise lead counts, on a call, in a message, or in writing.
- Never give tax advice. DWC is a design and marketing studio.
