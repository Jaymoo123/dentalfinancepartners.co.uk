## 1. Ranked opportunity lanes

### Lane A. Introducer / referral fees on traffic we already hold (build: near zero)

The highest £-per-conversion options, none need FCA permission, a qualification, or a face.
One signed introducer agreement per vertical, then route existing calculator/page traffic.

1. **Ltd-co BTL / commercial finance introducer.** Corporate BTL is not a regulated mortgage
   contract (FCA PERG 4.10B); unauthorised introducer may take the fee. Proc fee ~0.55% gross;
   introducer share ~50% = roughly £500-£950 per completed £200k case (market convention, not
   published tariff). We ALREADY have 34 Track B Tier 1 pages + 5 calculators built and
   build-green on Property (uncommitted/undeployed, 2026-07-30) and a live BTL-mortgage cluster
   ranking. Open items were always: broker principal + solicitor sign-off on CTA wording.
   This research puts a number on what that principal is worth.
2. **Probate / wills / IHT referral.** LASPO referral-fee ban is personal-injury only; SRA
   permits referral fees with disclosure; will-writing unregulated in E&W. Probate end-fee
   £3k-£10k+VAT. Wills-probate site is CONTENT COMPLETE and unmonetised. Conveyancing rides
   along at a corroborated £200-£400/case.
3. **Specialist tax referral (R&D, capital allowances).** We hold pos-1 rankings already
   (property CA 97 queries; property+generalist R&D). R&D referral models at ~£3.4k/claim
   (£85k avg claim x 20% adviser fee x 20% introducer share) but the market is shrinking
   (claims -26% TY23-24, HMRC blitz; horizon doc killed R&D as a BUILD niche and that stands).
   As pure re-route of existing traffic it is found money, not a build. CA introducer % is
   unpublished; term sheet before modelling.

### Lane B. Landlord SPV formation + registered-office annuity (build: small, recurring revenue)

The one genuinely SaaS-shaped play the data supports. The formation-agent model earns on
renewals, not formations: 1st Formations stacks registered office £39/yr + service address
£26/yr + mail £96/yr + confirmation statement (~£26 margin) + compliance bundle £149.99/yr
into roughly £311/yr recurring per company; Rapid sells the formation at £2.99 to buy that
annuity; Tide pays £50 per referred bank account. GetGround (QED-backed, $25m+) charges
£48-£198/mo for the landlord-specific version; Provestor £29.99/mo. Our property-tax traffic
IS the incorporation-intent audience (incorporation, SDLT, section 24 pages + calculators).
Formation + address services need no accountancy qualification. Faceless is normal in this
category.

**Sizing (researched 2026-09-01, sources in session):**
- Flow: 66,587 new BTL companies formed 2025 (Hamptons, record, +8% YoY); Jan 2026 +11% YoY
  implies ~73k for 2026. Stock: 443,272 BTL companies holding 755k+ titles. ~75% of new BTL
  purchases via company; 43% of mortgaged BTL completions 2025 (Paragon).
- Premium ceiling: GetGround Launch Transact £696 setup + Core from £48/mo (single property
  ~£1,270 year one); Provestor top tier £89/mo. Floor: 1st Formations All Inclusive £139.99/yr.
  Above ~£300 sells only on tax/legal substance (SPV articles/SIC, declarations of trust,
  s162/SDLT structuring, CT600+SA, MTD), not on the formation.
- Competitive gap: NO specialist owns the SPV-formation SERP head terms; mortgage brokers and
  accountant content hold them. GetGround = only scale brand (~30k investors, ~8% of annual
  flow, $25-31m raised) and it BUYS distribution (BuyAssociation acquisition Dec 2025), i.e.
  its bottleneck is acquisition, which SEO-led entrant sidesteps.
- Ops entry cost: ~£1,300-1,600 year one (HMRC TCSP AML £300 + £400/premises/yr + £500
  fit-and-proper + ACSP £63; ACSP mandatory for third-party filing from ~Nov 2026; IDV
  mandatory since 18 Nov 2025). Only 10k ACSPs registered vs 55k forecast.
- Demand tailwind: Autumn Budget 2025 property income tax +2pp from Apr 2027 (matches our
  ground truth, reducer to 22%); s162 incorporation relief needs a positive CLAIM from
  6 Apr 2026 (advice hook for the ~1/3 of landlords planning to transfer existing stock);
  dividend +2pp Apr 2026 makes the choice advice-shaped. Hard 12-18 month window.
- Size cases: 1% of ~70k flow at £500 setup + £600/yr = ~£770k yr-1 billings, ~£420k
  recurring base yr-2 at 10-15% assumed churn; 0.3% SEO-only base case = ~£230k yr-1.
- UK SERP check (DataForSEO live, 2026-09-01, $0.11, balance $41 after): 12 head terms.
  Volumes are MODEST: spv company 720/mo, property investment company 590, buy to let
  limited company 480, spv for property 210, the rest 20-110. AI Overview present on ALL 12
  (click-eaten heads, same constraint as the finance corpus). Rankers = mortgage brokers
  (commercialtrust, mfbrokers, charcol, togethermoney), lenders (YBS/TMW), accountant
  content (dnsassociates, taxd, uklandlordtax), commodity agents (companyservicesuk,
  propertyspv.co.uk, rapidformations) and mid-authority niche sites. Provestor on 4 of 12;
  **GetGround absent from the top 6 on all 12**. No specialist owns the cluster; incumbents
  are beatable-grade domains. Implication: heads alone are ~2-2.5k/mo, so the funnel is the
  wider ltd-co/SPV cluster (~12-15k/mo) + our existing tax traffic + long-tail/tools, not
  head terms.
- Shape note from research: above the £300 line the customer is buying tax/legal substance
  around the formation, not the filing itself. We keep formation + address + software +
  structure-pack distribution and the cross-sell stack (BTL mortgage intro £500-950, bank
  £50, accountant lead £85). Advice-layer delivery structure = owner's call, out of scope.
