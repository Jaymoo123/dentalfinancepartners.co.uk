## 4. Feasibility read

**Verdict: thin, and already owned. Not a rankable inbound niche worth building a
standalone site for.**

- Total volume is 2,440/mo across 42 phrasings, and half of the seed list returned
  zero search volume outright. Once buckets (c) and (d) are stripped out, the real
  addressable pool is buckets (a)+(b) = 2,380/mo, spread across ~28 live keywords,
  most sitting at 10-90/mo each. There is no single high-volume anchor term to build
  a site's homepage around; the entire estate model runs on stacking dozens of
  individually small terms, and here the stack simply is not big.
- CPC is a genuine buyer-value signal (11-20 pounds on the marketing/SEO terms), so the
  clients who do search are worth having. But that same CPC is what has already pulled
  in five-plus specialist competitors (Mercia, PracticeWeb, 2020 Innovation, JE
  Consulting, Cal Partners) who occupy 4-12 of the top-10 slots on every high-volume
  term in bucket (a). Competition metadata reads LOW on the DataForSEO Ads-competition
  scale, but that measures ad auction pressure, not organic difficulty; the SERP pull
  shows these incumbents holding multiple positions each, which LOW-competition-index
  alone does not capture.
- The pattern that has worked estate-wide (Property, Medical, Solicitors, Dentists,
  generalist, construction-cis) is volume across dozens of tail terms feeding a
  lead-gen funnel. This niche fails that test on volume alone before competition is
  even considered.
- The one soft spot: SEO-specific queries (seo for accountants, accountant seo, seo for
  accountancy firms - 740/mo combined) pull a more generalist, less entrenched SERP
  than the design-specific queries. If there is any wedge here it is narrower content
  on "accountancy SEO" rather than a full website-design-and-marketing site, but at
  740/mo total that is still a small target for a new domain to carry alone.

**Recommendation: do not build a standalone site for this niche.** If there is appetite
to use this research, the more defensible move is a page or two inside an existing
asset (e.g. a growth/marketing angle on the accountancy-adjacent estate) rather than a
new domain competing against five specialists already dominating the only sizeable
terms.
