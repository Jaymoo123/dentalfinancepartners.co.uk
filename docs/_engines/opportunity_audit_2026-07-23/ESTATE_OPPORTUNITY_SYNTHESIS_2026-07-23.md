(property + construction-cis 07-23, others 07-20 or re-ingesting), full content inventory

## Verdict
Yes, real opportunity remains, but it is unevenly distributed and NOT mostly "write more posts".
Three distinct plays:

1. NET-NEW demand still on the table (young sites + fresh legislation): large.
2. DEEPEN-NOT-NEW (Bing page 1 / Google page 2+ proven demand): ~50 clusters estate-wide.
3. FIX-FIRST sites where new content would be wasted (agency, Medical).

  construction software to construction-cis, pharmacy towns to pharmacies). Piggyback + canonical
  ownership decisions needed.
- Two structural bugs found incidentally: Solicitors duplicate category routes
