## What the corpus says

- **Most-covered topics** (8 domains each): end-to-end lease extension process, collective
  enfranchisement, LAFRA 2024 explainer. Calculator pages on 7 domains. These are table stakes:
  the site must have A* versions but will not win on existence alone; win on freshness
  (commencement tracking) and tool depth.
- **Structural gaps found** (low coverage, now in the pool): competent landlord, leasebacks,
  RTM vs RMC, shared ownership extensions, management packs (LPE1), s.47/s.48 demand validity,
  arrears blocking claims, Crown/excepted leases, valuation case law.
- **Accounting-estate synergies flagged**: RMC/RTM company accounts and s.21 reports, reserve
  funds, SDLT/CGT on premiums, probate lease extensions (cross-link to wills-probate site).
- **Model observations**: homehold.org and extension.lease both run programmatic borough landers
  (candidate later phase, not in the 130-topic pool); freeholdcalculator.com and
  servicechargecalculator.co.uk prove single-tool sites sustain rankings, supporting the
  6-tool fleet in PHASE0_CALCULATORS_2026-08-10.md; leaseholdknowledge.com holds the campaign
  and news position that pairs with our pro-consumer editorial stance.
