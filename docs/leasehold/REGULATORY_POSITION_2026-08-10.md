# Leasehold niche: regulatory position (verified 2026-08-10)

Question: is introducing leaseholders to solicitors, valuers and enfranchisement specialists (lease
extension, collective enfranchisement, right to manage, service charge disputes, freehold purchase) a
regulated activity? Verdict: **NO. Unregulated introduction, same posture as the wills-probate and
settlement-agreement models.** Checked frameworks below.

## Frameworks checked

1. **FCA / FSMA regulated activities (RAO 2001).** No credit broking, insurance distribution,
   mortgage or investment activity in a professional introduction. Not in scope. (If the site ever
   discusses financing a lease extension via remortgage, that stays informational; no introductions
   to lenders or brokers without separate analysis.)

2. **Claims management (FSMA s.419A; Claims Management Activity Order 2018; FCA-regulated since
   April 2019).** Regulated CMC activity covers six sectors only: personal injury, financial services
   and products, housing disrepair, specified benefit, criminal injury, employment. Lead generation is
   a permission within those sectors. Enfranchisement, lease extension, RTM and freehold purchase are
   the exercise of statutory property rights, not claims for compensation or redress, and are not in
   any of the six sectors. Service charge challenges (LTA 1985 s.27A) are applications about
   contractual liability, not claims in a CMC sector; housing disrepair (the nearest sector) means
   compensation/repair claims by occupiers against landlords for disrepair, which the site will not
   tout for. Rule: never solicit "compensation claim" business (e.g. mis-sold leasehold compensation
   claims against developers or conveyancers ARE plausibly CMC/professional-negligence territory;
   excluded from the model). Source: FCA claims management regulation pages, PS18/23.

3. **LASPO 2012 ss.56 to 60 referral fee ban.** Personal injury claims only. Not in scope.

4. **Legal Services Act 2007 (reserved legal activities).** The site introduces; it does not conduct
   litigation, exercise rights of audience, or do reserved instrument activities (conveyancing of the
   freehold transfer stays with the solicitor). Information plus "speak to a specialist" is not a
   reserved activity. Standard faceless-authority rules apply: no "legal advice" claims.

5. **Estate Agents Act 1979 (considered and cleared).** Estate agency work means introducing a client
   to a THIRD PARTY who wishes to acquire or dispose of the interest in land (buyer-to-seller
   matchmaking). Introducing a leaseholder to their own professional adviser (solicitor or valuer) is
   not introducing them to a counterparty and is not estate agency work. Guard rail: the site must
   never broker freehold sales/purchases between leaseholders and freeholders or market freehold
   interests (e.g. "sell your freehold" investor matchmaking), which would engage the Act.

6. **Buyer-side rules (their problem, not ours, but relevant to the pitch).** Solicitors may pay for
   introductions in non-personal-injury matters subject to SRA Codes (transparency to client,
   independence, no contingent breaches); RICS valuers similarly under RICS professional standards.
   The Data (Use and Access) framework and UK GDPR require our existing consent model: mandatory
   data-sharing consent checkbox at capture (already standard estate-wide), DSA with each buyer,
   PECR rules for any email nurture.

## Positioning constraints (editorial, not licensing)

- Pro-consumer editorial stance (against freeholder overcharging) is safe and matches the SERP
  incumbents (homehold.org, hoa.org.uk, lease-advice.org). No regulatory bar on strong consumer
  advocacy; defamation discipline applies to naming specific freeholders/managing agents (do not).
- Valuation content must be flagged indicative: premium calculation is not formulaic and depends on
  negotiated/tribunal-determined rates. Never present a calculator output as a valuation (RICS Red
  Book valuations are the valuer's job; we must not purport to provide one).
- Reform-status content must carry the commencement caveats in GROUND_TRUTH_2026-08-10.md; stating
  uncommenced law as current is the main mis-selling style risk in this niche.

## Verdict

Unregulated lead generation. No FCA permission, CMC authorisation or other licence required for
introductions of leaseholders to solicitors, surveyors/valuers or managing agents for
enfranchisement, lease extension, RTM, freehold purchase and service charge matters, provided the
site (a) never solicits compensation-claim business, (b) never brokers the land interest itself,
(c) makes no legal/valuation advice claims. Same Ashfield Trading Ltd trading-name model, /contact
only, consent checkbox mandatory.
