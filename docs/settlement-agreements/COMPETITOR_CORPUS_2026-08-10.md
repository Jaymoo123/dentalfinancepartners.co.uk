## Gaps and observations

### Under-covered topics (1 to 5 domains)

- Getting a new job after a settlement agreement: 1 domain (GTE only). Strong employee-intent question, almost nobody answers it.
- Garden leave: 3 domains, 7 pages total.
- How much should I get (settlement amounts, payout benchmarks): only 4 domains address amounts head-on despite it being the core employee question. Most firms hide behind "it depends".
- Tribunal case law commentary: only 4 domains produce it at volume (Redmans dominates with hundreds of case reports). High effort, low differentiation.
- Redundancy vs settlement agreement as an explicit comparison: 5 domains, 6 pages. The comparison page is a gap even though both topics are separately saturated.
- Settlement agreement calculators and tools: 5 domains have interactive assets (see below).
- Directors and senior executives (exit packages for senior people): 5 domains, mostly thin.
- Performance management, PIPs and capability dismissals: 6 domains, 12 pages. Rising search theme, weak coverage.
- References after settlement or dismissal: nominally 8 domains but only about 22 thin pages, mostly single paragraphs inside wider guides.

Interactive tools are clearly the arms race in this niche: the two single-purpose settlement sites both lead with a calculator, and tribunalclaimsolicitors has built an entire tools hub (the offer checker and clause explainer are the most original assets seen).

### The two single-purpose settlement sites

- settlementagreement.com trades as Settlement Agreement Solicitors, the trading name of Settlement Agreement Solicitors Ltd (company 12589585), an independent SRA-regulated law firm. Founder and CEO Patrick Knight, chair James Fairclough, legal director and COLP Jarred Bold. The site is conversion-first, not content-first: only about 8 content URLs (a few redundancy and negotiation posts), plus a calculator, reviews page, FAQs and a "Start My Settlement" funnel with a freephone line open 9am to 8pm, Monday to Saturday. It wins on the exact-match domain and conversion optimisation, not on corpus depth.
- gte-settlementagreements.co.uk is GTE Settlement Agreement Solicitors, formerly Gordon Turner Employment Lawyers (SRA number 563689, registered office 70 Gracechurch Street, London). Model: same-day or within-the-hour sign-off of agreements, employer pays the fees, phone numbers for London, Manchester and Birmingham, and a share of revenue donated to charity. About 37 content URLs covering settlement basics, negotiation, tax and redundancy guides, plus a calculator. The footer credits an SEO agency for lawyers, so the site is deliberately SEO-built around a converted specialist brand.

### Other observations

- The head terms (settlement agreement basics, unfair dismissal, redundancy, discrimination, tribunal process) are saturated at 9 to 12 domain coverage. Differentiation there requires format (tools, calculators, templates) or depth, not another guide.
- Monaco Solicitors is the strategic benchmark for an employee-side site: content organised by employee journey (evidence gathering, negotiations, templates, letters), free downloadable templates as lead magnets, and a "virtual lawyer" letter builder.
- Employer-pays-fees is the universal settlement agreement business model, every specialist leads with it. Fee transparency beyond that is rare.
- Settlement agreement tax coverage (9 domains) is broad but shallow: mostly the 30k exemption and PILON. Only landaulaw covers PENP by name. Given the estate's tax authority, this is the most natural wedge.
- News and law-change content (ERA 2025, April 2026 changes) is produced by 9 domains, but mostly as stale one-off posts. A maintained "what changed" hub would outrank them.
