# Wills/Probate/Estate-Planning Brand Shortlist

For owner G1 decision only. Do not surface before build completion.

Date: 2026-07-24
Sibling reference: "Trade Tax Specialists" (construction-cis) — descriptive, trust-led, no personal names, no regulated-title implication.

## Market naming pattern observed

Sampled specialist domains from `classify.json` plus live search (Kings Court Trust, Legacy Wills & Estate Planning, Certainty Estate Planners, Estate Planning Solutions, ProbateLondon, National Will Register, Trust & Estate, The Probate Network, RGW Will & Estate Planning). Two dominant patterns:

1. **Noun-stack descriptive**: [Trust/Legacy/Estate/Probate] + [Court/Network/Register/Solutions/Partners] — trades on gravitas words, avoids personal names.
2. **Reassurance-word + category**: Certainty, Clarity, Peace(ful) + Wills/Estate Planning.

Avoid: anything ending "Law", "Legal", "Solicitors" — implies regulated legal practice, which this faceless content brand is not (same constraint as "Trade Tax Specialists" avoiding "Accountants" as a regulated claim).

## Candidates

1. **Estate Planning Specialists**
   - Rationale: direct mirror of "Trade Tax Specialists" naming formula, broadest category coverage (wills + probate + IHT + trusts).
   - Tone: authoritative, plain.
   - Domain: estateplanningspecialists.co.uk
   - Risk: generic phrase, likely used descriptively by multiple existing firms (not a registered brand collision found, but low distinctiveness). Unverified domain availability.

2. **Probate & Estate Specialists**
   - Rationale: leads with "probate" (highest-intent, highest-volume term per GSC/SERP patterns in this space) while keeping estate-planning breadth.
   - Tone: authoritative.
   - Domain: probateandestatespecialists.co.uk
   - Risk: long domain string. No direct existing-brand collision found in search. Unverified.

3. **The Probate Authority**
   - Rationale: "authority" positioning fits faceless-content-authority strategy explicitly (site model = proprietary-data authority, not personal advisers).
   - Tone: bold, confident.
   - Domain: theprobateauthority.co.uk
   - Risk: "authority" claim may read as overstated/puffery for a non-regulated content brand; no collision found but check against any FCA/SRA-adjacent naming rules informally (not a regulated title, should be fine).

4. **Estate & Probate Bureau**
   - Rationale: "Bureau" echoes existing specialist probatebureau.com — same trust register, distinct enough to avoid direct clash, connotes an official clearing-house function.
   - Tone: institutional, faceless (a "bureau" implies process not people).
   - Domain: estateandprobatebureau.co.uk
   - Risk: **name proximity to existing "Probate Bureau" (probatebureau.com)** — real trademark-adjacency risk, recommend deprioritising unless owner confirms comfortable.

5. **Wills & Estate Register**
   - Rationale: echoes National Will Register naming convention (trusted incumbent), "Register" implies authoritative record-keeping, matches data-asset positioning of the estate's content strategy.
   - Tone: institutional, official-sounding without claiming to be a government body.
   - Domain: willsandestateregister.co.uk
   - Risk: **proximity to National Will Register** (an actual regulated register service) — could imply affiliation with an official register. Moderate risk, would need disclaimer.

6. **Estate Clarity**
   - Rationale: reassurance-word pattern (Certainty Estate Planners precedent), short, memorable, one distinctive word plus category noun — breaks from the noun-stack crowd for brand recall.
   - Tone: warmer, still faceless (no personal name).
   - Domain: estateclarity.co.uk
   - Risk: less "specialist/authority" signal than descriptive names; softer for E-E-A-T signalling in AI citation contexts. No collision found. Unverified domain.

7. **Probate & Inheritance Specialists**
   - Rationale: explicitly folds in IHT/inheritance tax breadth (a core content pillar alongside wills/probate), keeps "Specialists" formula consistent with Trade Tax Specialists.
   - Tone: authoritative, tax-adjacent (useful since site sits inside a tax/accounting estate).
   - Domain: probateandinheritancespecialists.co.uk
   - Risk: long domain. No collision found.

8. **The Estate Administration Bureau**
   - Rationale: "estate administration" is the neutral catch-all industry term for probate work (used by Co-op Legal Services, Kings Court Trust); avoids "probate" narrowness, avoids "law/legal".
   - Tone: institutional.
   - Domain: estateadministrationbureau.co.uk
   - Risk: shares "Bureau" ending with Probate Bureau — same adjacency caveat as #4, milder since full phrase differs more.

## Recommended top pick

**#1 Estate Planning Specialists** (estateplanningspecialists.co.uk)

Reasoning: closest direct match to the proven "Trade Tax Specialists" formula (descriptive category + "Specialists"), broadest coverage across wills/probate/IHT/trusts so it won't box the content strategy into probate-only, no proximity risk to any named incumbent brand (Kings Court Trust, National Will Register, Probate Bureau, Farewill), and no regulated-title implication. #7 (Probate & Inheritance Specialists) is the strongest runner-up if the owner wants the name to foreground probate/IHT search intent more explicitly.

Avoid #4 and #5 unless the owner explicitly accepts the brand-adjacency risk to Probate Bureau and National Will Register respectively.

All domain-availability notes above are search-inference only (no WHOIS/registrar check performed) — verify before any registration.
