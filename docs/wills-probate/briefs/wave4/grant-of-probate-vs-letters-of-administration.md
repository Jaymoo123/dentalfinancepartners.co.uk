## SERP Evidence

- Myerson, Saunders Law, Slater + Gordon, Kings Court Trust and Anthony Gold all rank with near-identical explainer articles: Grant of Probate goes to named executors under a valid will; Letters of Administration go to the person(s) entitled under intestacy rules (or where the will has no willing/able executor).
- No calculator or interactive tool in the top results — pure explainer content, several with 2026-dated freshness signals (wuhld.com, Swansea Legal Solutions).

## Gap / Angle
- Nobody frames this as a single decision-tree the reader can self-diagnose in under a minute — we can lead with a 3-question check (valid will? named executor willing/able? intestate?) before the detail.
- Weak on the 'will exists but no executor able to act' edge case, which routes to Letters of Administration with Will annexed — genuinely under-covered nuance worth a dedicated section.
- No one links this decision to what happens next practically (PA1P vs PA1A form choice, HMCTS fee, timeline) — natural bridge to our forms/costs cluster.
