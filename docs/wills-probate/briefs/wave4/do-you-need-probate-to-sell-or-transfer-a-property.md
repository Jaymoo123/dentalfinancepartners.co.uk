## SERP Evidence
Query run: "do i need probate to sell a house"

- HomeOwners Alliance, Property Solvers, WillSafe, Farra and NPS Law dominate with 2026-dated 'selling a probate house' guides. Consistent line: you can market/instruct agents before grant but cannot exchange contracts or complete without it, unless the property was jointly owned as joint tenants (survivorship, no grant needed for that asset) or sold by a surviving joint owner.
- GalimAI and Probate Bureau add a timeline framing: ~3-4 months to grant, 3-6 months to sell, 6-9 months total.

Verdict: **medium** competition.

## Gap / Angle
- Competitors rarely address the executor's power to instruct an estate agent immediately (a genuine time-saver) versus the hard block at exchange — we should be explicit about what CAN start on day one.
- No one connects this to the CGT-on-sale angle (base cost = probate value) — natural link to our CGT-on-inherited-property brief.
- Auction-route timelines and the interaction with the IHT direct payment scheme (using sale proceeds to cover tax before probate is granted) is thin across the SERP — genuine gap.
