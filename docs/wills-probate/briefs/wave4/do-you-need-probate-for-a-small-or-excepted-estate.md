## SERP Evidence
Query run: "do i need probate for a small estate"

- Farra, MP Estate Planning, WillSafe and PDA Law converge on the same clarification: there is no statutory probate threshold — each bank sets its own release limit (typically £25,000-£50,000) — which is separate from the £325,000 IHT nil-rate band and separate again from the 'excepted estate' IHT-reporting category.
- IHT205 was abolished 1 January 2022; excepted-estate declarations are now made within the probate application itself, not a standalone form.

Verdict: **soft** competition.
