Outcome depends entirely on legal structure: sole trader business dies with the owner (assets fall into the estate); limited company shares (not company assets) pass to the estate, company continues as a separate legal entity; partnership dissolves by default unless a partnership agreement says otherwise.

Verdict: **soft** competition.
