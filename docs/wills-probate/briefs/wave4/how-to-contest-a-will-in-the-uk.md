- Consistent grounds: lack of testamentary capacity, undue influence, lack of knowledge and approval, fraud/forgery, improper execution, plus separate 1975 Act claims and proprietary estoppel. Time limits: 6 months from grant for 1975 Act claims; no fixed limit for validity challenges but practical urgency (caveat window).
