## Gap / Angle
- The direct link between an under-valued probate figure (to minimise IHT) and a LARGER CGT bill on eventual sale is a genuine trap most pages don't connect — executors and beneficiaries who lowball the probate valuation to save IHT often unknowingly inflate a future CGT liability; this trade-off deserves its own worked example, tying to our RICS valuation brief.
