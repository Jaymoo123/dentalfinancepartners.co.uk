Common structure: separate wills per jurisdiction (a UK will for UK assets, local wills for overseas assets), because a UK grant of probate has no automatic authority over foreign property; forced heirship rules in some civil-law countries can override a UK will's stated wishes; double-taxation treaties provide credit relief where both HMRC and a foreign authority tax the same asset.

## Gap / Angle
- The 'multiple wills accidentally revoking each other' drafting trap is mentioned nowhere clearly — each will must have specific wording limiting its revocation effect to assets in that jurisdiction only, and getting this wrong can invalidate the whole estate plan; this is the standout gap worth leading a section on.
- No consumer-facing page tabulates which popular expat/holiday-home destinations (Spain, France, Portugal, UAE) apply forced heirship vs testamentary freedom — a comparison table would be a genuinely useful asset tying into our /for/expats segment.
