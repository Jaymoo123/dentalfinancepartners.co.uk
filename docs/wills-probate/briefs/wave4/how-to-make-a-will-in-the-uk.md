## SERP Evidence
Query run: "does estate planning include wills"

- ClearLegacy, Octopus Legacy, Farra, WillSafe, wuhld.com and templatesuk.com all rank with 2026-dated 'how to write a will' step-by-step guides — a mature but not brutally competitive SERP.
- Consistent steps: list assets, choose beneficiaries, pick executors, name guardians, draft, sign with two independent witnesses (Wills Act 1837), store safely. Cost split clearly framed: online guided service ~£50-150/15-30 min vs solicitor £300-1,000/1-3 weeks.

Verdict: **medium** competition.
