## Gap / Angle
- The 'can be used before capacity loss' distinction for the Property & Finance LPA is the most practically important and most under-explained fact — many readers assume both types only activate after capacity loss, which is wrong and can leave people without financial help during temporary incapacity (e.g. hospitalisation, stroke recovery).
