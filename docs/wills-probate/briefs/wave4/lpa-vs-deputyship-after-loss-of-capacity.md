## SERP Evidence
Query run: "lpa vs deputyship after loss of capacity"

- Kiteleys, Stephensons, SO Legal, Tayntons, Mayo Wynne Baxter, Tollers, Barr Ellison and Gavin Edmondson all rank with clean comparison pieces — mature, moderately competitive SERP.
- Core distinction: LPA is signed with capacity, chosen by the individual; deputyship is court-appointed after capacity is lost, with the court (not the person) choosing who acts. LPA registration ~£82; Court of Protection deputyship application fee ~£371, plus ongoing supervision/reporting.

Verdict: **soft** competition.

## Gap / Angle
- This is fundamentally a prevention story — nobody frames the cost/control gap (£82 vs £371+ plus years of OPG reporting) as urgently as it deserves; leading with 'this is what happens if you never made an LPA' is a stronger hook than the neutral comparison framing every competitor uses.
