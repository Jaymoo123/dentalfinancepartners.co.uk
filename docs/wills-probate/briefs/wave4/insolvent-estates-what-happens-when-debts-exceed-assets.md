## SERP Evidence
Query run: "insolvent estate debts exceed assets"

- mpestateplanning.uk, The Law Society, Bishop Fleming, wuhld.com, Tanners, Lexology and New Leaf Solicitors give the legal-framework version: Administration of Insolvent Estates of Deceased Persons Order 1986 applies bankruptcy-style rules to a deceased's estate once liabilities exceed assets.
- Strict statutory order of priority for paying creditors; personal representatives who pay out of order can be personally liable to repay higher-priority creditors.

Verdict: **soft** competition.
