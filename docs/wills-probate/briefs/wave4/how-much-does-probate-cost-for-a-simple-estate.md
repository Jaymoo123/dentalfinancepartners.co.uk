## SERP Evidence
Query run: "how much does a simple probate cost"

- Pocketwise, WillSafe, costofthings.co.uk, ClearLegacy, Farra, NPS Law and Probate Bureau all publish 2026-dated cost breakdowns. Consistent figures: DIY simple estate ≈ £300-350 (application fee + copies); solicitor-led £1,500-5,000+.
- Major upcoming change flagged across sources: the £300 HMCTS application fee is scheduled to rise to £526 from 13 July 2026 (subject to parliamentary approval) — a genuinely timely fact most competitors mention only in passing.

Verdict: **medium** competition.

## Gap / Angle
- The July 2026 fee rise deserves to be the lead fact on this page (timeliness + genuine reader value) — most competitors bury it; we should front-load it and keep the page fresh as the change lands.
- Nobody itemises the 'hidden' costs beyond the headline fee (valuation £200-600, Section 27 notice ~£200-300, bond costs) that apply even to simple estates — good differentiation opportunity.
