## Gap / Angle
- The 'doesn't protect beneficiaries' limitation is consistently underplayed — most pages frame the notice as a blanket shield, when in fact a creditor can still pursue beneficiaries directly (just not the executor) after distribution; making this limitation crystal clear is a genuine trust-building differentiator, and links to our Insolvent Estates brief.
