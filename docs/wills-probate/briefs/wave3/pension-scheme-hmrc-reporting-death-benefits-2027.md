Google's synthesized summary leaned heavily on the mechanics: from 6 April 2027, most death benefits paid by registered pension schemes are treated as part of the deceased's estate for IHT; personal representatives (PRs) become responsible for reporting and paying IHT due on unused pension funds and death benefits; pension scheme administrators must provide the value of unused pension funds/death benefits within four weeks of being notified of the death; a new Pensions Direct Payment Scheme lets administrators pay IHT direct to HMRC; PRs can instruct administrators to withhold 50% of the benefit entitlement for up to 15 months after the end of the month of death to help fund the liability. This reporting-chain/timeline shape is the implicit answer-box structure to beat — no result we saw presents it as a single clear step-by-step timeline for a lay reader (the coverage skews law-firm/adviser technical, not consumer-facing).
