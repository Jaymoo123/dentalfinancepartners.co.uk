Google's synthesized summary made the central point clearly: under current rules, most unused pension funds and lump-sum death benefits sit outside the estate for IHT largely BECAUSE most modern schemes use a discretionary trust structure (trustees decide who benefits, so it's not a fixed entitlement of the deceased). From 6 April 2027 this protection is removed — most unused pension funds and death benefits are brought into the estate regardless of whether scheme administrators/trustees retain discretion over who receives them. This is the single most misunderstood point in the whole reform (people assume "it's in a discretionary trust, so it's protected" — a belief that stops being true from 2027) and is under-explained in plain English across the results seen.
