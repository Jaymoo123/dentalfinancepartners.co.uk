## Gap/Angle
- Almost every competitor result is written for the pension holder doing forward planning ("should I still use my pension for wealth transfer"), not for a personal representative who is actually administering a death that falls after 6 April 2027 — this page should be framed explicitly for that PR/executor moment.
- Content must state these as fact and must NOT give product/financial advice (e.g. no "you should take pension advice to do X", no specific product recommendations — informational only, general guidance to seek regulated financial advice where relevant).
- State plainly: effective date 6 April 2027; PRs/executors become legally responsible for reporting and paying any IHT due on unused pension funds and death benefits; death-in-service benefits from registered pension schemes are excluded from the charge; HMRC estimates 10,500 estates newly become liable for IHT for the first time per year, and a further 38,500 pay more; average increase for those paying more is around £34,000; up to 50% of the pension amount can be withheld/retained by the scheme for up to 15 months to help fund the IHT liability; spouse/civil partner exemption still applies to pensions passing to a spouse/civil partner.
