## Gap/Angle
- Nearly every strong result is written either for financial advisers/solicitors (AO Shearman, Tax Adviser Magazine, L&G adviser academy) or is a syndicated news rewrite (MSN, London Business Insider) with no original depth — there is no genuinely planning-neutral, plain-English "here is what is changing and why, full stop" explainer aimed at ordinary readers/PRs. This is the clearest gap: a page that explains the reform completely without steering anyone toward a specific planning action or product.
- Content must state these as fact and must NOT give product/financial advice (e.g. no "you should take pension advice to do X", no specific product recommendations — informational only, general guidance to seek regulated financial advice where relevant).
- State plainly: effective date 6 April 2027; PRs/executors become legally responsible for reporting and paying any IHT due on unused pension funds and death benefits; death-in-service benefits from registered pension schemes are excluded from the charge; HMRC estimates 10,500 estates newly become liable for IHT for the first time per year, and a further 38,500 pay more; average increase for those paying more is around £34,000; up to 50% of the pension amount can be withheld/retained by the scheme for up to 15 months to help fund the IHT liability; spouse/civil partner exemption still applies to pensions passing to a spouse/civil partner.
- Only the AO Shearman and L&G pieces mention the guidance timeline gap (final HMRC guidance not due until spring 2027, weeks before go-live) — worth stating clearly as a "this is still being finalised in places" honesty note, without speculating on what might change.
- No consumer-facing result explains the "why" (Treasury rationale: preventing pensions being marketed as a wealth-transfer vehicle) in plain terms alongside the "what" — most either do why (legal/adviser pieces) or what (news rewrites), not both together.
- This page should function as the estate-wide anchor/hub piece for the pensions-and-IHT-2027 cluster, cross-linking out to the other three wave-3 articles rather than duplicating their depth.
