A single side-by-side table (DB: usually can't be inherited as a lump sum, reduced spouse/dependant pension only, generally NOT subject to the new IHT charge; DC: an actual pot, can be left to a chosen beneficiary, WILL generally fall within the new IHT charge from 2027) is the clear structural win.
