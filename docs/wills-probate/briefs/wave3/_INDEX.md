## Locked policy facts (every Pensions and IHT 2027 brief carries these; no product/financial advice permitted)
- Effective date: 6 April 2027
- Personal representatives (executors) are legally responsible for reporting and paying any IHT due on unused pension funds and death benefits
- Death-in-service benefits from registered pension schemes are EXCLUDED from the charge
- HMRC estimates 10,500 estates become newly liable per year, and a further 38,500 pay more
- Average increase for those paying more is around £34,000
- Up to 50% of the pension amount can be withheld by the scheme for up to 15 months to help fund the IHT liability
- Spouse/civil partner exemption continues to apply to pensions passing to a spouse/civil partner
