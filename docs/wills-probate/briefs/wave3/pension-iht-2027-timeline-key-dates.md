- LOCKED POLICY FACTS to state as fact in this brief's content (informational only — no product/financial advice; direct readers to seek regulated financial advice for planning decisions):
  - Effective date: 6 April 2027.
  - From that date, personal representatives (PRs/executors) become legally responsible for reporting and paying any IHT due on unused pension funds and death benefits.
  - Death-in-service benefits from registered pension schemes are excluded from the charge.
  - HMRC estimates 10,500 estates become newly liable for IHT for the first time each year, and a further 38,500 pay more, as a result of these changes.
  - The average increase for those paying more is around £34,000.
  - Up to 50% of the pension amount can be withheld/retained by the scheme for up to 15 months to help fund the IHT liability (describe factually; do not invent further mechanics).
  - Spouse/civil partner exemption still applies to pensions passing to a spouse or civil partner.
