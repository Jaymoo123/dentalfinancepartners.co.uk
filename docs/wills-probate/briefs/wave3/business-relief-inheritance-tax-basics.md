## Competitor Coverage Outline
**gov.uk (Business Relief for Inheritance Tax: Overview)**:
- Three-part guide structure: overview, what qualifies, giving away business property/assets
- Definition: reduces the value of a business or its assets when calculating IHT
- Relief rates: 100% or 50%
- How executors claim: Form IHT400 + Schedule IHT413
- Eligible assets: property/buildings, unlisted shares, machinery
- Valuation at market value

**Royal London (adviser technical page)**:
- April 2026 reform detail: £2.5m combined cap, AIM/EIS shares moving to 50% relief

- Uncertain/unconfirmed figures to flag rather than invent: exact mechanics of spousal transfer of unused allowance, and treatment of relief for estates that already hold both agricultural and business property near the cap — do not state specifics beyond gov.uk/HMRC-confirmed detail.
