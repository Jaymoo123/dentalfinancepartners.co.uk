12-step checklist embedded: register death → locate/secure documents → secure property → Tell Us Once → notify institutions manually → value estate → determine probate necessity → apply for probate → address IHT → collect assets/settle debts → Gazette notice → distribute estate.

**insuristic.co.uk** — risk/insurance-angled, not a clean chronological checklist; organised around "phases" of risk management (insure property, pre-probate risk steps, post-Grant liability protection) plus a section on unknown creditors and Will search. Useful for gap-spotting (liability angle) but weaker as a pure checklist reference.

## Gap/Angle
- No competitor offers a genuinely downloadable/printable checklist (PDF or on-page print-friendly checklist widget) — differentiation opportunity.
- Most content is time-banded narrative prose; none pair the checklist with an interactive timeline tool users can personalise to their own estate.
- Liability/personal-risk framing (executor personal liability, Gazette notice protection) is present but scattered — consolidate into one clear "protect yourself" section with UK-specific legal grounding (Trustee Act 1925 s.27 Gazette notice, Grant of Probate requirement).
- Scotland vs England & Wales differences (confirmation vs probate, death registration windows: 5 vs 8 days) are under-addressed — most competitors write England/Wales only and imply UK-wide.
- Tie the checklist to a live progress/timeline estimator rather than static text — none of the competitors link out to an interactive tool.
