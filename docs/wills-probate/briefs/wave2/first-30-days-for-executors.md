## Gap/Angle
- Neither gov.uk nor Age UK organises tasks by **urgency/deadline** (e.g., "within 5 days" vs "within 30 days" vs "can wait") — our post should lead with a prioritised, time-boxed checklist, not a flat sequential list.
- No competitor visualises a **timeline** (day 1 / week 1 / weeks 2-4) — a simple day-banded structure is a clear differentiation and matches the "first 30 days" framing several competitors are already testing.
- Coverage of the **insurance-lapse trap** (empty property cover invalidated after ~30 days unoccupied) is buried or missing on gov.uk/Age UK — worth a dedicated callout since it's a genuine cost risk for executors.
- Most competitor content stops at "register death + notify + funeral" and treats probate/estate value as a separate later concern — we should explicitly bridge into probate-readiness tasks executors can start within 30 days (locating assets, requesting valuations) without overclaiming legal/tax expertise.
- None of the top results clearly distinguish "things only the next of kin can do" vs "things the named executor specifically must do" — useful clarity gap for our Executors category angle.
