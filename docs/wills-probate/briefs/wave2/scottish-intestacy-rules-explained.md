- The 1 May 2024 change (spouse/civil partner takes whole estate when no surviving children/grandchildren) is mentioned by only some sources — should be treated as current ground truth and stated clearly, not buried.
- Opportunity to be the clearest explainer of ultimus haeres (bona vacantia equivalent) and the executor-appointment/court petition step, which competitors gloss over.
