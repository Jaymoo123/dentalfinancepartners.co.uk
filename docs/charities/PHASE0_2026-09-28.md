### What I changed

1. **No visible focus ring anywhere on the site.** Tab traversal measured `outline-style: none`,
   `box-shadow: none` on every `LeadForm` and `DetailsForm` control (`role`, `message`, and the rest)
   on all nine form pages, and the same on both calculator inputs. Two separate causes:
   `src/components/forms/LeadForm.tsx:30` carries `focus:outline-none` with nothing behind it but a
   `focus:border-primary-600` colour change; and the shared tools Field
   (`packages/web-shared/tools/components/Field.tsx:88`) loses a same-layer race between its own
   `focus:outline-none` and the focus-visible outline appended to it. Unlike care and
   contractors-ir35, this site's forms had not been done at all. Fixed at the root with one unlayered
   rule in `src/app/globals.css`: `input/textarea/select:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid #1a5c4a;
   outline-offset: 2px }`. An unlayered rule beats every Tailwind utility, so it covers the site
   forms, the calculator inputs, the mini captures and anything added later, in one place instead of
   four. The shared half is logged below for the shared agent.
2. **The 13 Sonnet closers rewritten.** All six in `src/data/charity-types.ts` ran
   "Tell us about your X. We will explain what ... in plain English, with no obligation." and all
   seven in `src/data/charity-services.ts` ran "Tell us ... and we will confirm ..., in plain
   English, with no obligation." One template each, varying only the noun. Each is now a
   two-sentence, audience-specific closer naming the actual rule: the asset lock and dividend cap for
   CICs; three income types in three places for social enterprises; Charity Commission filing with
   format and scrutiny following the governing document for CIOs; receipts and payments against
   accruals for small charities; reserves, restricted funds and related-party disclosure for
   trustees; grant commitments and the reserves policy for funders; and for services, the
   examination-or-audit test, the SORP edition following the accounting period, separating restricted
   income when it arrives, a missing declaration costing a whole Gift Aid claim, reliefs attaching to
   supplies rather than to the charity, the public benefit test, and auto-enrolment from the first
   person on the payroll.
3. **The eight `CTA_BY_CATEGORY` entries in `src/lib/blog-cta-map.ts`.** The bodies stand: they
   already speak in the firm voice and name a specific rule per category, so I left them. The button
   labels did not. Every one read "our" from the visitor's side ("Check our VAT position",
   "Ask our governance question") while the page's own "we" is the firm, so the same word meant two
   different parties on the same panel. Relabelled as plain requests: "Request a compliance check",
   "Request a CIC review", "Confirm which one applies", "Request a Gift Aid check", "Request a VAT
   review", "Request a finance review", "Request a SORP check", "Ask a governance question".
   The file comment records the reasoning.
4. **"free review" cleared, prospect-facing:** `src/app/book/page.tsx:16,17`,
   `src/components/forms/BookingPicker.tsx:171`, and three strings in `src/config/lead-nurture.ts`
   (312, 427, 610). The nurture free-call line now reads "The first call is free, about 20 minutes on
   where your organisation stands, and if you want us to act you get a fixed fee in writing before
   anything starts."
