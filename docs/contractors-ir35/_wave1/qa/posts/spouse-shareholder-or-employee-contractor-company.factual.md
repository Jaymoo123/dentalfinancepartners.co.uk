## Edit log

1. Frontmatter: added `updatedDate: "2026-09-27"` alongside `dateModified`, because the site's loader reads `updatedDate`.
2. `summary`: WRONG as written. "A salary for real work is deductible and can unlock the Employment Allowance, worth around £6,000 of household tax on £12,570 of profit" attached the £6,000 to the Employment Allowance. The £6,000 is the salary-versus-dividend saving; the Employment Allowance is worth up to £10,500 of employer NIC relief. Rewritten so each figure sits on its own claim and the £10,500 is named. Still in band at 57 words.
3. `keyTakeaways[0]`: added the Employment Allowance condition. Without it the £6,000 overstates, because employer NIC at 15% on the £7,570 above the £5,000 secondary threshold (£1,135.50) is otherwise a real cost of the salary route.
4. Body, H2.1 opening sentence: added "the company can claim the Employment Allowance" to the conditions on the £6,000, for the same reason. Seven words.
5. `faqs[1]`: the arithmetic walked the reader from profit to net with no mention of employer NIC. Added "the Employment Allowance covers the employer National Insurance on it".

Nothing was removed. No UNSOURCED figure required removal: the only unsourced number (row 30) is a rhetorical hypothetical inside an example, not a rate or threshold presented as fact.
