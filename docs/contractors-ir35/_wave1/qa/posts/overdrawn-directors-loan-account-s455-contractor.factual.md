## The section point the writer raised

The writer's report is correct, and it is the headline finding of this review.

- CTA 2010 **s.464C** ("Treatment of certain repayments and return payments") is **omitted**.
  legislation.gov.uk shows the whole of Part 10 Chapter 3B omitted **with effect from
  30 October 2024** by **Finance Act 2025 s.81(3)(b) and (4)**.
- CTA 2010 **s.464ZA** ("Treatment of certain repayments") **exists** and was **inserted on
  30 October 2024** by **Finance Act 2025 s.81(3)(a) and (4)**.
- The substance carried over. s.464ZA(1): qualifying repayments of **£5,000 or more** within
  **30 days**, matched against relevant chargeable payments of **£5,000 or more** made in a
  **later accounting period**. s.464ZA(3): **£15,000 or more** owed immediately before the
  repayment, **arrangements** in place for replacement payments, replacement payments of
  **£5,000 or more**; no 30-day window. s.464ZA(7) gives the Treasury an order power over
  those amounts.

The post already cites s.464ZA, already attributes it to Finance Act 2025 with effect from
30 October 2024, and already states both limbs with the right thresholds and the right
"later accounting period" and "arrangements" qualifiers. Nothing to fix.

**Note for the manager, estate-wide.** `house_positions.md` §14 and the statute list at the
foot of that document cite "**s.464A onward**" and name no bed-and-breakfasting section, so
the house document is silent rather than wrong. Any older contractors or Property post that
names **s.464C** as live law is now **STALE** and should be swept by rule, not by list.
