### Link routing finding

`contractors-ir35/web/src/app/blog/` has exactly two dynamic routes, `[category]/page.tsx` and `[category]/[slug]/page.tsx`. There is no flat `/blog/[slug]` route, and `next.config.ts` declares no redirects or rewrites. A flat `/blog/<post-slug>` therefore lands on the category route, where `categories.find(...)` misses and `notFound()` fires: a hard 404. All four flat links were dead. The correct form is `/blog/<slugifyCategory(category)>/<slug>`, with `slugifyCategory` (`contractors-ir35/web/src/lib/blog.ts:96`) lowercasing, dropping parentheses, mapping `&` to "and" and hyphenating whitespace.
