## How currency is represented on this site

**Two representations, both live.** A pound-sign grep alone would have missed roughly a third of the money on this site.

- **Literal UTF-8 `£` (U+00A3): 3,049 occurrences.** The dominant form. Used in all page TSX, `src/data/`, `src/lib/calculators/`, and most of `content/blog/`.
- **ASCII `GBP <number>`: 178 occurrences**, concentrated in 6 files: `content/blog/limited-company-vs-umbrella-contractor.md`, `content/resources/ir35.md`, `content/resources/pay-planning.md`, `content/resources/structure.md`, `src/lib/support/faq.ts`, `src/tests/assistant-journey-opener.test.ts`. The whole of the support-widget FAQ and the whole of the gated resources corpus are ASCII-only.
- **`&pound;` HTML entity: 0.**
- **`£` escape: 1**, in `src/data/uk-contractor-index.json` (a data file, not copy).

Deriving command:
`python - <<'EOF'` walking the tree, counting `'£'`, `'&pound;'`, `'\\u00a3'` and `re.findall(r'GBP\s*[\d]')` per file (scratch script, deleted).

**Finding: no published pricing for our own services exists on this site in either representation.** Every `£` and every `GBP` traces to third-party or statutory figures (HMRC rates, Companies House £50, umbrella margins, market quotes for other accountants).
