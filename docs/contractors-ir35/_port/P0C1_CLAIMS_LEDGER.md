### Recompute note (how the take-home rows were derived)

Every take-home figure above was recomputed by hand from the HP §5/§6/§7 constants,
using the same algorithm the site's own library implements, rather than by running the
library (audit-only, no dev server):

- **Limited (outside):** `profit = turnover − salary − employerNI − expenses`;
  `employerNI = (salary − 5000) × 0.15`;
  `CT = profit × 0.25 − (3/200) × (250000 − profit)` in the marginal band, `profit × 0.19` at or below £50,000;
  `dividends = profit − CT`; dividends then taxed with residual PA first, £500 allowance at 0% consuming band space, £37,700 basic band at 10.75%, balance to £125,140 at 35.75%.
- **Umbrella (inside):** `gross = (assignment − margin + 0.15 × 5000) / (1 + 0.15 + 0.005)`;
  PAYE on the rUK bands with the £100,000 PA taper; employee NIC 8% on £12,570–£50,270 then 2%.

Cross-checked against `src/lib/calculators/tax2026.ts` and the golden values in
`src/lib/calculators/tax2026.test.ts` and `premium-tools.test.ts`, which agree with HP.
