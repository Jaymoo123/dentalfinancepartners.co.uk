## What to ADD

Three additions, roughly **400 to 700 new words** total.

1. **The exemption carve-out the page is missing.** The live page says a genuinely separate room let with no services attached can still be exempt land, and hedges it. Stage 1B B §7 gives the actual shape of that carve-out from the Schedule 9 Notes: exemption is restored only where the grant is of a **clearly demarcated area**, an entire building, floor or defined area, **with no related hairdressing services supplied with it**. Name what tips a grant back into the standard-rated side: assistants, reception, appointment booking, towel laundering, washbasins, a client waiting area. Those are not ancillary to a licence to occupy; they are an integral part of a single taxable supply of facilities. This is real added precision, not a restatement.

## BOUNDARY TABLE (mandatory, one new table in the added section)

The live page does not have this table and it is the clearest form the added carve-out can take. Columns: **"Standard rated: a supply of facilities"** against **"Potentially exempt land: a clearly demarcated area with no related services"**. Four trade-anchored row pairs minimum:

| Standard rated: a supply of facilities | Potentially exempt land: a clearly demarcated area with no related services |
|---|---|
| A stylist renting a chair on the salon floor with the salon's reception taking bookings | An entire self-contained first floor let to a stylist who takes their own bookings and employs their own help |
| A chair let with towel laundering and a shared washbasin included | A defined treatment room let with no services, where the tenant supplies everything |
| A barber's chair let with a junior assistant provided by the shop | A separately accessed unit within the building, let as land |
| A beauty room let with the salon's client waiting area and card machine | A demarcated area let where the only thing supplied is the space itself |

Carry the hedge every row: the exempt side is the exception, it is fact-specific, and relabelling an invoice does not create it.
