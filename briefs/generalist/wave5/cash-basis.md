## Required structure (H2 skeleton)

1. **First 60 words, the answer.** The cash basis means you count income when the money arrives and expenses when you pay them. Since 6 April 2024 it is the default for sole traders and partnerships of individuals, there is no turnover limit of any kind, and you leave it only by electing on your tax return to use traditional accounting.
2. There is no turnover threshold, and there never will be one you have to watch. What was abolished and when.
3. It is the default now, not a scheme you join.
4. Who can use it, and who is shut out (excluded trades; limited companies).
5. **Boundary table** (see below).
6. What changes in practice: timing of income, timing of expenses.
7. The three old drawbacks that are gone: the £500 interest cap, the loss-relief bar, and the thresholds.
8. Capital spending on the cash basis, in summary, then link `sole-trader-capital-allowances`.
9. Electing out into traditional accounting.
10. Worked examples (two, below).
11. What people get wrong.
12. FAQ (10 to 14).
13. Links out.

## BOUNDARY TABLE (mandatory, exact columns)

Columns: **"Cash basis"** against **"Traditional accounting (accruals)"**. Four trade-anchored row pairs minimum, each a real business situation, not an abstraction:

| Cash basis | Traditional accounting (accruals) |
|---|---|
| A mobile mechanic invoices £2,400 on 30 March and is paid on 12 April: taxed in the later year | Same job, taxed in the year of the invoice |
| A hairdresser buys £900 of stock in March and pays the supplier in May: deducted in the later year | Deducted when the bill is received |
| A window cleaner buys a £1,200 pressure washer: the full cost is an ordinary expense when paid, no pool, no AIA | The cost goes into the capital allowances regime, AIA or WDA |
| A driving instructor buys a car: still capital allowances, because a car is the one exception (CAA 2001 s.1A(4)) | Same treatment, capital allowances by CO2 |
| A builder with a £40,000 bad debt from an unpaid invoice: never taxed on it, because it was never received | Taxed on the invoice, then a separate bad-debt write-off |
