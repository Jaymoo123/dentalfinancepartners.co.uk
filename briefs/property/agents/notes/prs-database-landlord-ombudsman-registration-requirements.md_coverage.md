## 6. Write-time verification outcomes

**1. Section 74's content: VERIFIED, from the locked house position.** The pack recorded that
house_positions did not resolve this. It does now: §26.5 carries an [08-21] lock added by the
pre-write verification pass. Section 74 is headed "Local Commissioners' investigation of complaints
by persons who are not tenants" and **amends s.26 Local Government Act 1974** to adjust the boundary
between Local Commissioner investigations and the Housing Act 1996 housing ombudsman scheme,
excluding certain housing matters where the complainant is eligible for the housing ombudsman route.
It is a **jurisdictional boundary provision**, NOT "interpretation and consequential provisions" and
NOT an extension of landlord redress. The page is written to that lock. The incoming page's
"extends the existing Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman jurisdiction" was wrong and is
gone. The frozen sibling's "interpretation and consequential provisions" is also wrong and was **not
edited**; it is logged at `notes/delta.md` items 1 and 6.

**2. The possession bar: VERIFIED in part, refinement DECLINED.** Checked against
legislation.gov.uk section 90 on 2026-08-21. Section 90 inserts a new subsection into section 7 of
the **Housing Act 1988**: the court may not make an order for possession while the landlord (or any
joint landlord) is in breach of the database marketing-and-registration duty, with **two exceptions,
Ground 7A and Ground 14**. That much is kept, written conditionally, and framed as the strongest
"why this matters" line on the page. **The "validity of the notice goes to the registration status
at the date of service" refinement is not supported by the section text and has been removed.** No
substitute proposition was invented. Ground numbers are not pinned in prose; the page says "apart
from on the anti-social behaviour grounds".

**3. The rogue-landlord database contrast: operative status VERIFIED, access claim DECLINED.**
Checked against legislation.gov.uk (Housing and Planning Act 2016 section 28) and gov.uk on
2026-08-21. Confirmed: the section **remains in force**; the database began operating on **6 April
2018**; the Secretary of State establishes and operates it and local housing authorities make and
edit entries; inclusion is offence-driven (banning order, conviction for a banning-order offence, or
repeat civil penalties); and the Renters' Rights Act 2025 section 94(2) inserts a new subsection 4
cross-referring to the section 75 database, a prospective amendment not yet applied. **Not verified
from a primary source: that the register is restricted-access and not publicly searchable.** The
gov.uk publication page carries only document links, and the claim could not be confirmed at write
time. The contrast is therefore kept in three sentences built only on verified facts, and the page
**asserts nothing about who can access the 2016 register, in either direction**. The old six-row
table, which asserted "restricted to local authorities + database operator; not publicly
searchable", is gone.

**4. Penalty section attribution: DECLINED by design, no verification sought.** house_positions
§26.5 attributes both limbs to Section 66 on the redress side; §26.6 names Section 92 for the
criminal limb on the database side and names no civil-penalty section. Rather than resolve the
conflict, the page writes the penalties **without pinning section numbers at all**: "up to £7,000
where it treats the failure as a breach, or up to £40,000 where it deals with it as an offence".
That is accurate against both sections, sits in the reader's register, and satisfies hard rule 1.

**5. HMRC manual reference: VERIFIED, with a refinement.** Checked at gov.uk on 2026-08-21.
BIM38500 is the **contents** page, titled "Wholly and exclusively: fines, penalties and damages:
contents". The operative proposition, "penalties for infractions of the law are not allowable", sits
at **BIM38515**. The page's foot reference line therefore cites "BIM38500 to BIM38515". Neither
reference appears in prose; the page says only that a financial penalty for a regulatory breach is
never deductible and that professional fees defending a notice usually are, where they are revenue
in character. house_positions §26.9 anchors "BIM38500+", which is imprecise rather than wrong;
logged at `notes/delta.md` item 9 rather than patched from inside a content task.

**6. house_positions internal inconsistency: RESOLVED, no delta needed.** The pack warned that
§26.6's older "Practical writing rule" paragraph might still carry the pre-patch "expected before
April 2027" phrase. It does not. That paragraph is now headed "superseded 2026-08-21; use the
[08-21] framing" and states the retirement explicitly. The [08-21] framing was used.
