## 6. Ground truth (house_positions.md sections that govern)

- **§20 whole regime**, esp. **§20.12 commencement timeline** (verified 2026-05-22;
  see §8 mandatory re-verification) and **§20.13 do-not-write**. The Act is
  **Renters' Rights Act 2025 (2025 c. 26)**; "2026" is commencement context only.
- **§26.3 MEES (critical do-not-write):** EPC E floor under SI 2015/962, £3,500
  cap in reg 24 as amended (NOT reg 25(2)); EPC C 2030 NOT enacted, no SI laid;
  £10,000 cap is consulted-on only. This constraint IS explainer 3's hook.
- **§26.5 redress:** plural approved-scheme regime, never "the single statutory
  ombudsman"; £25,000 compensation figure is policy commentary, not the Act;
  penalties £7,000/£40,000; **agents' own redress duty is the PRE-EXISTING 2014
  regime (SI 2014/2359), never conflate with the RRA landlord regime.** That
  distinction is explainer 5's core.
- **§26.6 PRS Database:** ss.75-96, no commencement order as of 2026-05-24
  (re-verify per §8); never "landlords must register from 1 May 2026".
- **§19.13 MTD managed portfolio:** landlord is the filer, agent statement flows
  to categories, gross-not-net trap. Plus §19.14 digital links, §19.17 do-not-write.
- **§26.7 tax hooks:** scheme/database fees deductible (ITTOIA 2005 s.272); MEES
  spend capital/revenue split per PIM2030 (double-glazing = repair); grants reduce
  CGT base cost.
- Deposit tax edges (explainer 4): verify against PIM1051/PIM2510 + TDP rules at
  write time; no house_positions lock exists yet, so the pack must carry its own
  verified positions and QA must check them adversarially.

## 8. Pre-write verification (COMPLETE 2026-08-21, house_positions back-patched)

Run 2026-08-21 against legislation.gov.uk (primary) + gov.uk (secondary);
house_positions §20.12, §26.3, §26.5, §26.6, §26.8 patched the same day. Writers
read house_positions fresh; the headlines that change what this cluster writes:

- **SI 2026/638 (Commencement No. 3, in force 22 Jun 2026)** exists: category 1
  hazard civil penalties up to £7,000; NOT the Decent Homes Standard.
- **The 1 May 2026 wave is private (non-social-housing) assured tenancies only**
  (SI 2026/421 reg 2 carve-out); social housing is Phase 2 (2027, expectation).
- **The "information sheet" is a statutory duty** (SI 2026/324): existing
  tenancies had a 31 May 2026 deadline; the 5,400/mo query is duty-shaped, page 1
  explains the duty and links gov.uk's sheet (never republish the PDF).
- **Form 4A** is the prescribed s.13 rent-increase notice (SI 2026/354); the s.8
  form is a live published version, never a static download.
- **Database:** s.75 Prospective, no fee regs, NO citable fee figure; "regional
  rollout late 2026" = roadmap expectation. **Redress:** no scheme SI exists;
  membership expected 2028, expectation only.
- **MEES:** enacted position unchanged (EPC E + £3,500 reg 24 cap). Policy
  hardened 21 Jan 2026: Oct 2030, £10,000, dual metric, BUT government itself
  says primary legislation is needed first, SI aimed at 2027. That is page 2's
  hook stated even more strongly: "must hit EPC C by 2030" is not just unenacted,
  the government's own response says it cannot lay the SI yet.
- SI 2026/325 revoked the 2015 AST prescribed-requirements regs; three new
  do-not-writes added to house_positions §26.8.
