## Subject (the H1 IS the question)
Capital gains tax when you sell a second home. The searcher owns a holiday home, a flat they
kept after moving in together, or an inherited property they held onto: not a portfolio
landlord. Answer-first: yes, a second home is taxed at 18%/24% on the gain above 3,000 GBP,
and here is the arithmetic, the reliefs that can shrink it, and the 60-day deadline.

## Facts (verify against `docs/property/house_positions.md` section 5 + gov.uk)
- 2026/27: AEA 3,000 GBP; residential rates 18% basic / 24% higher; gain stacks on income.
- 60-day report-and-pay where tax is due (UK residents).
- PRR: only if the property was at some point your only or main residence; final 9 months
  deemed occupation; election mechanics belong to
  /blog/capital-gains-tax/cgt-main-residence-election-two-properties (link, do not restate).
- Spouse/civil partner transfers: no gain no loss, s.58 - inherits base cost.
- Letting relief: shared-occupation only since April 2020 - do not overstate.
- Do NOT write "CGT rate is 28%" (pre-Oct-2024 rate).
