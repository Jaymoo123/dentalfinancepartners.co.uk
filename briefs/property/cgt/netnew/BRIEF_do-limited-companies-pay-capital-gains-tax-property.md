## Subject (the H1 IS the question)
Do limited companies pay capital gains tax on property? Direct answer: no. Companies pay
corporation tax on chargeable gains, individuals pay CGT. The page owns that question and the
company-side mechanics that follow from it.

## Facts (verify every one against `docs/property/house_positions.md` section 5 + gov.uk)
- Companies do NOT pay CGT; they pay corporation tax on chargeable gains (25% main rate /
  19% small profits rate 2026/27, marginal relief between).
- Companies get NO annual exempt amount and NO 18%/24% CGT rates.
- Indexation allowance frozen at December 2017 still applies to companies' pre-2018 base costs
  (house_positions section 5) - individuals get nothing.
- Individuals selling shares IN the company: that is CGT at 18%/24%, a different event - one
  section, forward-link, do not restate BADR depth
  (link /blog/capital-gains-tax/business-asset-disposal-relief-residential-property-qualification).
- 60-day CGT return does NOT apply to companies (they report through CT); non-resident companies
  have their own rules - flag, do not deep-dive.
- ATED-CGT abolished (house_positions section 18.5) - do not resurrect it.
