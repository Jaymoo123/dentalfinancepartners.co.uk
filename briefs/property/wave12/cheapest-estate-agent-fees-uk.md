## CGT hook (mandatory, the wave's organising rule)
Chasing the cheapest fee is a rational move only until you compare it with the tax on the same sale. Under **TCGA 1992 s.38(1)(c)**, the agent's commission is an incidental cost of making the disposal and comes off the gain, and **s.38(2)** puts "agent" and "advertising to find a buyer" expressly in the list. So on a property that is not fully sheltered by private residence relief, the real cost of an agent is the fee **after tax relief**: at the 24% higher residential rate, saving £2,000 of commission saves you only about £1,520 net, because the £2,000 you did not spend is £2,000 you no longer deduct. That reverses the usual comparison logic in a way no fee-comparison site will publish, because their business is the comparison. The other half of the hook is the sale price itself: a cheaper agent who achieves £5,000 less on the sale price costs a taxable seller far more than the fee saving, and where tax is due the **FA 2019 Sch 2 para 3(1)(b)** 60-day clock from completion is running while you are still arguing about it.
