## 4. Judgement calls recorded

1. **NIM74250 Tier 3 wording is stale and the page says so.** The manual (updated 22 July
   2026) still reads "liable to pay Class 2 NICs under section 11(2)", but S5 confirms that
   liability was removed from 6 April 2024. The page states the tier framework as HMRC
   writes it, then explicitly tells the reader to read "liable to pay Class 2" as shorthand
   for being inside the self-employed regime, with the practical 2026/27 effect being a free
   qualifying year at/above £7,105 plus a Class 4 bill. Flagged for the QA track: this is a
   deliberate correction of a live HMRC manual, and it is the only place the page departs
   from the literal words of a source.
2. **Statute discipline: 3 references, cap was 4.** NIM74250 (once, as the decision aid for
   which tier you are in), "section 11(6)" and "Social Security Contributions and Benefits
   Act 1992" (once, in the sentence telling a Tier 2 reader to get HMRC to confirm the tier
   in writing before relying on cheap years). ITTOIA, s.15, s.2(1)(b), s.11(2) and FA 2026
   were all verified (S4, S5, S6, S14) and deliberately **not** named in the body, per
   `_language_spec.md` rules 1-3. No Royal Assent, no enactment dates, no amendment chains.
3. **No devolved carve-out beyond one line.** NI genuinely is UK-wide, so the one-line
   statement is factual rather than a carve-out, and it disposes of the question instead of
   inviting it. Income tax devolution is pushed to the linked 2027-rates page.
4. **The Budget angle is answered, not amplified.** `landlords could face national insurance
   on rental income in budget` (50/mo) is a live query with a factual answer: it did not
   happen, and S14 shows the government chose a 2pp property rate rise instead and named the
   NI gap as the reason. That is a better answer than the peer's, which treats the proposal
   as live speculation.
5. **The £35,000-three-ways table is the page's differentiator.** No competitor quantifies
   the exemption. It also sets up the 2027 rate rise as the partial clawback, which links the
   page into the cluster without a forced cross-sell.
6. **Second person carries every figure** per `_language_spec.md` rule 10. Named third-person
   examples (Marcus, Priya) are confined to their own worked-example blocks; the first draft
   used "a landlord with £20,000..." at the point of the headline figure and it was rewritten
   to "if your only income is £20,000 of rental profit, you pay...".
7. **No closing hedge, no qualifier bullets in the CTA** (rules 12-13). CTA H2 is a question,
   names one service (landlord tax review) and gives the reason it beats guessing with the
   two failure modes priced (£956.80 for a year you already had; trading treatment found
   after a sale).
8. **Tier 2 voluntary Class 2 is presented as a "get it in writing" item, not a plan.** Most
   readers are Tier 1 and cannot pay at all; the page says so before it prices Class 2, so
   nobody budgets £189.80 they are not entitled to pay.
