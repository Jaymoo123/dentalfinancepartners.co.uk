# MEES regulations for landlords: the EPC E floor, exemptions, the £3,500 cap and penalties, as the law stands

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: Energy Efficiency (Private Rented Property) (England and Wales) Regulations 2015 (SI 2015/962): reg 22 (sub-standard property definition), reg 23 (prohibition on letting sub-standard domestic PR property), reg 25 (relevant energy efficiency improvements/cost cap route), reg 27 (non-domestic prohibition, named only for the A19 cross-link), regs 36-40 (PRS Exemptions Register, compliance notices, penalty notices, publication penalty, domestic breaches). WebFetch-VERIFIED at legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/962/contents on 2026-08-15.
- Supporting: EPC E floor applies to new domestic tenancies since 1 April 2018 and all continuing domestic lets since 1 April 2020; landlord cost cap £3,500 including VAT (reg 25(2)); per HP §26.3 (verified against SI 2015/962 + gov.uk MEES guidance). Domestic penalty amounts under regs 38-40: Stage 2 verifies the per-breach figures (and the widely-quoted £5,000 aggregate) against the SI text before any figure enters the page.
- House position reference: §26.3 (MEES regime + EPC C 2030 IS NOT ENACTED, critical do-not-write) and §26.7 (capital vs revenue on energy improvement spend). No new lock needed; this page is the natural third anchor on §26.3.
