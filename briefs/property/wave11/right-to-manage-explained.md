- **Lock now exists:** §31.C (locked 2026-08-15) replaces the seed's "NEW LOCK NEEDED" note. Write against §31.C, not the seed's proposal text.
- **Amended costs position, precise (resolved for lock §31.C verify list, "the precise amended s.50 costs position"):** LFRA 2024 s.50 fetched 2026-08-15 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/22/section/50): inserts CLRA 2002 **s.87A** (costs: general) and **s.87B** (tribunal power to order costs where claim ceases), amends s.82, REPEALS ss.88-89. s.87A: the RTM company and its members have "no liability for any costs incurred by any other person" in consequence of a claim, except by statute or court/tribunal order. s.87B exception: the tribunal may make the company liable for a landlord's reasonably incurred non-litigation costs only where the claim notice is withdrawn or ceases to have effect AND the company acted unreasonably in giving or failing to withdraw it. In force 3 Mar 2025 (SI 2025/131 reg 2(b)). Decision-page framing: a well-founded RTM claim now carries no freeholder-costs bill; the residual cost risk is a badly run or abandoned claim.
- **Qualifying thresholds (re-confirmed from section text):** s.79(5): membership at claim-notice service must include qualifying tenants of at least HALF the flats (both, where only two); s.72 + LFRA s.49: non-residential limit 50% since 3 Mar 2025.
- **No-fault character:** s.71-84 impose no mismanagement test (contrast LTA 1987 Pt 2 manager appointment); confirmed in Shelter pack recheck.
