# Landlord licensing explained: mandatory, additional and selective licensing, and how to check which applies to you

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: Housing Act 2004, Part 2 (licensing of houses in multiple occupation, ss.55-78) and Part 3 (selective licensing of other residential accommodation, ss.79-100); s.249A financial penalties. WebFetch-VERIFIED at legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/34/contents on 2026-08-15 (Parts 2, 3 and s.249A all confirmed present).
- Supporting: SI 2018/221 (Prescribed Description Order: mandatory licensing = 5+ persons, 2+ households); s.254 HMO definition; s.72/s.95 offences; s.249A civil penalty up to £40,000 per offence from 1 May 2026 per SI 2026/319 (per HP §26.9, do NOT write £30,000); RRO 2-year window per RRA 2025 (§20.10); Rent Smart Wales and Scottish licensing named as out-of-scope regimes.
- House position reference: §26.9 (HMO + selective licensing mini-lock, Wave 7) + §20.10 (RRO extension). No new lock needed; this seed anchors on the existing lock.

THE ACCOUNTANT'S ANGLE: licence fees are deductible revenue expenses (ITTOIA 2005 s.272; existing page `hmo-licensing-fees-tax-deductible-uk-landlords` is the canonical deductibility treatment, forward-link rather than restate); civil penalties and fines are NOT deductible (BIM38500+); unlicensed letting also blocks s.21 (historic) and exposes 2 years of rent to an RRO, which is a repayment out of taxed income, an angle no lettings-compliance site writes; company-vs-personal makes no difference to deductibility but the licence holder entity must match the landlord entity.

## Key questions this page must answer
1. What are the three licensing regimes and one-line definitions of each (mandatory HMO, additional HMO, selective)?
2. How do I find out in two minutes whether MY property in MY council area needs a licence?
3. What is an HMO for these purposes (s.254: 3+ persons, 2+ households, shared amenities) and when does mandatory licensing bite (5+ persons per SI 2018/221)?
4. Does selective licensing really cover ordinary single-family lets? (Yes, within a designation.)
5. How much does a licence cost, per type, and how long does it last? (cost table, Stage 2 verified ranges; note fees vary by LA)
6. Are licence fees tax-deductible? Are penalties?
7. What happens if I let without a licence (s.72/s.95 offence, s.249A civil penalty up to £40,000, RRO up to 2 years' rent, banning orders)?
8. Do I need a new licence when I buy a tenanted property, or when the licence holder changes? (Licences are not transferable, Stage 2 verifies s.68.)
9. How does licensing interact with the other compliance certificates (EICR, gas, EPC as licence conditions)?
10. Jurisdiction: England framework; Wales (Rent Smart Wales), Scotland and NI have separate regimes (first-screen statement per §5.3).
