# Landlord licensing explained: mandatory, additional and selective licensing, and how to check which applies to you

## Statutory anchor

WebFetch-VERIFIED at legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/34/contents on 2026-08-15 (Parts 2, 3 and s.249A all confirmed present).
