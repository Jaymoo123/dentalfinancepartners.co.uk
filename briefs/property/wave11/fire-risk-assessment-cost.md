## Stage 2 extensions (2026-08-15, cluster B session)

### 1. Verify-at-write resolutions

- **Fire Safety Act 2021: RESOLVED (resolved for lock §26.12).** Fetched https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2021/24/section/1 on 2026-08-15. s.1 amends art 6 RRFSO: where a building contains two or more sets of domestic premises, the Order applies to "the building's structure and external walls and any common parts" and "all doors between the domestic premises and common parts" (flat entrance doors); external walls include doors, windows and anything attached (cladding, balconies). Commencement: England 16 May 2022 (Wales 1 Oct 2021). Writers may cite FSA 2021 s.1 with this scope.
- **BSA 2022 s.156: RESOLVED (resolved for lock §26.12).** Fetched https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/30/section/156 on 2026-08-15. Amends the RRFSO, in force 1 October 2023: art 9 recording duty extended, the responsible person must now record the fire risk assessment IN FULL (the previous significant-findings-only threshold removed); fire safety arrangements must be recorded (art 11 exemption language removed); new art 21A (comprehensible fire safety information to residents of buildings with 2+ domestic premises); new art 22A (outgoing responsible person must hand over fire safety information to successor); art 22/new 22B strengthened cooperation duties incl. with accountable persons in higher-risk buildings. Answers seed key question 4: since 1 Oct 2023 EVERY responsible person records the full assessment, whoever carries it out.
- **SI 2022/547 height thresholds: RESOLVED (was the §26.12 verify-at-write threshold item).** Fetched https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2022/547/made on 2026-08-15. reg 3: "high-rise residential building" = 2+ sets of domestic premises AND at least 18 metres above ground level OR at least 7 storeys. Regs 4-8 (secure information box, external wall design/materials, floor plans, lifts/fire-fighting equipment, wayfinding signage) apply to high-rise buildings ONLY. reg 9 (information to residents) applies to ANY building with 2+ sets of domestic premises and common parts through which residents would evacuate. reg 10 (fire doors): the information duty applies to any 2+-premises building with such common parts; the CHECKING duty (quarterly common-parts doors / annual flat entrance doors, writer confirms cadence from reg 10 text at write) applies to buildings above 11 metres. NOTE the seed's reg numbering was one off (it had reg 9 as fire doors, reg 10 as resident info); correct allocation is reg 9 = resident information, reg 10 = fire doors.
- **Smoke/CO alarm SI: RESOLVED.** SI 2015/1693 confirmed as the Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarm (England) Regulations 2015, fetched https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1693/made on 2026-08-15: reg 4(1)(a)(i) smoke alarm on each storey with living accommodation; reg 4(1)(a)(ii) CO alarm in rooms with a solid fuel combustion appliance; reg 4(1)(b) working-order check on day tenancy begins; reg 5 remedial notices; reg 8(2) penalty cap £5,000. STILL FLAGGED: the 2022 amendment (SI 2022/707, CO duty extended to any fixed combustion appliance other than a gas cooker) was NOT verified (as-made text predates it); writer must verify the amended reg 4 text before stating the CO scope, or cite the 2015 duties with an "as amended in 2022" hedge only after fetching the current version.
