# Service charge disputes: a leaseholder's statutory toolkit for challenging the bill

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 (c. 70): s.18 (meaning of service charge and relevant costs), s.19 (limitation: reasonableness), s.20 (consultation requirements), s.20B (18-month time limit on demands), s.20C (costs of proceedings), s.21 (service charge information), s.22 (inspection of supporting accounts), s.27A (FTT jurisdiction on liability). ALL headings WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1985/70/contents on 2026-08-15.
- Supporting: Service Charges (Consultation Requirements) (England) Regulations 2003 (SI 2003/1987): s.20 consultation triggers at more than £250 per tenant for qualifying works (reg 6) and more than £100 per tenant per accounting period for qualifying long term agreements (reg 4(1)). WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/1987/made on 2026-08-15. LFRA 2024 Part 4 service charge transparency provisions exist on the statute book; Stage 2 verifies their commencement position on write date and states in-force vs not-in-force explicitly (§31.3a pattern).

## Key questions this page must answer
1. What legally counts as a service charge (s.18), and why does the definition matter for what you can challenge?
2. What does "reasonably incurred" mean under s.19, and who decides?
3. When must the landlord consult before charging (s.20 + SI 2003/1987: over £250 per tenant for works, over £100 per year for long-term agreements), and what happens to the recoverable amount if they did not?
4. What is the s.20B 18-month rule and how does it kill late demands?
5. What information can I demand (s.21) and can I inspect the invoices and accounts behind the charge (s.22)?
6. How do I challenge at the First-tier Tribunal under s.27A, what does it cost, and what is a s.20C costs-protection application?
7. Should I withhold payment while disputing? (Risks: forfeiture threats, mortgage conditions, admissibility.)
8. What should I actually look for in the service charge accounts (apportionment, reserves, management fees, insurance commissions)?
9. Are service charges tax-deductible for a landlord leaseholder, and how do disputes and refunds affect that?
10. What do the LFRA 2024 transparency reforms change, and are they in force yet? (Stage 2 states verified position; §31.3a discipline.)
