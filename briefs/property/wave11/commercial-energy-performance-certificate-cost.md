# Commercial EPC cost: what drives the price and what you should pay

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: Energy Performance of Buildings (England and Wales) Regulations 2012, SI 2012/3118 (framework only: reg 6 duty verified 2026-08-15 per the A17 seed; reg 22 accreditation schemes; reg 26 meaning of energy assessment). This is a COST page: the statute sets the duty, the market sets the price; every figure on-page is market data, not statute, and carries Stage 2 source verification.
- Supporting: NDEA accreditation levels (Level 3 / Level 4 / Level 5 building categories under the government's SBEM/DSM assessment conventions, published in the gov.uk approved software and assessor guidance); reg 11 (10-year validity, drives cost-per-year framing).
- House position reference: none directly (cost page). Framework lock per F-73 item 4 (SI 2012/3118 EPC machinery, raised by cluster B; A6/A7/A17/A18 all anchor on it). No HP conflict surface.

## Framing differentiator (anti-templating, anti-cannibalisation)
This is the COST page of the A17/A18/A19 trio. Lane lock: A18 prices the certificate and ONLY the certificate. It does NOT re-litigate when an EPC is legally required (A17, forward-link once in the opening section) and does NOT cover the MEES E floor or the cost of improvement works to reach it (A19 owns MEES; improvement-works spend is A19's capital allowances territory, forward-link once). The near-identical SERP for the three terms is the sameness trap: this page's spine is a pricing model, not a regulation walk. Structure the body around the five cost drivers: (1) floor area, (2) building complexity level (Level 3 simple existing buildings assessed in SBEM, Level 4 more complex new/existing, Level 5 buildings needing Dynamic Simulation Modelling: atria, complex HVAC, large glazed facades), (3) assessment method (SBEM vs DSM, DSM typically multiplies the fee), (4) building services complexity (air-conditioning, mixed heating fuels, multiple zones), (5) survey logistics (access, drawings available vs measured survey, multi-unit portfolios). A DEC (public buildings, annual for over 1,000m²) is a different product at a different price: one short boundary paragraph, no more.

Existing-page boundary: `energy-performance-certificates-epc` quotes "£150 to £500 for commercial" in one line; this page supersedes that with a banded table and should trigger an INTERNAL_LINK back-patch from that page (raised as F-80, extending cluster B's F-74 pattern). The Wave 11 sibling A6 `epc-certificate-cost-uk` owns residential pricing; name it, do not duplicate a single residential figure. Competitor read (verified pack): the SERP is assessor firms, two of the three London-weighted; none splits pricing by SBEM Level 3/4/5 and none touches VAT or deductibility. Our win = a national, level-split benchmark table from a neutral publisher plus the tax layer.

The accountant's angle no assessor directory can write: the EPC fee is a deductible revenue expense of the property business (regulatory compliance cost, wholly-and-exclusively); the VAT question is the hidden 20%: a landlord letting exempt without an option to tax cannot recover the input VAT on the assessor's fee, so the true cost comparison between quotes is VAT-inclusive for most small commercial landlords, VAT-exclusive for opted buildings; and for portfolio landlords the procurement play (batching assessments across units, aligning renewal dates with the 10-year cycle and planned transactions) is a cashflow decision an accountant would actually model. Cost table plan (ALL figures marked for Stage 2 source verification against at least two live assessor-market sources): rows by building band, e.g. small retail/office under 100m² · small industrial unit · mid-size office 100-1,000m² · large/multi-zone over 1,000m² · Level 5 DSM buildings; columns: typical range, assessor level, method, turnaround. Seed expectation from current market reads: roughly £150-£400 at the simple end, £400-£1,000 mid-band, £1,000-£5,000+ for DSM-class buildings; VERIFY AND REPLACE at Stage 2, never ship seed ranges unverified.

First-screen jurisdiction statement mandatory (England and Wales; Scotland and NI price and regulate separately).

### 1. Verify-at-write items RESOLVED (Stage 2, 2026-08-15)

**Statutory corrections and confirmations:**
- **10-year validity is reg 9(2), NOT reg 11** (seed cited reg 11 twice; reg 11 is the advertisement rating-statement duty). Verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/3118/regulation/9 on 2026-08-15: valid = "entered on the register no more than 10 years before the date on which it is made available" (reg 9(2)(a)). Use reg 9(2) for all cost-per-year and renewal-cycle framing. **Resolved for lock §26.13.**
- **reg 22 accreditation verified** (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2012/3118/regulation/22, 2026-08-15): assessor must belong to a Secretary-of-State-approved accreditation scheme; the Level 3/4/5 categories are scheme/convention machinery under gov.uk guidance, not regulation text; cite them to the gov.uk approved-software and assessor guidance, never to the SI.
- **Penalty exposure one-liner (Q10):** reg 38 amounts now resolved (see A17 extension for full text + URLs): non-dwelling = 12.5% of rateable value, minimum £500, maximum £5,000, £750 default where no rateable value applies. This page carries ONE sentence with the range and forward-links A17; no penalty table here (lane rule).

**Market cost ranges: VERIFIED against two live assessor-market sources on 2026-08-15 (the seed's "verify and replace" condition is met):**
- fast-epc.co.uk/commercial-epc-prices-2025/ (fetched 2026-08-15): banded by floor area, "up to 25m² £159 · 26-50m² £199 · 51-150m² £249 · 151-250m² £299 · 251-500m² £349 · 501-750m² £459 · 751-1,000m² £550 · over 1,000m² custom quote". London/SE assessor, treat as the competitive low anchor.
