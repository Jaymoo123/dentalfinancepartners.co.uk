# EICR certificate cost for landlords: what you will pay, what drives it, and what is deductible

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: The Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020 (SI 2020/312), reg 3 (duties of private landlords: inspection by a qualified person at intervals of no more than 5 years, report to existing tenants within 28 days, to a new tenant before occupation, to the local housing authority within 7 days of written request, remedial work within 28 days) and reg 11 (financial penalty, "must not exceed £30,000"). WebFetch-VERIFIED at legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/312 (contents, reg 3, reg 11) on 2026-08-15.
- Supporting: regs 4-10 (remedial and urgent remedial action procedure); reg 12 (penalty procedure and appeals). BS 7671 (IET Wiring Regulations) as the referenced standard, not legislation.

THE ACCOUNTANT'S ANGLE IS THE HOUSE DIFFERENTIATOR: Checkatrade and electrician directories can publish price ranges but structurally cannot write the tax layer. This page owns: EICR fee = revenue expense deductible against rental income (ITTOIA 2005 s.272 wholly-and-exclusively, compulsory regulatory cost); remedial work split = like-for-like rewiring/consumer-unit repair is revenue, a first-time full rewire materially upgrading the installation can be capital (CGT base-cost addition, TCGA 1992 s.38(1)(b), mirroring the §26.7 MEES analysis); reg 11 penalties NOT deductible (BIM38500+); company landlords deduct against corporation tax the same way; pre-letting EICR before first tenancy and the pre-trading rules.

## Key questions this page must answer
1. How much does an EICR cost for a typical rental property (1-2 bed flat, 3 bed house, 4+ bed/HMO)? (cost table, Stage 2 verified ranges)
2. What drives the quote up or down: circuits, property age, access, region, urgency?
3. What does the price include, and what counts as extra (remedial works, re-test, certificate reissue)?
4. How often must a landlord pay for one (reg 3: at least every 5 years, or sooner if the report says so)?
5. What do C1/C2/C3/FI codes mean for the follow-on bill, and which codes legally require remedial spend within 28 days?
6. Is an "unsatisfactory" report a failed purchase, i.e. do you pay again for the re-test?
7. Is the EICR fee tax-deductible, and how is remedial work split between revenue and capital?
8. What does non-compliance cost instead (reg 11 penalty up to £30,000 per breach) and is that penalty deductible? (No.)
9. Do HMOs and licensed properties pay more, and does the licence application require the EICR?
10. England-only regs: what applies in Scotland (EICR required since 2015 under different rules), Wales and NI? (jurisdiction statement in first screen per §5.3)
