# The Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024: what is actually in force (and what is not)

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024 (c. 22): s.27 (removal of qualifying period before enfranchisement and extension claims), s.33 (longer lease extensions, 990 years), Sch 4 (standard valuation method, determining and sharing market value, the marriage value abolition vehicle) with s.36(5) (prospective omission of LRHUDA 1993 Sch 13), ss.49-52 (right to manage), s.124 (commencement by regulations). WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/22/contents on 2026-08-15.
- Supporting (the commencement evidence class, THE page's spine): commencement regulations at legislation.gov.uk are the ONLY authority for what is in force. Verified 2026-08-15: SI 2024/1018 (Commencement No. 1); SI 2025/57 (Commencement No. 2 and Transitional Provision) commencing s.27 on 31 Jan 2025 (verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/57/made); SI 2025/131 (Commencement No. 3) commencing ss.49-52 and s.64 (RTM scope) on 3 Mar 2025 (verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/131/made). No commencement regulation commences the valuation provisions (Sch 4, s.33, marriage value abolition) as at 15 Aug 2026. Corroborating: legislation.gov.uk's own "Changes and effects yet to be applied" annotation on LRHUDA 1993 Sch 13 (verified 2026-08-15), which still lists the repeal as prospective. High Court dismissal of the freeholders' judicial review (Oct 2025) survived-but-did-not-commence context per §31.3a.
- House position reference: §31.3a (the in-force table, re-locked 2026-08-15) is the canonical source; §31.3, §31.9 (do-not-write list), §31.10.

## Framing differentiator (anti-templating, anti-cannibalisation)
CORRECTION-OF-RECORD FLAGSHIP. Most 2026 web content, including national press and conveyancer blogs, asserts marriage value is abolished. It is not. The page's entire angle is being the accurate in-force ledger: an IN FORCE / NOT IN FORCE / DRAFT ONLY table, each row citing its commencement regulation (or the absence of one) at legislation.gov.uk, because the commencement SI is the only evidence class that settles the question, not news coverage, not the Act's own text. Planned table rows: 2-year rule abolished (IN FORCE, 31 Jan 2025, SI 2025/57); RTM reforms ss.49-52 (IN FORCE, 3 Mar 2025, SI 2025/131); marriage value abolition (NOT IN FORCE, remains payable in full today); 990-year term (NOT IN FORCE, extensions today are +90 years); prescribed deferment/capitalisation rates (NOT IN FORCE, consultation closes 23 Sep 2026); £250 ground rent cap (DRAFT ONLY, Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill, not this Act at all, a category error the SERP constantly makes). Safe formulation per §31.9: "LFRA 2024 abolishes marriage value, but that provision is not yet in force; a claim made today still pays it." NEVER give a specific commencement date for the valuation provisions (2027-28 at the earliest, any sourced date is unreliable).

## Key questions this page must answer
1. Is marriage value abolished? (No. On the statute book, not commenced, still payable in full; the flagship correction.)
2. How do I check for myself what is in force? (Commencement regulations at legislation.gov.uk; show the reader the method, cite SI 2024/1018, SI 2025/57, SI 2025/131.)
3. What IS in force today, and since when? (2-year rule gone 31 Jan 2025; RTM reforms 3 Mar 2025.)
4. Are 990-year lease extensions available? (No; today's statutory extension is existing lease + 90 years at peppercorn.)
5. Why does most of the internet say marriage value is gone, and why is it wrong? (Enactment vs commencement, s.124.)
6. When will the valuation reforms commence? (Unknowable; rates consultation closes 23 Sep 2026, secondary legislation still required, 2027-28 at the earliest, no reliable date exists.)
7. Did the freeholders' legal challenge stop the reforms? (No, JR dismissed Oct 2025, but dismissal commenced nothing.)
8. Is the £250 ground rent cap part of this Act? (No, draft Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill; forward-link A15 and the Bill page.)
9. Should I extend now or wait? (Decision framework, not advice: 80-year cliff, rate uncertainty cuts both ways.)
10. What does the in-force position mean for RTM claims and for buyers who just completed? (Forward-links A11, A9.)
