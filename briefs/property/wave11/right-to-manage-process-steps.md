# The Right to Manage process step by step: notices, deadlines and the acquisition date

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 (c. 15) Part 2 Chapter 1 procedure: s.78 (notice inviting participation), s.79 (notice of claim to acquire right), s.84 (counter-notices), s.87 (deemed withdrawal), s.90 (the acquisition date). WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15/contents on 2026-08-15.
- Supporting: LFRA 2024 ss.50-52 (costs of RTM claims, compliance, tribunal-only first-instance route), IN FORCE 3 Mar 2025 via SI 2025/131 (WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/131/made on 2026-08-15); prescribed-form regulations for s.78/s.79 notices (Stage 2 verifies the current England SI and whether forms were updated post-LFRA).
- House position reference: §31.3a (RTM reforms IN FORCE Mar 2025). NEW LOCK NEEDED: shares proposed §31.C RTM lock with A11/A12 (flag F-76).

## Framing differentiator (anti-templating, anti-cannibalisation)
RTM TRIO LANE LOCK. A13 = THE PROCEDURAL TIMELINE PAGE, and only that. Structure the body as the statutory sequence with statutory time limits attached to each step: (1) confirm qualification and form the company (one sentence each, forward-links to A11 and A12); (2) s.78 notice inviting participation to every qualifying tenant not already a member, and the mandatory waiting period before the claim notice; (3) s.79 claim notice on the landlord and other required recipients, contents and prescribed form, membership threshold at service (at least half the flats); (4) the landlord's s.84 counter-notice window and the two possible responses (admit or dispute); (5) dispute route to the First-tier Tribunal (post-LFRA s.52: no first-instance High Court applications); (6) s.87 deemed withdrawal traps; (7) s.90 acquisition date and the handover mechanics (contract notices, contractor novation, service charge balances and accrued funds transfer, insurance from day one); (8) costs exposure at each stage under the amended s.50 regime (Mar 2025).

It does NOT re-argue whether RTM is worth it or restate qualifying criteria in depth (A11), and does NOT cover running the company after acquisition (A12). Timeline visual: seed a step/deadline/who-acts TABLE, statutory deadlines cited to sections, procedural durations marked for Stage 2 verification. THE ACCOUNTANT'S ANGLE lives at the handover step: the service charge reconciliation at the acquisition date (uncommitted funds, accrued liabilities, final accounts from the outgoing agent) is where claims go financially wrong, and it is the layer LEASE and solicitor content skims.
