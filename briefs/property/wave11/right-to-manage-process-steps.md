# The Right to Manage process step by step: notices, deadlines and the acquisition date

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 (c. 15) Part 2 Chapter 1 procedure: s.78 (notice inviting participation), s.79 (notice of claim to acquire right), s.84 (counter-notices), s.87 (deemed withdrawal), s.90 (the acquisition date). WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15/contents on 2026-08-15.
- Supporting: LFRA 2024 ss.50-52 (costs of RTM claims, compliance, tribunal-only first-instance route), IN FORCE 3 Mar 2025 via SI 2025/131 (WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/131/made on 2026-08-15); prescribed-form regulations for s.78/s.79 notices (Stage 2 verifies the current England SI and whether forms were updated post-LFRA).

## Stage 2 extensions

### Verify-at-write items resolved (Stage 2, 2026-08-15)
All fetched at legislation.gov.uk on 2026-08-15, from section text (resolved for lock §31.C verify list, "statutory day-counts inside ss.78-90"):
- **s.78(5) (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15/section/78):** the participation notice must offer inspection of the articles for "at least two hours on each of at least three days" within "the seven days beginning with the day following that on which the notice is given", with copies available on request during those seven days; a notice failing this is invalid (s.78(6)).
- **s.79(2) (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15/section/79):** "The claim notice may not be given unless each person required to be given a notice of invitation to participate has been given such a notice at least 14 days before." So: minimum 14-day wait between s.78 notices and the s.79 claim notice.
- **s.79(5):** membership at claim-notice service must include qualifying tenants of "not less than one-half of the total number of flats" (both tenants where the premises contain only two).
- **s.80(6) (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15/section/80):** the claim notice must specify "a date, not earlier than one month after the relevant date" for counter-notices. **THE CLAIMED 35-DAY COUNTER-NOTICE WINDOW IS NOT STATUTORY.** The statute sets a minimum of ONE MONTH from the day the claim notice is given; the Brady Solicitors "35-day" figure is a practice convention (a month plus a service margin), and leaseholderled's "one month" matches the statute. Page states one month as law and may note claim notices commonly allow a little longer.
- **s.80(7):** the claim notice must specify an intended acquisition date "at least three months after" the s.80(6) counter-notice date. So the statutory floor from claim notice to acquisition is roughly four months even uncontested.
- **s.84 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15/section/84):** counter-notice no later than the s.80(6) date; either admitting entitlement or disputing on stated grounds. If disputed, the RTM company must apply to the appropriate tribunal "not later than the end of the period of two months beginning with the day on which the counter-notice... was given". Post-LFRA s.52 (in force 3 Mar 2025): no first-instance High Court route; FTT (Property Chamber) is first instance in England.
- **s.87 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15/section/87):** deemed withdrawal where (1) counter-notice disputes and NO tribunal application is made within the two-month period; (2) the tribunal application is withdrawn; (3) company insolvency events (winding-up, administration, receiver, CVA, strike-off under CA 2006 ss.1000/1001/1003). Escape hatch: written agreement from the counter-notice giver that the company was entitled.
- **s.90 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15/section/90):** acquisition date = (uncontested) the date specified in the claim notice, s.90(2); (tribunal-determined) "three months after the determination becomes final", s.90(4); (landlord agrees late in writing) three months after the agreement, s.90(5); (s.85 court-order cases) the date in the order, s.90(6).
- **Costs at each stage (resolved for lock §31.C, "the precise amended s.50 costs position"):** LFRA 2024 s.50 (verified, see URL in A11 block) inserts CLRA 2002 s.87A (no liability for others' costs, general rule) and s.87B (tribunal may award a landlord's reasonably incurred NON-litigation costs only where the claim notice is withdrawn or ceases AND the company acted unreasonably), and repeals ss.88-89. In force 3 Mar 2025, SI 2025/131 reg 2(b). Timeline consequence: the old "you pay the freeholder's process costs win or lose" warnings across the SERP are dead law.
- **Prescribed forms (resolved for lock §31.C, "current prescribed-form SI... whether forms were updated post-LFRA"):** SI 2010/825 (Right to Manage (Prescribed Particulars and Forms) (England) Regulations 2010) checked at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2010/825, 2026-08-15: "There are currently no known outstanding effects"; last amended 1 Jul 2013 (tribunal-name changes). Forms were NOT updated post-LFRA; the 2010 forms remain the prescribed forms for s.78/s.79 notices in England.
