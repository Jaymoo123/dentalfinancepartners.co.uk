Three things must change to clear these pages, in priority order.

1. **The costs section duplication between A11 and A13** is the one real lane violation:
   two writers produced near-identical s.87A/s.87B expositions under near-identical H2s
   with near-identical "Until March 2025..." openers. A13 keeps it, A11 cuts to two
   sentences and a link.

   share a closing formula, an enumerate-then-First/Second device, "cheap insurance",
   and "rarely ... they are the ones who". Rename and de-duplicate.

   the exact banned construction ("the part the standard rights guides skip", "where the
   process guides are thinnest"). Cap at one per page and rewrite the two banned ones.

freeholder's membership right "stays exactly as it was" (it is created on acquisition,
and A12 says so), and the service charge page's flagship apportionment example totalling
