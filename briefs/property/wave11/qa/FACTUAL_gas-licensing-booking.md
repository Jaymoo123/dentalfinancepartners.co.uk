[ADVISORY] landlord-licensing-explained: "Section 254 of the Housing Act 2004 sets the definition. Under the standard test a property is an HMO when it is occupied by three or more people forming two or more households" (body H2 "What counts as an HMO here" and FAQ 3) — right figure, wrong section. I fetched s.254(2): the standard test contains NO three-person threshold. Its limbs are (b) "occupied by persons who do not form a single household" and (f) "two or more of the households ... share one or more basic amenities". The three-person floor comes from Schedule 14 (a building occupied by only two persons forming two households is not an HMO), which I fetched and confirmed. §26.9 carries the same shorthand, so the lock wins and this is not a blocker, but the citation as written is unsupported by the provision named. Drop-in fix: "Section 254 of the Housing Act 2004 sets the definition, and Schedule 14 sets its floor. Under the standard test a property is an HMO where it is occupied by people who do not form a single household and two or more of those households share a toilet, personal washing facilities or cooking facilities; a building occupied by only two people in two households is excluded by Schedule 14, which is where the familiar three-person threshold comes from."

[ADVISORY] landlord-licensing-explained: metaDescription "fees from £600" contradicts the page's own fee table and FAQ, both of which put selective licences "from around £500". Drop-in fix: "fees from £500" (metaDescription length stays inside limit at 152 characters).

[ADVISORY] landlord-licensing-explained: "Budget alongside the fee for the conditions the licence carries: an annual gas safety record supplied to the council, an electrical installation condition report on request ..." — the HA 2004 Schedule 4 mandatory condition is a *declaration as to the safety* of electrical appliances and furniture on demand, not an EICR. An EICR is commonly imposed as a discretionary local condition and separately required by SI 2020/312, but it is not the statutory licence condition the sentence implies. Drop-in fix: "an annual gas safety record supplied to the council, a safety declaration (and, under most councils' own conditions, the EICR) on request ...".
