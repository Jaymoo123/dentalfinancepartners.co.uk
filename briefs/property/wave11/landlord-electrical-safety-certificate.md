# Landlord electrical safety certificate (EICR): the full duty cycle, deadlines and penalties

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: The Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020 (SI 2020/312), reg 3 (duties of private landlords in relation to electrical installations: inspection and testing by a qualified person at intervals of no more than 5 years or sooner if the last report specifies; copy of report to each existing tenant within 28 days; to a new tenant before occupation; to the local housing authority within 7 days of written request; further investigative or remedial work completed within 28 days or any shorter period the report specifies). WebFetch-VERIFIED at legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/312/regulation/3 on 2026-08-15.
- Supporting: regs 4-10 (remedial action, urgent remedial action, local authority powers); reg 11 (financial penalty per breach "must not exceed £30,000", verified at /regulation/11 on 2026-08-15); reg 12 (penalty procedure + appeal to First-tier Tribunal).

## Key questions this page must answer
1. Is an "electrical safety certificate" the same thing as an EICR, and what document does the law actually require? (Terminology: EICR = the report; there is no separate "certificate".)
2. Which tenancies are in scope of SI 2020/312, and which are excluded (Sch 1 exclusions: social landlords, lodgers, long leases etc., Stage 2 verifies the exclusion list)?
3. How often is an inspection required and what resets or shortens the 5-year clock?
4. Who counts as a "qualified person" to carry out the inspection?
5. What are the four supply deadlines (28 days to existing tenants; before occupation to new tenants; 7 days to LHA on request; retention until the next report)?
6. What happens on an unsatisfactory report: what do C1, C2, C3 and FI mean, and what must be done within 28 days?
7. What can the council do if the landlord does not comply (remedial notice, arranging urgent remedial action, recovering costs)?
8. How big is the penalty and per what (reg 11: up to £30,000 per breach; multiple breaches can stack)? Can you appeal (reg 12, FTT)?
9. Is any of this deductible: the report fee, remedial works, the penalty?
10. Jurisdiction: England-only SI; Scotland has required EICRs since 2015 under separate rules, Wales and NI differ (first-screen statement per §5.3).
