# Landlord electrical safety certificate (EICR): the full duty cycle, deadlines and penalties

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: The Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020 (SI 2020/312), reg 3 (duties of private landlords in relation to electrical installations: inspection and testing by a qualified person at intervals of no more than 5 years or sooner if the last report specifies; copy of report to each existing tenant within 28 days; to a new tenant before occupation; to the local housing authority within 7 days of written request; further investigative or remedial work completed within 28 days or any shorter period the report specifies). WebFetch-VERIFIED at legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/312/regulation/3 on 2026-08-15.
- Supporting: regs 4-10 (remedial action, urgent remedial action, local authority powers); reg 11 (financial penalty per breach "must not exceed £30,000", verified at /regulation/11 on 2026-08-15); reg 12 (penalty procedure + appeal to First-tier Tribunal).

- **SI 2020/312 Sch 1 excluded tenancies: RESOLVED (resolved for lock §26.10).** Fetched https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/312/schedule/1/made on 2026-08-15. Eight excluded classes: (1) private registered providers of social housing; (2) shared accommodation with the landlord or landlord's family (shared toilet/washing facilities/kitchen/living room); (3) long leases (LRHUDA 1993 long lease, or a tenancy granting occupation for 7 years or more); (4) student halls of residence; (5) hostels and refuges; (6) care homes (Care Standards Act 2000); (7) hospitals and hospices; (8) other healthcare-provision accommodation (NHS bodies).
