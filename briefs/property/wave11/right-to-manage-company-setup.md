# Setting up an RTM company: formation, prescribed articles, and the obligations nobody warns you about

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 (c. 15) s.73 (RTM companies: private company limited by guarantee) and s.74 (RTM companies: membership and regulations). WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15/contents on 2026-08-15.
- Supporting: RTM Companies (Model Articles) (England) Regulations 2009 (SI 2009/2767), prescribed model articles for RTM companies (limited by guarantee, no share capital; directors' general authority; membership rules). WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2767/contents/made on 2026-08-15. Companies Act 2006 obligations (accounts, confirmation statement, directors' duties ss.171-177) cited generically; Landlord and Tenant Act 1987 s.42 (service charge trust money) and LTA 1985 ss.21-22 (service charge information and inspection) for the money-handling layer, Stage 2 verifies the s.42 citation before use.
- House position reference: §31.3a (RTM reforms IN FORCE Mar 2025). NEW LOCK NEEDED: shares proposed §31.C RTM lock with A11/A13 (flag F-76).

## Framing differentiator (anti-templating, anti-cannibalisation)

Scope: incorporating the company at Companies House (limited by guarantee, prescribed name conventions, the 2009 model articles apply automatically and override inconsistent bespoke articles); who must be allowed to join as members (qualifying tenants, and the landlord after acquisition, s.74); directors' appointment and their Companies Act 2006 duties in a building where the directors are also the customers; the ongoing compliance calendar (annual accounts, confirmation statement, registered office, PSC register); dormant-vs-active accounting treatment before and after the acquisition date; how service charge money must be handled once the company manages the block (trust money under LTA 1987 s.42 kept separate from company funds, and why RTM company accounts are usually dormant or near-dormant while service charge accounts run separately); corporation tax position (mutual trading and why a compliant RTM company typically has little or no CT liability, marked for Stage 2 verification against HMRC guidance); insurance and VAT edge cases.

## Stage 2 extensions

### Verify-at-write items resolved (Stage 2, 2026-08-15)
- **Model articles AMENDED (new finding; resolved for lock §31.C, conductor should add at Stage 2b):** SI 2009/2767 was amended by **SI 2025/130** (RTM Companies (Model Articles) (England) (Amendment) Regulations 2025), in force alongside the 3 Mar 2025 reforms. Reg 2 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/130/regulation/2/made, fetched 2026-08-15): inserts a definition of "lease" (long lease per ss.76-77 CLRA 2002) and replaces article 33(3)(f) so that "the votes exercisable by landlords under leases of the whole or any part of the Premises shall be capped at one third of the votes exercisable by qualifying tenants". Page states the articles as SI 2009/2767 as amended by SI 2025/130; the landlord-vote cap is a strong differentiator (no pack page carries it).
- **HMRC mutual-trading / flat-management references (resolved for lock §31.C verify list; CORRECTION):** CTM40145 fetched 2026-08-15: it covers **Community Interest Companies**, not flat management companies; the lock's "CTM40145 area" pointer is wrong (F-81 raised). Verified correct references:
  - **BIM24000** (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim24000, "Meaning of trade: mutual trading and members clubs: contents"): "a mutual trader is not liable to tax on any profits arising from their mutual trade". Use for the mutuality principle.
  - **TSEM5710** (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/trusts-settlements-and-estates-manual/tsem5710, "Trusts for particular purposes: flat management companies - S42 Landlord and Tenant Act 1987"): service charge funds held on s.42 trust; from 2016-17 trust income charged at the standard rate not trust rates; from 6 Apr 2024 trusts with income up to £500 need not report or pay tax on it. This ALSO resolves the LTA 1987 s.42 citation check (HMRC's own manual title confirms the s.42 trust basis).
  - gov.uk "Dormant companies and associations" (https://www.gov.uk/dormant-company, verified live): dormancy for CT and for Companies House are separate tests; use for the dormant-vs-active section.
  WRITER RULE: state the CT position as: a compliant RTM company typically has little or no CT because service charge money is s.42 trust money (not company income), member transactions are mutual (BIM24000), and non-mutual income (e.g. bank interest above the £500 trust de minimis, third-party income) is what creates a CT exposure. Do not cite CTM40145.
- **Companies House figures (resolved for lock §31.C verify list "Companies House fees and penalty bands"):** gov.uk fees page fetched 2026-08-15: digital incorporation **£100** (paper £124); confirmation statement **£50** digital (£110 paper). Late filing penalties, private company (gov.uk/annual-accounts/penalties-for-late-filing, fetched 2026-08-15): up to 1 month £150; 1-3 months £375; 3-6 months £750; over 6 months £1,500; "the penalty is doubled if your accounts are late 2 years in a row". Note the Scanlans pack page's "£12 registration" is years stale; carry current figures as a freshness signal (writer re-verifies the fees page at write, fee levels moved recently).
