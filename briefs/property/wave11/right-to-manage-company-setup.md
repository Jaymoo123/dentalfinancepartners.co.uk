# Setting up an RTM company: formation, prescribed articles, and the obligations nobody warns you about

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 (c. 15) s.73 (RTM companies: private company limited by guarantee) and s.74 (RTM companies: membership and regulations). WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2002/15/contents on 2026-08-15.
- Supporting: RTM Companies (Model Articles) (England) Regulations 2009 (SI 2009/2767), prescribed model articles for RTM companies (limited by guarantee, no share capital; directors' general authority; membership rules). WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2009/2767/contents/made on 2026-08-15. Companies Act 2006 obligations (accounts, confirmation statement, directors' duties ss.171-177) cited generically; Landlord and Tenant Act 1987 s.42 (service charge trust money) and LTA 1985 ss.21-22 (service charge information and inspection) for the money-handling layer, Stage 2 verifies the s.42 citation before use.
- House position reference: §31.3a (RTM reforms IN FORCE Mar 2025). NEW LOCK NEEDED: shares proposed §31.C RTM lock with A11/A13 (flag F-76).

## Framing differentiator (anti-templating, anti-cannibalisation)
RTM TRIO LANE LOCK. A12 = THE COMPANY PAGE, the accountant's home turf and the page the conveyancer/managing-agent SERP cannot write properly. Scope: incorporating the company at Companies House (limited by guarantee, prescribed name conventions, the 2009 model articles apply automatically and override inconsistent bespoke articles); who must be allowed to join as members (qualifying tenants, and the landlord after acquisition, s.74); directors' appointment and their Companies Act 2006 duties in a building where the directors are also the customers; the ongoing compliance calendar (annual accounts, confirmation statement, registered office, PSC register); dormant-vs-active accounting treatment before and after the acquisition date; how service charge money must be handled once the company manages the block (trust money under LTA 1987 s.42 kept separate from company funds, and why RTM company accounts are usually dormant or near-dormant while service charge accounts run separately); corporation tax position (mutual trading and why a compliant RTM company typically has little or no CT liability, marked for Stage 2 verification against HMRC guidance); insurance and VAT edge cases.

This page does NOT cover: whether RTM is the right move or who qualifies (A11); serving notices and the statutory timeline (A13). One forward-link sentence to each. ## Key questions this page must answer
1. What kind of company is an RTM company required to be? (Private limited by guarantee, s.73.)
2. Do we write our own articles? (No; SI 2009/2767 model articles are prescribed and prevail.)
3. Who is entitled to membership, and does the freeholder really get to join? (s.74; yes, after acquisition.)
4. How do we incorporate it, step by step, and what does it cost at Companies House? (Figures Stage 2 verified.)
5. What duties do RTM company directors take on, and what is the realistic liability exposure?
6. What accounts and filings does an RTM company have to make every year, even if dormant?
7. How is service charge money handled: company funds or trust money? (LTA 1987 s.42 separation; LTA 1985 ss.21-22 information rights.)
8. Does an RTM company pay corporation tax? (Mutual trading position, Stage 2 verifies HMRC reference.)
9. Can the RTM company employ a managing agent, and who contracts with whom?
10. What happens if the company is struck off or fails to file, and does RTM survive?
