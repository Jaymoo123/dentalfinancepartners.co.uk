# What a lease extension solicitor does, step by step, and what the fees look like

## Statutory anchor
- Primary: Leasehold Reform, Housing and Urban Development Act 1993 (c. 28) s.42 (tenant's notice of claim), s.56 (obligation to grant new lease), s.60 (costs incurred in connection with new lease to be paid by tenant). WebFetch-verified at https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/28/contents on 2026-08-15 (s.60 heading confirmed: "Costs incurred in connection with new lease to be paid by tenant").
- Supporting: LRA 1967 s.14 (houses, obligation to grant extended lease); LFRA 2024 s.27 (2-year rule removal, IN FORCE 31 Jan 2025 via SI 2025/57) so a solicitor can now serve a s.42 notice for a buyer immediately on completion; LFRA 2024 costs-cap framework (s.60 recoverability changes NOT commenced for extension claims, Stage 2 verifies current s.60 position on write date).
- House position reference: §31.6 (process timeline 6-12 months, cost loading), §31.3a (in-force table), §31.10 (practical writing rule).

## Stage 2 extensions

### Verify-at-write items resolved (Stage 2, 2026-08-15)
- **GATING: post-LFRA s.60 recoverability position (resolved; note for conductor: patches the A10 row of the Stage 2b verify list and confirms §31.3a discipline).** Three findings, all legislation.gov.uk, 2026-08-15:
  1. LRHUDA 1993 s.60 live text (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1993/28/section/60, version dated 15 Aug 2026): still fully in force. Tenant pays the landlord's reasonable costs of "any investigation reasonably undertaken of the tenant's right to a new lease", "any valuation of the tenant's flat obtained for the purpose of fixing the premium", and "the grant of a new lease". Reasonableness test: costs are reasonable only if they "might reasonably be expected to have been incurred" had the landlord been paying them personally. Tribunal costs excluded: "A tenant shall not be liable under this section for any costs which a party to any proceedings under this Chapter before the appropriate tribunal incurs in connection with the proceedings."
  2. The replacement regime is enacted but NOT commenced: s.60 is "omitted by 2024 c. 22 s.39(8)" under "changes yet to be applied" (prospective annotation). LFRA 2024 s.38 (LRA 1967 costs) and s.39 (LRHUDA 1993 costs) appear in NO commencement SI (only SI 2024/1018, SI 2025/57, SI 2025/131 exist; SI search 2026-08-15).
  3. LFRA 2024 s.64 (restriction on recovery of non-litigation costs of enfranchisement, extension and right to manage) IS partially commenced, but SI 2025/131 reg 2 commences it only "insofar as it inserts section 20J of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 as regards the right to manage" (verbatim, https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/131/regulation/2/made). The extension limb of s.64 is NOT in force.
  WRITER RULE: state plainly that on a s.42 claim today you still pay the freeholder's reasonable non-litigation costs under s.60; the LFRA 2024 costs reforms for extension claims are enacted but not in force, no commencement date knowable. Never say "you no longer pay the freeholder's costs" (true only for RTM claims, different regime, A13's lane).
- **2-year rule (re-confirmed):** s.27 in force 31 Jan 2025 via SI 2025/57 (per lock §31.3a row 1); a solicitor can serve a s.42 notice for a buyer on completion day.
- **Fee benchmarks (SAM pack page, fetched 2026-08-15, returns 200, page updated 22 Apr 2025):** RICS valuation £600-£899; s.42 notice drafting/service £600-£1,200; own conveyancing £600-£1,200; freeholder's legal costs £600-£1,200; freeholder's valuation £600-£899; surveyor negotiation £150-£200/hour; stated total for legal work £3,600-£6,598. Cross-checked against HOA (A9 fetch): solicitor £800-£1,300, valuer £600-£900 per side. Use both as the verified benchmark ranges with source lines.
- **Tax treatment of fees:** both own fees and s.60 costs are capital (they attach to acquiring the extended lease); CGT base-cost principle per CG71141 (verified live 2026-08-15, "Short leases: disposal: allowable expenditure"; see A9 resolution).
