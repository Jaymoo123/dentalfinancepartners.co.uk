<p>For disposals from 6 April 2026, Business Asset Disposal Relief taxes qualifying gains on the sale of a care business at 18% rather than the standard higher rate of 24%. The relief is capped at £1 million of qualifying gains across your lifetime, so the most it can now save is £60,000. Most care sector exit plans were built against a different number: when the rate was 10% and the standard rate was already 24%, the same lifetime limit was worth £140,000.</p>

<h2>What is the rate, and which date fixes it?</h2>

<p>The rate is 18% for qualifying disposals from 6 April 2026. It was 14% for disposals between 6 April 2025 and 5 April 2026, and 10% for disposals on or before 5 April 2025, as <a href="https://www.gov.uk/business-asset-disposal-relief">HMRC's guidance on the relief</a> sets out. The rate is fixed by the date of disposal, which for a sale is normally the date the contract becomes unconditional rather than the completion date. On a deal that exchanges in one tax year and completes in the next, the exchange date is the one that matters, and a conditional contract does not create a disposal until the conditions are satisfied.</p>

<p>That distinction has already cost sellers money through two rate rises in two years. If you are negotiating now and the deal is expected to straddle a tax year end, the disposal date belongs in the heads of terms discussion rather than being left to whichever date the lawyers happen to land on.</p>

<h2>How much is the relief worth on a real care disposal?</h2>

<p>The saving is the gap between 24% and 18%, which is 6 percentage points, applied to gains within the £1 million lifetime limit set by section 169N of the <a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1992/12/section/169N">Taxation of Chargeable Gains Act 1992</a>. On a £400,000 gain the relief saves £24,000. On a £1 million gain it saves £60,000, and every pound of gain above the lifetime limit is taxed at the standard rates: 24% for a higher-rate taxpayer, or 18% to the extent the gain falls within an unused basic rate band. The annual exempt amount is £3,000 for 2026-27 and comes off the gain before the rate is applied.</p>

<p>The narrowing gap changes how much planning the relief justifies. When it was worth 14 percentage points, contorting a structure to preserve it was often worth the cost and disruption. At 6 points, the relief is one input among several, and a restructure that triggers stamp duty land tax on a property transfer can easily cost more than it protects. Compare any move against what the move itself costs.</p>

<h2>What breaks eligibility in a care business?</h2>

<p>The conditions have to hold for the two years ending with the disposal. For a sole trader or partner, that means owning the business throughout that period. For shares, it means holding at least 5% of ordinary share capital and voting rights, being entitled to at least 5% of distributable profits and assets on a winding up or of the proceeds on a sale of the whole company, and being an officer or employee of a trading company or the holding company of a trading group.</p>

<p>Three patterns remove the relief in this sector specifically. The first is the propco and opco split, where the property sits in one company and the regulated activity in another. If the property company's activity is letting to the operator, it may be an investment company, and its shares fall outside the relief even though the group as a whole plainly trades. The second is a share reorganisation or family dilution inside the two-year window, which resets the clock on the new holding. The third is bringing a manager or a family member in below 5%, or issuing a class of shares without full voting rights, which leaves that person holding something that cannot qualify at all.</p>

<p>Where the service has closed rather than sold, the assets must be disposed of within three years of cessation. Domiciliary agencies that hand contracts back and then sell a lease, vehicles or equipment months later are the common case, and the three-year point is absolute.</p>

<h2>Does an asset sale or a share sale change what you keep?</h2>

<p>It changes it more than the rate does, because the relief only reaches gains you make personally, so a share sale and an asset sale are not comparable on rate alone. In a share sale, you dispose of the shares, the relief applies to your gain if the conditions hold, and the buyer inherits the company with its history. In an asset sale, the company itself disposes, meets corporation tax on the result at <a href="/blog/business-structure-and-acquisition/care-structure-before-cqc">the rate for its profit level</a>, and you then face a second tax event extracting what is left, whether by liquidation distribution or by dividend at the rates that apply from 6 April 2026, running from 10.75% to 39.35%. Buyers frequently prefer assets and the seller's tax position usually runs the other way, which makes it a price negotiation rather than a technicality.</p>

<p>An asset sale also brings capital allowances into the calculation. Fit-out expenditure that was relieved on the way in can generate balancing charges on the way out, so <a href="/blog/business-structure-and-acquisition/capital-allowances-care-home-fit-out">the allowances history of the home</a> needs reconciling before the numbers go to a buyer rather than after. The <a href="/services/selling-a-care-home">sale-side work</a> and the <a href="/services/buying-a-care-home">diligence a buyer runs</a> look at the same file from opposite ends.</p>

<h2>What should you do with two years or more to run?</h2>

<p>Settle the entity and shareholdings now, because every condition is measured backwards from a date you do not yet know. Check each shareholder individually against the 5% tests, including anyone holding a separate share class. Document the trading status of any property-holding entity, with evidence rather than assertion. Reconcile the capital allowances pools and the <a href="/blog/fees-fnc-and-local-authority-rates/fnc-chc-la-fee-mix-accounting">fee income split between local authority, NHS and private payers</a>, since a buyer will price the mix and an unclear one invites a discount larger than the relief. Then keep the disposal date under your control when the deal comes.</p>
