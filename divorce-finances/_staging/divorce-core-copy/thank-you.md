## What happens next

1. **We read your message.** A person, not an auto-responder, and we reply within two working days.
2. **If you asked for an introduction,** we match you with a vetted family law firm or accredited mediator suited to your situation and pass your details across, exactly as you consented on the form.
3. **They contact you directly.** You are under no obligation to use them, and saying no thanks costs nothing and needs no explanation.

Reminder line: `As we set out on the contact form, we may receive a fee from the firm we introduce you to if you use their services. It does not change what you pay.`
