## Section: What you can work out here

- Eyebrow: `Free calculators`
- H2: `Put numbers on it before you commit to anything.`
- Lead: `Every tool shows its working and states its assumptions. Court fees are taken from the published HMCTS schedule and dated, so you know the figures are current.`

Cards:

1. **Divorce cost calculator.** What your divorce is likely to cost end to end, by route: doing it yourself online, a fixed-fee service, or a solicitor-led case. Includes the July 2026 court fee increases.
2. **Help with Fees checker.** Court fees can be reduced or waived if your income and savings are below set thresholds. Check in two minutes whether you are likely to qualify before you pay anything.
3. **Consent order and clean break cost calculator.** A financial order is the only thing that makes your settlement binding. See what one costs across the DIY, fixed-fee and solicitor routes.
4. **Financial settlement range estimator.** There is no formula for a divorce settlement, and anyone who gives you a single number is guessing. Our estimator gives you a realistic range based on the factors courts actually weigh, with every assumption stated.
5. **Mediation vs solicitor cost comparison.** Most people do not need a courtroom. Compare what mediation, solicitor negotiation and court proceedings typically cost, including the Family Mediation Voucher.

## Section: Who we help

- Eyebrow: `Made for your situation`
- H2: `Divorce finances look different depending on what you own and who depends on you.`

Cards (link to /for pages):

1. **Divorcing homeowners.** The house is usually the biggest asset and the hardest question. Buyouts, transfers of equity, Mesher orders and what happens to the joint mortgage.
2. **Business owners divorcing.** Company shares, director loans and how a business is valued and treated in a settlement, without putting the business itself at risk.
3. **Separated parents.** The money side only: child maintenance, the family home while children are at home, and keeping two households afloat. Handled carefully, because this is about more than money.
4. **Divorcing over 50.** Later-life divorce is the fastest-growing kind. Pensions often outweigh the house, retirement plans change shape, and the decisions are harder to undo.
